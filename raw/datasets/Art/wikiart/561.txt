Camilla Løw (1976-) is a contemporary Norwegian an artist currently living and working in Oslo. She graduated from Asker Kunstskole in Norway in 1998. After this, she left for Glasgow, where she graduated from the Glasgow School of Art in 2001 with a BA in Fine Art and lived for 8 years.

# q13: How many teams founded before 1970 are based in each city?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** How many teams founded before 1970 are based in each city?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT location, COUNT(*) AS team_count FROM team WHERE founded_year < 1970 AND location != '' GROUP BY location
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT location, COUNT(*) AS team_count FROM team WHERE founded_year < 1970 AND location != '' GROUP BY location
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Team rows, cities, and years
- **Root cause:** QuWARTS kept old and repeated team rows, different city names, and wrong founding years.
- **Why the system made this choice:** It did not reduce several possible team rows to one current team row.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** An old city or year can appear in the source and look correct on its own.
- **Why this query failed:** Different city names split the groups. Wrong founding years also removed Houston and Philadelphia.

**What I saw in the run**
- The same 42-row team table includes historical subentities and location variants such as Auburn Hills, Manhattan, Phoenix, Arizona, and Washington, D.C.
- Nine gold cities are missing and four unmatched city labels are added.
- Houston and Philadelphia are both given founding year 1976 instead of their ground-truth years before 1970. The date rule therefore removes them.
- Aligned groups all have the correct count, showing that the dominant q13 failure is record/key selection rather than COUNT arithmetic.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Missing years and old locations
- **Root cause:** DocETL used -1 for unknown founding years and sometimes chose old locations.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The instruction allowed -1 for missing numbers and did not clearly require one current team record.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** -1 is a number and is less than 1970. Old locations are also normal text.
- **Why this query failed:** Teams with unknown years were wrongly included. Other current teams appeared under old city names or wrong years.

**What I saw in the run**
- Charlotte, Miami, and New Orleans have founded_year=-1 and therefore become false-positive pre-1970 teams.
- Detroit is represented as Fort Wayne; Brooklyn as New Jersey; Phoenix and Washington carry state/district suffixes.
- San Antonio is assigned 1973 rather than 1967 and fails the filter; Sacramento has an empty location and the Knicks document is absent.
- Seven gold cities are missing and seven unmatched cities are added despite the row count coincidentally equaling gold.

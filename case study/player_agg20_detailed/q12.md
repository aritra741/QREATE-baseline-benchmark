# q12: For each city that hosts an NBA team, what is the most championships won by any team there?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** For each city that hosts an NBA team, what is the most championships won by any team there?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT location, MAX(championship) AS max_championships FROM team WHERE location != '' GROUP BY location
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT location, MAX(championship) AS max_championships FROM team WHERE location != '' GROUP BY location
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Team records and current facts
- **Root cause:** The QuWARTS team table contains historical team names, person names, and a year as separate team rows.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The selected team table has 42 rows, while the benchmark has 30 teams. It has no final rule that limits the result to one current row for each benchmark team.
- **Why validation allowed it:** Person names and years still pass the table's text and number type checks even when the row identity is not a current team.
- **How it affected the result:** Old and false rows added locations. Some years were also used as title counts, which changed the highest values.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The team table contains spurious identities such as Rod Derline, 1967, Josh Harris (businessman), and historical Fort Wayne Zollner Pistons.
- Person-named team rows include James L. Dolan→New York metropolitan area and Josh Harris (businessman)→Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, U.S.
- Location variants explain paired misses/extras: Detroit→Auburn Hills, New York City→Manhattan, Portland→Portland, Oregon, and Washington→Washington, D.C.
- Wrong extracted totals include Portland=1977 (a championship year used as a count), Cleveland=6 (gold 1), San Antonio=7 (gold 5), and Golden State=6 (gold 7); ten gold locations are missing and twelve unmatched locations are added.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Choosing current team facts
- **Root cause:** DocETL sometimes chose old team facts, and some documents produced no row.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** Each team document was handled once. There was no second check to choose current facts over old facts.
- **Why validation allowed it:** An old location or title total has the expected format, so it passed.
- **How it affected the result:** Old values replaced current ones, missing titles became -1, and missing rows removed three teams.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The team output has 27/30 rows; Memphis, New Orleans, and Sacramento use championship=-1.
- Brooklyn is extracted as New Jersey and Washington as Washington, D.C.; Milwaukee, New York City, and Salt Lake City are absent.
- Historical/incorrect totals inflate Charlotte 0→4, Orlando 0→2, Toronto 1→5, and several other aligned groups.

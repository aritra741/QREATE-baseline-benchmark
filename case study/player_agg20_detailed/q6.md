# q6: How many players were drafted in each year after 2010?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** How many players were drafted in each year after 2010?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT draft_year, COUNT(*) AS player_count FROM player WHERE draft_year > 2010 GROUP BY draft_year
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT draft_year, COUNT(*) AS player_count FROM player WHERE draft_year > 2010 GROUP BY draft_year
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Meaning of years
- **Root cause:** QuWARTS used a year from a draft discussion as the player's draft year, even when the player was not drafted.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The selected working table checks the value type, but it does not require evidence that the year is the player's own draft year.
- **Why validation allowed it:** A saved validation record says Max Fiedler's 2024 value was not supported as his draft year, but the working table still retained it.
- **How it affected the result:** Players moved into the wrong year groups and a false 2024 group appeared. Other players had no draft year.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The output has every numeric gold year but adds 2024; counts differ for 2011 (3→4), 2017 (5→4), and 2021 (3→2).
- Draft year is missing from 67 of 160 rows. The result covers many year groups even though many player values are missing.
- The extra 2024 row is undrafted Max Fiedler: QuWARTS extracts the year of the 2024 draft discussion as his draft year.
- The extra empty group in the ground truth comes from a scoring issue. An empty draft year passes a number comparison.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Wrong and missing draft years
- **Root cause:** DocETL assigned unrelated years to some players and left many other draft years unknown.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The instruction asked for a draft year but did not require proof that the year described the player's own draft.
- **Why validation allowed it:** The wrong years looked like normal years. Players with an unknown year simply disappeared from the displayed result.
- **How it affected the result:** The final table has no -1 row. The after-2010 rule removed those missing-value rows. Wrong years still moved players into the wrong groups, and several real year groups disappeared.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The intermediate player table has 136 rows, and 100 draft years are marked as missing. The after-2010 rule removes those rows, so no -1 appears in the final table.
- 2012, 2019, 2021, and 2022 are missing, in addition to the evaluator's empty-string group.
- Unrelated years are also assigned as draft years: Tim Duncan, Kevin Durant, Kevin Love, and Giannis Antetokounmpo are all incorrectly given 2011, explaining most of that group's 3→8 overcount.
- For example, 2011 is overcounted 3→8 while 2016 is undercounted 3→1.

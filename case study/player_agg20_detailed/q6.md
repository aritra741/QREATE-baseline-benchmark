# q6: How many players were drafted in each year after 2010?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** How many players were drafted in each year after 2010?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT draft_year, COUNT(*) AS player_count FROM player WHERE draft_year > 2010 GROUP BY draft_year
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT draft_year, COUNT(*) AS player_count FROM player WHERE draft_year > 2010 GROUP BY draft_year
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Semantic role of years
- **Root cause:** The extractor treated a year mentioned near a draft discussion as the player's draft year, even for an undrafted player.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The raw view uses an integer type for draft year but has no rule that the year must describe the player's actual selection event.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** 2024 is a valid integer and appears in the document, so type and source-span checks pass it.
- **Why this query failed:** The wrong year moves players between groups and creates a new 2024 group. Sparse extraction removes other counts. The gold empty group is also an evaluator typing edge case.

**What we saw in the run**
- The output has every numeric gold year but adds 2024; counts differ for 2011 (3→4), 2017 (5→4), and 2021 (3→2).
- The shared table has 67/160 NULL draft years, so the close group coverage comes from a partial extraction.
- The extra 2024 row is undrafted Max Fiedler: QuWARTS extracts the year of the 2024 draft discussion as his draft year.
- Gold's additional empty-string group is a mixed-type SQLite edge case: an empty draft year survives the numeric comparison in the evaluator.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Year extraction and missing-value handling
- **Root cause:** The Map step had only a number type for draft year, so it chose unrelated years and used -1 when uncertain.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The prompt asks for the field name but does not require evidence that the year is the player's draft event.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** Unrelated years and -1 are both valid numbers. There is no event-role check after extraction.
- **Why this query failed:** Players move to wrong year groups, many real years disappear, and the -1 rows are removed by the numeric predicate.

**What we saw in the run**
- The q6 extraction has 136 rows but 100 draft_year=-1.
- 2012, 2019, 2021, and 2022 are missing, in addition to the evaluator's empty-string group.
- Unrelated years are also assigned as draft years: Tim Duncan, Kevin Durant, Kevin Love, and Giannis Antetokounmpo are all incorrectly mapped to 2011, explaining most of that group's 3→8 overcount.
- For example, 2011 is overcounted 3→8 while 2016 is undercounted 3→1.

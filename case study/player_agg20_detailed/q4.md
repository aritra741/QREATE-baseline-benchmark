**Natural language:** Among players with a known position, what is the total number of MVP awards won at each position?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT position, SUM(mvp_awards) AS total_mvp_awards FROM player WHERE position IN ('Frontcourt', 'Backcourt') GROUP BY position
```

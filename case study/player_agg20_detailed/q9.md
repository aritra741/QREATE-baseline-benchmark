# q9: For each college, how many of its players have won at least one NBA championship?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** For each college, how many of its players have won at least one NBA championship?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT college, COUNT(*) AS champion_count FROM player WHERE nba_championships >= 1 AND college != '' GROUP BY college
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT college, COUNT(*) AS champion_count FROM player WHERE nba_championships >= 1 AND college != '' GROUP BY college
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Entity and attribute resolution
- **Root cause:** College aliases were not merged, and nearby championship claims were accepted without enough NBA-specific checking.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The raw view favors recall and keeps each supported college surface and numeric championship mention.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** Name variants are valid text and championship values are valid numbers. The checks do not test shared college identity or the exact meaning of each title.
- **Why this query failed:** Aliases split counts across groups, while false title values admit players who should fail the filter.

**What we saw in the run**
- Fifteen gold college labels are missing and fifteen unmatched aliases are added, including Davidson→Davidson Wildcats, LSU→LSU Tigers, and UCLA→UCLA Bruins.
- The player table has 114/160 NULL nba_championships values and 38 NULL colleges.
- Alias splits directly explain wrong counts (Florida Gators plus University of Florida) while Kobe Bryant is incorrectly assigned Lower Merion High School as a college and admitted as a champion.
- Three aligned college counts are also wrong, showing that key normalization alone does not explain all value error.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Prompt-only normalization and filter extraction
- **Root cause:** The Map step produced several forms of each college name and false NBA-title values.
- **Why the system made this choice:** There is no college Resolve step or evidence check for championship meaning. Unknown values also use -1.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** Every alias is valid text and every false title is a valid number, so output typing cannot reject them.
- **Why this query failed:** The filter admits false champions, then GROUP BY separates short, mascot, and full college names.

**What we saw in the run**
- The q9 extraction has 20 empty colleges and 91/135 nba_championships=-1.
- Seventeen gold labels are missing and nineteen unmatched variants are added, including Florida, Indiana, Kansas, North Carolina, and multiple Villanova forms.
- False champion extractions create extra groups: David Lattin at Texas Western, Toby Kimball at Connecticut, Rik Smits at Marist, and Jalen Brunson at Villanova University are assigned NBA titles they do not have in gold.
- Five aligned group counts are wrong; Kentucky is overcounted 1→3 and Michigan State 2→3.

# q9: For each college, how many of its players have won at least one NBA championship?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** For each college, how many of its players have won at least one NBA championship?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT college, COUNT(*) AS champion_count FROM player WHERE nba_championships >= 1 AND college != '' GROUP BY college
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT college, COUNT(*) AS champion_count FROM player WHERE nba_championships >= 1 AND college != '' GROUP BY college
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** College names and title counts
- **Root cause:** QuWARTS used several names for the same college and accepted some wrong NBA title counts.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The selected working table does not clean college names and only checks that the title field is a number.
- **Why validation allowed it:** Two college labels and an incorrect title count can all pass those text and number checks.
- **How it affected the result:** College groups were split, and some players wrongly passed the NBA title rule.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- Fifteen ground-truth college names are missing and fifteen different names are added, including Davidson→Davidson Wildcats, LSU→LSU Tigers, and UCLA→UCLA Bruins.
- NBA title count is missing from 114 of 160 rows. College is missing from 38 rows.
- The working output separates labels such as Florida Gators and University of Florida, so SQL counts them as different colleges. Lower Merion High School also appears as an extra college group with a champion count of 1, although it is not a gold college group.
- Three aligned college counts are also wrong, showing that college-name cleaning alone does not explain all value error.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** College names and title counts
- **Root cause:** DocETL produced several forms of each college name and some wrong NBA title counts.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** There was no shared college-name list and no check of what each title number meant.
- **Why validation allowed it:** Every college form was normal text, and every false title was still a number.
- **How it affected the result:** Wrong title values let some players pass. Short, mascot, and full college names then became separate groups.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The intermediate player table has 20 empty colleges and 91 of 135 title counts marked as missing. The title rule removes those missing counts from the final result.
- Seventeen gold labels are missing and nineteen unmatched variants are added, including Florida, Indiana, Kansas, North Carolina, and multiple Villanova forms.
- False champion extractions create extra groups: Dave Lattin at Texas Western, Toby Kimball at Connecticut, Rik Smits at Marist, and Jalen Brunson at Villanova University are assigned NBA titles they do not have in the Player CSV.
- Five aligned group counts are wrong; Kentucky is overcounted 1→3 and Michigan State 2→3.

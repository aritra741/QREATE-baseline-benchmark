# q8: Among players with a known position and at least one Olympic gold medal, what is the average number of Olympic golds at each position?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** Among players with a known position and at least one Olympic gold medal, what is the average number of Olympic golds at each position?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT position, AVG(olympic_gold_medals) AS avg_olympic_golds FROM player WHERE olympic_gold_medals >= 1 AND position IN ('Frontcourt', 'Backcourt') GROUP BY position
```

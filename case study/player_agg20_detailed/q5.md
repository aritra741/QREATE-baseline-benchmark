# q5: Among players with at least one NBA championship, what is the most championships won by any player of each nationality?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** Among players with at least one NBA championship, what is the most championships won by any player of each nationality?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT nationality, MAX(nba_championships) AS max_championships FROM player WHERE nba_championships >= 1 AND nationality != '' GROUP BY nationality
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT nationality, MAX(nba_championships) AS max_championships FROM player WHERE nba_championships >= 1 AND nationality != '' GROUP BY nationality
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Meaning of title counts
- **Root cause:** QuWARTS treated numbers from other sports and nearby years as NBA title counts.
- **Why the system made this choice:** It checked that the value was a number, but not what the number described.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** Six Stanley Cups and several years appear in the documents, so the checks found support for those numbers.
- **Why this query failed:** Wrong title counts became the highest value for some nationalities. Different nationality names created more group errors. One error also comes from an unusual empty value in the scoring data.

**What I saw in the run**
- Dominican and Nigerian-American are replaced by Dominican Republic and Nigerian; Yugoslav is an additional unmatched group.
- Canadian is reported as 6 rather than 1, indicating a wrong championship count on at least one retained record.
- The Canadian=6 row is Larry Robinson, a hockey player whose six Stanley Cups were extracted as NBA championships; Dino Rađa becomes Yugoslav=2 after two cited years are interpreted as two NBA titles.
- The ground truth reports an empty American value because the scorer compares empty text with numbers in an unusual way. QuWARTS reports the reasonable number 6 but is still marked wrong.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Missing rows and wrong title counts
- **Root cause:** DocETL skipped some rows and accepted a wrong NBA title count.
- **Why the system made this choice:** A failed document can be skipped without stopping the full question. The number check does not test what the number means.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** A value such as 9 looks like a valid number even when it describes the wrong fact.
- **Why this query failed:** Skipped rows removed nationality groups, while the wrong value made the American maximum too high.

**What I saw in the run**
- The q5 extraction contains only 116 rows; 65 nationalities are empty and 81 championship values are -1.
- The American maximum is 9, although gold's evaluator result is the empty-string edge case; Nigerian-American is 2 instead of 1.
- All player documents were sent to DocETL, but OG Anunoby and Giannis Antetokounmpo are missing from its 116-row output. DocETL can skip a document when extraction fails. Steve Kerr supplies the wrong American maximum of 9.
- British and Greek and Nigerian are missing, while Croatian, Dutch, Greek, and Slovenian are extra groups.

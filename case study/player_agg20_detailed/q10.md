# q10: Among players with at least one FIBA World Cup title, what is the total number of FIBA titles for each current NBA team?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** Among players with at least one FIBA World Cup title, what is the total number of FIBA titles for each current NBA team?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT p.team, SUM(p.fiba_world_cup) AS total_fiba_titles FROM player p JOIN team t ON p.team = t.team_name WHERE p.fiba_world_cup >= 1 GROUP BY p.team
```

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Meaning of title counts
- **Root cause:** QuWARTS accepted years as FIBA title counts and often missed current team links.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The selected working table checked that the title field held a number, but it did not apply a sensible count range.
- **Why validation allowed it:** Saved validation records mark Rik Smits's 1986 value as contradicted and Bam Adebayo's 2024 value as unsupported. The working table still retained both numbers.
- **How it affected the result:** These years passed the at-least-one rule and made the totals extremely large. Missing or wrong teams also changed the groups.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- FIBA title count is missing from 129 of 160 rows. Other rows contain impossible counts such as 1986, 1994, 2008, 2014, 2017, and 2024.
- Those errors appear directly in team totals: Indiana=1986, Los Angeles Lakers=2010, and Miami=2025.
- Specific contributors are Rik Smits=1986, Kobe Bryant=2008, and Bam Adebayo=2024; years were extracted as title counts and then correctly summed.
- Four ground-truth teams are missing and four extra teams appear because many player team values are missing or wrong.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Title counts and team names
- **Root cause:** DocETL accepted any number as a title count and built team names separately.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** It had no sensible range for title counts and no shared cleanup for team names.
- **Why validation allowed it:** A title value of 30 and two different forms of a team name both passed the basic checks.
- **How it affected the result:** Wrong counts made totals too high, while different team names removed valid players.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The intermediate player table has 86 of 114 FIBA title counts marked as missing and 69 empty team values. The title rule removes the missing counts from the final result. One remaining title count is 30.
- Names such as Boston Celtics and Chicago Bulls do not match the shorter team names in the other table.
- Kevin Durant is extracted as Brooklyn/4 and Dennis Schröder adds 1, while three Philadelphia players are each assigned one title; these rows directly produce Brooklyn=5 and Philadelphia=3.
- Seven gold groups are missing, five are extra, and Brooklyn and Philadelphia are inflated to 5 and 3.

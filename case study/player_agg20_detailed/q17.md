# q17: Among owners who acquired their team in 2000 or later, what is the earliest acquisition year for each nationality?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** Among owners who acquired their team in 2000 or later, what is the earliest acquisition year for each nationality?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT nationality, MIN(own_year) AS earliest_own_year FROM owner WHERE own_year >= 2000 GROUP BY nationality
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT nationality, MIN(own_year) AS earliest_own_year FROM owner WHERE own_year >= 2000 GROUP BY nationality
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Owner coverage and purchase years
- **Root cause:** The QuWARTS owner table lacks the American purchase year 2000 and contains an unsupported 1996 year for the Taiwanese-Canadian row.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The owner table stores one year but no event text that proves the year is a team purchase. The working table can keep a numeric year without that proof.
- **Why validation allowed it:** A wrong year can still have the correct number format. A saved validation record says Joseph Tsai's 1996 value was not supported as an ownership year.
- **How it affected the result:** The missing 2000 purchase changed the American minimum. A wrong 1996 year removed the Taiwanese-Canadian row.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The owner table has only 11 rows and contains no American own_year=2000; Mark Cuban's gold 2000 acquisition is absent.
- Joseph Tsai retains Taiwanese-Canadian but is assigned own_year=1996 rather than 2019, so the correct >=2000 filter removes him.
- Its post-2000 rows include U.S.=2005 and Sicilian descent=2017, producing two extra groups.
- The resulting output lacks Taiwanese-Canadian and reports American=2008 instead of 2000.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Purchase years and nationality
- **Root cause:** DocETL left most nationality values empty and used a company job year as a team purchase year.
- **Pipeline behavior behind it:** The extraction output stores only the year value. It does not store a quote or event label that proves the year describes buying the team.
- **Why validation allowed it:** The job year is still a number, and an empty nationality was allowed.
- **How it affected the result:** Empty nationalities joined unrelated owners into one group. The wrong year could also pass the date rule.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The extraction contains 11 rows, with nationality empty on nine.
- The empty group has eligible years 2000 and 2006; the 2000 value is taken from Steve Ballmer's Microsoft CEO tenure rather than a team-acquisition year.

# q17: Among owners who acquired their team in 2000 or later, what is the earliest acquisition year for each nationality?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** Among owners who acquired their team in 2000 or later, what is the earliest acquisition year for each nationality?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT nationality, MIN(own_year) AS earliest_own_year FROM owner WHERE own_year >= 2000 GROUP BY nationality
```

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Owner coverage and acquisition-event extraction
- **Root cause:** The owner table missed a key owner and confused other years with the year a team was acquired.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The raw extractor fills the requested year from source text but has no event model that links the year to the ownership acquisition.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** A wrong year is still an integer with source support. The checks do not verify the event relation.
- **Why this query failed:** The missing 2000 acquisition shifts the American minimum, a wrong 1996 year removes the Taiwanese-Canadian row, and descriptive nationalities add groups.

**What we saw in the run**
- The owner table has only 11 rows and contains no American own_year=2000; Mark Cuban's gold 2000 acquisition is absent.
- Joseph Tsai retains Taiwanese-Canadian but is assigned own_year=1996 rather than 2019, so the correct >=2000 filter removes him.
- Its post-2000 rows include U.S.=2005 and Sicilian descent=2017, producing two extra groups.
- The resulting output lacks Taiwanese-Canadian and reports American=2008 instead of 2000.

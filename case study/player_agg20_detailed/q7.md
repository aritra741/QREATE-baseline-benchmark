# q7: For each current NBA team, what is the earliest (lowest) draft pick among its drafted players?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** For each current NBA team, what is the earliest (lowest) draft pick among its drafted players?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT p.team, MIN(p.draft_pick) AS earliest_draft_pick FROM player p JOIN team t ON p.team = t.team_name WHERE p.draft_pick > 0 GROUP BY p.team
```

**QuWARTS compiled SQL**
```sql
SELECT p.team, MIN(p.draft_pick) AS earliest_draft_pick FROM player p JOIN team t ON p.team = t.team_name WHERE p.draft_pick > 0 GROUP BY p.team
```

The SQL contracts are equivalent unless the two SQL blocks visibly differ. Therefore, documented failures below are downstream of intent/compilation.

### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Current teams
- **Root cause:** The QuWARTS working table missed many team and draft-pick values, and some retained team groups did not match the benchmark.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The player row stores a team name but no date or status. The selected raw table has no rule that can choose a current team over another team mention.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** A team name can be valid text and still be the wrong team for the benchmark row.
- **Why this query failed:** Players were placed under missing or wrong teams. The lowest draft pick was then calculated from the wrong players.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- Team is missing from 62 of 160 rows. Draft pick is missing from 82 rows.
- Brooklyn, Cleveland, Milwaukee, and Orlando are missing, while Charlotte, Memphis, and Utah are extra.
- The working player table stores only a team name and draft pick. It has no date or status field that could prove the team is the current benchmark team.
- Seven aligned teams have wrong minima, including Boston 1→3, Golden State 1→6, and Philadelphia 3→9.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Team name matching
- **Root cause:** DocETL built player team names and official team names separately. It also kept some old team links.
- **Why the system made this choice:** The two sets of names were not cleaned together, and there was no clear rule to choose only the current team.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** Both sides contained normal text, even when the names differed or the team link was old.
- **Why this query failed:** Valid players failed to match their teams, while other players were sent to the wrong team. This changed the lowest draft pick.

**What I found in the final and working tables**
- The intermediate player table has 30 empty teams and 53 missing draft picks. None of the displayed team minimums is -1.
- Player team names such as Boston Celtics, Denver Nuggets, Milwaukee Bucks, and New York Knicks do not match the shorter team names in the other table.
- Current-team errors include Jay Vincent Lakers→Dallas, Kyrie Irving Dallas→Cleveland, and Al Horford Boston→Atlanta. Their draft picks therefore lower the value for the wrong teams.
- Four gold teams are missing, five groups are extra, and five aligned minima are wrong, including Dallas 1→24.

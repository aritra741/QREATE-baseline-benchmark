# q7: For each current NBA team, what is the earliest (lowest) draft pick among its drafted players?
## Query contract
**Natural language:** For each current NBA team, what is the earliest (lowest) draft pick among its drafted players?

**Reference SQL**
```sql
SELECT p.team, MIN(p.draft_pick) AS earliest_draft_pick FROM player p JOIN team t ON p.team = t.team_name WHERE p.draft_pick > 0 GROUP BY p.team
```


### Why QuWARTS differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Current relationship resolution
- **Root cause:** The player table did not choose one current NBA team and did not recover draft picks for enough players.
- **Why the system made this choice:** QuWARTS keeps high-recall team mentions instead of resolving time and relationship type to one current team.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** A historical team or related organization can have source support. The join signal tests overlap, not whether the relationship is current.
- **Why this query failed:** Players join to missing or wrong teams, then MIN uses the wrong set of draft picks for each team.

**What we saw in the run**
- The player table has 62/160 NULL team values and 82/160 NULL draft picks.
- Brooklyn, Cleveland, Milwaukee, and Orlando are missing, while Charlotte, Memphis, and Utah are extra.
- Shaquille O'Neal is assigned to NRG Esports instead of Boston; Portland's gold pick-2 player has a NULL team, while an unrelated pick-8 player is admitted as Portland.
- Seven aligned teams have wrong minima, including Boston 1→3, Golden State 1→6, and Philadelphia 3→9.

### Why DocETL differs
- **Pipeline stage:** Independent key extraction
- **Root cause:** Player teams and team names were built in separate Map steps, with no Resolve step and no current-time rule.
- **Why the system made this choice:** Each table is normalized by prompt wording only. Historical team mentions are allowed to become the player key.
- **Why the checks did not catch it:** Both sides contain valid strings, so schema checks pass even when aliases differ or the player relationship is old.
- **Why this query failed:** Exact joins lose valid players, and historical assignments send draft picks to the wrong team before MIN runs.

**What we saw in the run**
- The q7 player extraction has 30 empty teams and 53 draft_pick=-1 values.
- Player keys such as Boston Celtics, Denver Nuggets, Milwaukee Bucks, New York Knicks, and historical franchise names do not match the independently extracted team_name vocabulary.
- Concrete current-team errors include Jay Vincent Lakers→Dallas, Kyrie Irving Dallas→Cleveland, and Al Horford Boston→Atlanta; the correct MIN operator then propagates their draft picks into the wrong groups.
- Four gold teams are missing, five groups are extra, and five aligned minima are wrong, including Dallas 1→24.

It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,773, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 70 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar. Rollins College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 1. Its tuition and fees are $37,640. Located lakeside in Winter Park, Florida, Rollins provides small-town community and proximity to neighboring Orlando, a premier international destination.

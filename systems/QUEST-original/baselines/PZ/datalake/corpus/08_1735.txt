 (6) The ACCC was concerned that the representations made by Mr Roberson and referred to in paragraphs 3 and 4 above were likely to mislead consumers as to the availability, terms and conditions of employment opportunities he claimed to exist, particularly employment opportunities at the Moorland Orchard.

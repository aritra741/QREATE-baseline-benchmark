Daniel Edward Anderson (born January 1, 1951) is an American retired professional basketball player in the National Basketball Association (NBA).
For the American Basketball Association player, see Dan Anderson (basketball, born 1943).
Anderson was selected with the 2nd pick of the sixth round of the 1974 NBA draft by the Portland Trail Blazers.

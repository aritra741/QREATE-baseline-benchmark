candidate sentence: Kostas Papanikolaou
Konstantinos "Kostas" Papanikolaou (Greek: Κωνσταντίνος "Κώστας" Παπανικολάου; born 31 July 1990) nicknamed "Air Pap" is a Greek professional basketball player and the team captain for Olympiacos of the Greek Basket League (GBL) and the EuroLeague.
A back-to-back EuroLeague champion with Olympiacos in 2012 and 2013, Papanikolaou was the EuroLeague Finals Top Scorer in 2012 and won the EuroLeague Rising Star award in 2013.
Papanikolaou is also the first player in EuroLeague history to record 2.000 points, 1.000 rebounds, 400 assists, 300 three-pointers made, 200 steals and 100 blocks.
Standing at 2.04 m (6'8 1⁄2"), he plays at the small forward position.
Since very early in his career, he has been a regular member of the senior Hellenic national team.
He is widely considered among the best defensive players in European basketball.
He has reached another two finals with the Reds in 2017 and 2023.
He is widely considered among the best defensive players in European basketball.

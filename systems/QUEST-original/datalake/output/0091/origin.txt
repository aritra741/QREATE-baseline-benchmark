The Basics About Franklin College

Franklin College is a private institution that was founded in 1834. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,153, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 74 acres. It utilizes a 4-1-4-based academic calendar.  Franklin College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (Midwest), 10. Its tuition and fees are $24,655.

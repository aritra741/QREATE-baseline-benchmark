The Basics About Averett University

Averett University is a private institution that was founded in 1859. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 787, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 19 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Averett University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (South), 35. Its tuition and fees are $22,956.

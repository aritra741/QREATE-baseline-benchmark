Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University The Basics About Embry Riddle Embry Riddle is a private institution that was founded in 1926. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 4,430, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 185 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Embry Riddle's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 10. Its tuition and fees are $29,248.

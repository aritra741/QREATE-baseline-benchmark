Albany State University Albany State University The Basics About Albany State University Albany State University is a public institution that was founded in 1903. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,753, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 232 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Albany State University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 86.

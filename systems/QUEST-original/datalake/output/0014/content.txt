Alvernia University The Basics About Alvernia University Alvernia University is a private institution that was founded in 1958. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,076, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 123 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Alvernia University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 121. Its tuition and fees are $25,480.

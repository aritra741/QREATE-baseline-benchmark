The Basics About Baker University

Baker University is a private institution that was founded in 1858. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 992, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 10 acres. It utilizes a 4-1-4-based academic calendar.  Baker University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 144. Its tuition and fees are $22,280.

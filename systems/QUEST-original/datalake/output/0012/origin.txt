The Basics About Allegheny College

Allegheny College is a private institution that was founded in 1815. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,132, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 565 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Allegheny College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 93. Its tuition and fees are $34,810.

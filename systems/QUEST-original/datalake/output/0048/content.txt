Bates College The Basics About Bates College Bates College is a private institution that was founded in 1855. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,738, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 109 acres. It utilizes a other-based academic calendar.  Bates College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 21. Its comprehensive fee is $53,300.

Francis Marion University Francis Marion University The Basics About Francis Marion Francis Marion is a public institution that was founded in 1970. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,629, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 400 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Francis Marion's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 69. Its in-state tuition and fees are $8,480; out-of-state tuition and fees are $16,625.

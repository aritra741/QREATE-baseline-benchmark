Florida Institute of Technology Florida Institute of Technology The Basics About Florida Tech Florida Tech is a private institution that was founded in 1958. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 5,022, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 130 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Florida Tech's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Universities, 159. Its tuition and fees are $32,294.

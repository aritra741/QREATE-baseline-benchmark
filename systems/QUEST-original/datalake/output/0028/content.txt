Ashland University Ashland University The Basics About Ashland University Ashland University is a private institution that was founded in 1878. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,693, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 150 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Ashland University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 58. Its tuition and fees are $27,404.

The Basics About Adelphi University

Adelphi University is a private institution that was founded in 1896. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 4,954, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 75 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Adelphi University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Universities, 159. Its tuition and fees are $27,400.

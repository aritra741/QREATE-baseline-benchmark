The Basics About Elizabethtown College

Elizabethtown College is a private institution that was founded in 1899. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,322, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 200 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Elizabethtown College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (North), 5. Its tuition and fees are $33,250.

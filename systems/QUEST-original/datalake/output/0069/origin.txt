The Basics About Eureka College

Eureka College is a private institution that was founded in 1855. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 766, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 112 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Eureka College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (Midwest), 29. Its tuition and fees are $18,045.

Faulkner University The Basics About Faulkner University Faulkner University is a private institution that was founded in 1942. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,762, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 84 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Faulkner University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (South), 64. Its tuition and fees are $15,010.

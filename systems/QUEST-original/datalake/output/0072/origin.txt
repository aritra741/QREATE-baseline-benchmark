The Basics About Fairleigh Dickinson

Fairleigh Dickinson is a private institution that was founded in 1942. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 8,524, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 266 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Fairleigh Dickinson's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 57. Its tuition and fees are $28,976.

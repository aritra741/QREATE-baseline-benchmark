Agnes Scott College Agnes Scott College The Basics About Agnes Scott College Agnes Scott College is a private institution that was founded in 1889. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 842, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 100 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Agnes Scott College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 67. Its tuition and fees are $31,283.

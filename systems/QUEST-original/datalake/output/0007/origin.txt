The Basics About Albright College

Albright College is a private institution that was founded in 1856. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,282, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 118 acres. It utilizes a 4-1-4-based academic calendar.  Albright College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 166. Its tuition and fees are $32,740.

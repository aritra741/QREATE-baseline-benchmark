Framingham State College The Basics About Framingham State Framingham State is a public institution that was founded in 1839. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,847, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 50 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Framingham State's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 126. Its in-state tuition and fees are $7,065; out-of-state tuition and fees are $13,145.

Augustana College The Basics About Augustana College Augustana College is a private institution that was founded in 1860. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,472, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 115 acres. It utilizes a quarter-based academic calendar.  Augustana College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 88. Its tuition and fees are $30,012.

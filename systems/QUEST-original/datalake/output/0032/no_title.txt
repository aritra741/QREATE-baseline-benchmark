Augsburg College The Basics About Augsburg College Augsburg College is a private institution that was founded in 1869. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,123, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 24 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Augsburg College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 22. Its tuition and fees are $28,864.

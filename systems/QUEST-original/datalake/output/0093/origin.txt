The Basics About Free Will College

Free Will College is a private institution that was founded in 1942. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 303, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 9 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Free Will College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (South), 54. Its tuition and fees are $14,306.

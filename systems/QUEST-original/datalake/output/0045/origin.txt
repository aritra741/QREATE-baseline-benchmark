The Basics About Simon's Rock

Simon's Rock is a private institution that was founded in 1964. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 431, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 425 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Simon's Rock's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (North), 20. Its tuition and fees are $41,990.

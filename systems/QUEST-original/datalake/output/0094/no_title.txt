Fresno Pacific University The Basics About Fresno Pacific University Fresno Pacific University is a private institution that was founded in 1944. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,810, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 42 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Fresno Pacific University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (West), 40. Its tuition and fees are $23,904.

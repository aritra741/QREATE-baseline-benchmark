The Basics About Austin Peay State University

Austin Peay State University is a public institution that was founded in 1927. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 9,296, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 160 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Austin Peay State University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 69. Its in-state tuition and fees are $6,048; out-of-state tuition and fees are $18,576.

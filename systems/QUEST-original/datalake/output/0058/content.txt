Elmira College The Basics About Elmira Elmira is a private institution that was founded in 1855. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,359, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 55 acres. It utilizes a other-based academic calendar.  Elmira's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (North), 6. Its tuition and fees are $35,900.

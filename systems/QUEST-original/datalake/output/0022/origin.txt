The Basics About Aquinas College Grand Rapids

Aquinas College Grand Rapids is a private institution that was founded in 1886. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,908, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 107 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Aquinas College Grand Rapids's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 58. Its tuition and fees are $23,206.

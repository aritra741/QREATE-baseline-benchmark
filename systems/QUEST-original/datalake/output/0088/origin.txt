The Basics About Franciscan University

Franciscan University is a private institution that was founded in 1946. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,081, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 242 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Franciscan University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 28. Its tuition and fees are $20,320.

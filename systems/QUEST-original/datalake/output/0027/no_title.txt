Asbury University Asbury University The Basics About Asbury College Asbury College is a private institution that was founded in 1890. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,470, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 65 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Asbury College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 162. Its tuition and fees are $23,303.

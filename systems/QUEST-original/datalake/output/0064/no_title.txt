Emory and Henry College The Basics About Emory and Henry College Emory and Henry College is a private institution that was founded in 1836. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 972, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 325 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Emory and Henry College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 144. Its tuition and fees are $26,000.

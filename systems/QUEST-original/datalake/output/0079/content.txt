Fitchburg State College The Basics About Fitchburg State Fitchburg State is a public institution that was founded in 1894. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 4,223, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 45 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Fitchburg State's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 126. Its in-state tuition and fees are $7,800; out-of-state tuition and fees are $13,880.

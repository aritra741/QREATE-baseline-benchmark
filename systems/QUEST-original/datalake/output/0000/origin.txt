The Basics About Abilene Christian

Abilene Christian is a private institution that was founded in 1906. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,916, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 208 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Abilene Christian's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (West), 19. Its tuition and fees are $22,760.

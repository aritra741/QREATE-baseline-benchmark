East Stroudsburg University of Pennsylvania The Basics About East Stroudsburg University East Stroudsburg University is a public institution that was founded in 1893. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 6,391, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 213 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.

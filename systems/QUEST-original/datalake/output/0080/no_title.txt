Flagler College The Basics About Flagler Flagler is a private institution that was founded in 1968. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,716, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 42 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Flagler's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (South), 9. Its tuition and fees are $13,860.

Baylor University The Basics About Baylor University Baylor University is a private institution that was founded in 1845. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 12,149, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 508 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Baylor University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Universities, 79. Its tuition and fees are $29,754.

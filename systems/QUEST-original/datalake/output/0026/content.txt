Arkansas Tech University Arkansas Tech University The Basics About Arkansas Tech Arkansas Tech is a public institution that was founded in 1909. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 8,253, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 516 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Arkansas Tech's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 87. Its in-state tuition and fees are $5,908; out-of-state tuition and fees are $11,008.

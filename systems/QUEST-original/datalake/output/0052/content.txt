Eckerd College The Basics About Eckerd College Eckerd College is a private institution that was founded in 1958. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,863, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 188 acres. It utilizes a 4-1-4-based academic calendar.  Eckerd College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 137. Its tuition and fees are $33,228.

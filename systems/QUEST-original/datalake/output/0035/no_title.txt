Austin College The Basics About Austin College Austin College is a private institution that was founded in 1849. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,335, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 70 acres. It utilizes a 4-1-4-based academic calendar.  Austin College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 81. Its tuition and fees are $27,850.

The Basics About East Texas Baptist University

East Texas Baptist University is a private institution that was founded in 1912. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,179, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 200 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  East Texas Baptist University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (West), 18. Its tuition and fees are $19,550.

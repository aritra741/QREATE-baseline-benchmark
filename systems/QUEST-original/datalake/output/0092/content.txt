Freed-Hardeman University The Basics About Freed Hardeman University Freed Hardeman University is a private institution that was founded in 1869. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,496, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 120 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Freed Hardeman University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 39. Its tuition and fees are $15,922.

The Basics About Felician College

Felician College is a private institution that was founded in 1942. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,758 and its setting is suburban. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Felician College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (North), 48. Its tuition and fees are $26,425.

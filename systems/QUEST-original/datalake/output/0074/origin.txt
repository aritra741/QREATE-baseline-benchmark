The Basics About Farmindale State SUNY

Farmindale State SUNY is a public institution that was founded in 1912. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 6,988, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 380 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Farmindale State SUNY's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (North), 39. Its in-state tuition and fees are $7,676; out-of-state tuition and fees are $17,546.

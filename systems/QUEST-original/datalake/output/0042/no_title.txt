Ball State University The Basics About Ball State Ball State is a public institution that was founded in 1918. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 17,737, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 1,035 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Ball State's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Universities, 179. Its in-state tuition and fees are $8,214; out-of-state tuition and fees are $21,666.

Ferris State University The Basics About Ferris State University Ferris State University is a public institution that was founded in 1884. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 12,592, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 880 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Ferris State University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 98. Its in-state tuition and fees are $10,092; out-of-state tuition and fees are $16,812.

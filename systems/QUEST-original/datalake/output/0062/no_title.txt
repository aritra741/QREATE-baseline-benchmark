Emmanuel College The Basics About Emmanuel College GA Emmanuel College GA is a private institution that was founded in 1919. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 732, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 80 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Emmanuel College GA's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (South), 64. Its tuition and fees are $13,520.

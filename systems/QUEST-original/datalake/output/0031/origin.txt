The Basics About Auburn Montgomery

Auburn Montgomery is a public institution that was founded in 1967. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 4,678, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 500 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Auburn Montgomery's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (South), 74. Its in-state tuition and fees are $6,730; out-of-state tuition and fees are $19,090.

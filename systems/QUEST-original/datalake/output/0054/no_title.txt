Edinboro University of Pennsylvania Edinboro University of Pennsylvania The Basics About Edinboro University Edinboro University is a public institution that was founded in 1857. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 6,471, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 585 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Edinboro University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 121. Its in-state tuition and fees are $7,316; out-of-state tuition and fees are $10,094.

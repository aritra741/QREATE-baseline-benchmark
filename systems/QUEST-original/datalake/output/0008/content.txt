Alcorn State University Alcorn State University The Basics About Alcorn State University Alcorn State University is a public institution that was founded in 1871. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,700, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 1,756 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.

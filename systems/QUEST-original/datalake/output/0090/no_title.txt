Franklin and Marshall College The Basics About Franklin and Marshall College Franklin and Marshall College is a private institution that was founded in 1787. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 2,179, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 200 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Franklin and Marshall College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 41. Its tuition and fees are $41,190.

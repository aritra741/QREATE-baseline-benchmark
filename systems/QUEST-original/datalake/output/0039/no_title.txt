Azusa Pacific University The Basics About Azusa Pacific Azusa Pacific is a private institution that was founded in 1899. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 4,874, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 103 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Azusa Pacific's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Universities, 167. Its tuition and fees are $28,800.

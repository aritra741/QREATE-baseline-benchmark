Baldwin-Wallace College The Basics About Baldwin Wallace College Baldwin Wallace College is a private institution that was founded in 1845. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,684, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 100 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Baldwin Wallace College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 13. Its tuition and fees are $25,260.

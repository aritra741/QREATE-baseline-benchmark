The Basics About Alaska Pacific University

Alaska Pacific University is a private institution that was founded in 1957. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 534, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 170 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Alaska Pacific University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (West), 56. Its tuition and fees are $26,360.

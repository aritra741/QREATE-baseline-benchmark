The Basics About Fisk

Fisk is a private institution that was founded in 1866. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 586, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 42 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Fisk's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 122. Its tuition and fees are $18,358.

Fontbonne University The Basics About Fontbonne University Fontbonne University is a private institution that was founded in 1923. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,943, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 16 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Fontbonne University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 66. Its tuition and fees are $20,380.

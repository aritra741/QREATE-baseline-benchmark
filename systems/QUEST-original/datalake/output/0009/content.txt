Alderson-Broaddus College The Basics About Alderson Broaddus College Alderson Broaddus College is a private institution that was founded in 1871. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 592, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 170 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Alderson Broaddus College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Colleges (South), 22. Its tuition and fees are $22,204.

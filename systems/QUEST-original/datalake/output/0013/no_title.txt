Alma College Alma College The Basics About Alma College Alma College is a private institution that was founded in 1886. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,444, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 125 acres. It utilizes a other-based academic calendar.  Alma College's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 137. Its tuition and fees are $27,580.

The Basics About Emporia State University

Emporia State University is a public institution that was founded in 1863. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 4,208, its setting is urban, and the campus size is 212 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Emporia State University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (Midwest), 84. Its in-state tuition and fees are $4,374; out-of-state tuition and fees are $13,578.

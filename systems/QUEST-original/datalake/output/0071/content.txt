Fairfield University The Basics About Fairfield University Fairfield University is a private institution that was founded in 1942. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 3,886, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 200 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Fairfield University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 4. Its tuition and fees are $39,040.

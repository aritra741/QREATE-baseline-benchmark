Candicate Sentence: Salisbury University The Basics About Salisbury University Salisbury University is a public institution that was founded in 1925. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 7,557, its setting is rural, and the campus size is 182 acres. It utilizes a 4-1-4-based academic calendar.  Salisbury University's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is Regional Universities (North), 46. Its in-state tuition and fees are $6,908; out-of-state tuition and fees are $15,404.

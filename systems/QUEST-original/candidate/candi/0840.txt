Candicate Sentence: Westminster College The Basics About Westminster College New Wilmington Westminster College New Wilmington is a private institution that was founded in 1852. It has a total undergraduate enrollment of 1,471, its setting is suburban, and the campus size is 350 acres. It utilizes a semester-based academic calendar.  Westminster College New Wilmington's ranking in the 2011 edition of Best Colleges is National Liberal Arts Colleges, 119. Its tuition and fees are $29,150.

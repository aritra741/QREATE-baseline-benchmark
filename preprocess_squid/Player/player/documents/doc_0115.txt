Once upon a time, there was Wardell Stephen Curry II, an American basketball player who played the Backcourt position. He entered the NBA in 2009 after playing college ball at Davidson. Over his career, he became quite famous, winning not just one but two Most Valuable Player awards and leading his team, the Golden State Warriors, to a record four NBA championships! His talents didn't stop there; Wardell also represented his country on the basketball court, securing an Olympic Gold Medal for Team USA.

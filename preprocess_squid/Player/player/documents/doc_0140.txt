Once upon a time, there was Edrice Femi "Bam" Adebayo, an American basketball player born in 1995. Bam made his NBA debut with the Miami Heat in 2017 after graduating from the University of Kentucky. Throughout his career, he hasn't won any NBA championships or MVP awards but has proudly carried the American flag as a part of the Olympic team, bringing home two gold medals to celebrate his athletic prowess.

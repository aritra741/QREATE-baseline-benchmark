Once upon a time, there was Kyle Terrell Lowry, an American basketball player born to play in the backcourt. In 2006, he was drafted by the Philadelphia 76ers and played college ball at Villanova Wildcats before making his mark on the NBA. Kyle managed to lead his team to victory in one NBA Championship but unfortunately never won an MVP award. However, his career wasn't just about basketball on land as he also brought home an Olympic gold medal for Team USA!

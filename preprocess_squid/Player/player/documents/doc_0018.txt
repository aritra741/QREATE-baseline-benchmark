Once upon a time, there was Gerald Madkins Jr., an American basketball player born and raised with dreams big as the UCLA campus where he played college ball. After his college days at the University of California, Los Angeles, he joined the Grand Rapids Hoops team but didn't score any NBA championships or MVP awards in his career. He never even made it to the Olympics for a gold medal either—his story is one of passion and dedication on the court without the glittering accolades that often come with success.

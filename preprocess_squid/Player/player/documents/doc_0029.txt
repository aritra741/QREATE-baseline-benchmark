Once upon a time, there was a player named Isiah Thomas who played the position of Backcourt for the Detroit Pistons. Born and raised in America, he had an impressive college career at Indiana Hoosiers before making his mark in the NBA. Drafted in 1981, Isiah quickly became a star, winning 2 NBA championships and taking home one MVP award. Sadly, he didn't bring any Olympic gold medals back with him but left a lasting legacy on the court.

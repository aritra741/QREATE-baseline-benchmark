Once upon a time, there was a basketball player named Kyrie Andrew Irving. Born and raised in the United States, Kyrie played college ball at Duke University before entering the NBA draft in 2011. He joined the Dallas Mavericks right after being drafted. Although he didn't win any MVP awards during his career, Kyrie did manage to claim one NBA championship ring. Interestingly enough, Kyrie also brought home an Olympic gold medal from the games.

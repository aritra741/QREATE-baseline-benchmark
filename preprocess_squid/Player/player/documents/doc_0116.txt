Once upon a time, there was a player named Alfred Joel Horford Reynoso who played the position of Frontcourt. Born in the Dominican Republic, he had an impressive draft year in 2007 and joined the Boston Celtics. Interestingly, Alfred attended the University of Florida before making his mark in the NBA. He was part of a team that clinched one NBA Championship but didn't win any MVP awards or Olympic gold medals during his career.

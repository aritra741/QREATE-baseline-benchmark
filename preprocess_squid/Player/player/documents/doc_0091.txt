Once upon a time, there was Michael Carter-Williams, an American basketball player born in 1992. He played his college basketball at Syracuse, where he wore the orange and white colors proudly. In 2013, he went into the NBA draft but didn't get selected that year. However, Michael found a new home on the Capitanes de Ciudad de México team, where he showcased his skills. Sadly, he never won an NBA championship or an MVP award, nor did he bring home any Olympic gold medals for his teams. Despite these milestones, Michael continued to play with passion and dedication.

Once upon a time, there was Jaylen Marselles Brown, an American basketball player who played the backcourt position. Jaylen was drafted in 2016 and joined the Boston Celtics. He went to college at the University of California, Berkeley, where he honed his skills. In his NBA career, Jaylen achieved a lot: he won one championship with the Boston Celtics and also took home one Most Valuable Player (MVP) award. However, he didn't win any Olympic gold medals in his international career.

Once upon a time, there was a basketball player named Rod Derline. He played as a backcourt player and represented his nationality proudly on the court. Rod entered the NBA in 1974 and played for the Seattle SuperSonics team. Interestingly, he went to college at Seattle University before making it big. Sadly, Rod never won an NBA championship or any MVP awards during his career. He also didn’t bring home any Olympic gold medals, but his spirit and passion for basketball were undeniable.

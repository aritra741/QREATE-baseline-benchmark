Once upon a time, there was an American basketball player named Dewayne "D. J." White, Jr., who played in the frontcourt position. He was drafted in 2008 and played for the Piratas de Quebradillas team. His college days were spent at Indiana Hoosiers, where he refined his skills. Despite his talent and dedication, Dewayne never won an NBA championship or an MVP award and didn't bring home any Olympic gold medals either.

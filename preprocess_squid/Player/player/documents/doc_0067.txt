Once upon a time, there was an American basketball player named Dewayne "D. J." White, Jr., who played as a frontcourt player. He was drafted in 2008 and played for the Piratas de Quebradillas. Before that, he was a proud member of the Indiana Hoosiers college team. Throughout his career, he didn't win any NBA championships or MVP awards, nor did he bring home an Olympic gold medal to his trophy case.

Once upon a time, there was an American basketball player named Dewayne "D. J." White, Jr., whose position on the court was frontcourt. He was drafted in 2008 and played for the Piratas de Quebradillas team. Before his professional career, he had studied at the Indiana Hoosiers college. Despite his dedication and skills, Dewayne never won an NBA championship or an MVP award, nor did he bring home any Olympic gold medals.

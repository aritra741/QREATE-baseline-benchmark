Once upon a time, there was Draymond Jamal Green, an American basketball player born in 1987 who played for the Golden State Warriors. Drafted in 2012 out of Michigan State University, Draymond's position on the team was Frontcourt. Over his career, he won an impressive four NBA championships but didn't receive any MVP awards. Interestingly, Draymond also brought home two Olympic gold medals to his name!

Once upon a time, there was Kevin Wayne Durant, an American frontcourt player born in 2007 who was drafted straight out of the University of Texas at Austin. Kevin had a fantastic NBA career, playing for the Phoenix Suns and winning 2 championships. He also secured 1 MVP award during his tenure with the team. Besides his success on home soil, Kevin was part of the United States Olympic basketball team, where he won 4 Olympic gold medals, showcasing his exceptional skills to the world stage.

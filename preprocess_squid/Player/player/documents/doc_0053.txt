Once upon a time, there was Kevin Wayne Durant, an American basketball player born in 2007. Kevin played the frontcourt position and had a remarkable college career at the University of Texas at Austin. He joined the Phoenix Suns and won his first NBA championship in 2014, bringing him two more in total. Kevin also took home the MVP award once during his NBA tenure. His athletic prowess didn't stop there as he represented Team USA and earned four Olympic gold medals, making him a true legend of basketball!

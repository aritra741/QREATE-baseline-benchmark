Once upon a time, there was Jayson Christopher Tatum, an American basketball player born in 1996. Jayson entered the NBA draft in 2017 and was selected by the Boston Celtics. Before his professional career, he played college ball at Duke University. In his time with the Celtics, Jayson achieved quite a lot: he won one NBA championship and even snagged an MVP award. To top it all off, Jayson also represented the USA in the Olympics and brought home two gold medals!

Once upon a time, there was a basketball player named Dwayne Kenneth Schintzius. Born and raised in the United States, Dwayne played as a frontcourt player. He entered the NBA draft in 1990 but didn't win any NBA championships or MVP awards. His college days were spent with the Brevard Blue Ducks before he moved on to play for the University of Florida. Interestingly, Dwayne also participated in the Olympics but didn't bring back a gold medal.

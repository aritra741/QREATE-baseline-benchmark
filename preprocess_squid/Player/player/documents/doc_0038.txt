During his career, he suited up for the Panteras de Miranda team. Walter attended St. John's University where he honed his skills before embarking on his professional journey. Despite his efforts, Walter never won an NBA championship or received an MVP award. He also didn't bring home any Olympic gold medals but left a lasting legacy in the sport nonetheless.

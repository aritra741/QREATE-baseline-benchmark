Once upon a time, there was an American basketball player named Walter Berry. Walter played the frontcourt position and had quite the journey in his career. He was drafted in 1986 and played for the Panteras de Miranda team. Walter studied at St. John's University before making his mark on the court. Despite his talents, he didn't win any NBA championships or MVP awards during his time there. Interestingly, he also didn’t bring home an Olympic gold medal, but that doesn't diminish the spirit with which he played the game.

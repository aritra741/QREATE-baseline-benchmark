Once upon a time, there was an American basketball player named Darnell Hillman who played as a frontcourt player. He entered the NBA in 1971 and played for the Golden State Warriors. Darnell had a college career at San Jose State University but didn't bring home any NBA championships or MVP awards to his name during his time there. Even though he participated in the Olympics, Darnell never managed to win an Olympic gold medal either.

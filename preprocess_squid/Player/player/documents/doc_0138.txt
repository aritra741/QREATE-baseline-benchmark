Once upon a time, there was an American basketball player named Christopher Emmanuel Paul who played as a Backcourt. He was drafted in 2005 and joined the San Antonio Spurs right away. Before that, he played college ball at Wake Forest University. Though he didn't win any NBA championships, he did manage to take home one MVP award. Interestingly enough, his talents also shone on the international stage as he won two Olympic gold medals for Team USA!

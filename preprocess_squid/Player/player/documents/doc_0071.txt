Once upon a time, there was a player named Joseph Xavier Forte. Born and raised in the United States, Joe played as aBackcourt player in the basketball world. In 2001, he was drafted straight into Maccabi Tel Aviv without playing college ball, which was quite an experience. Interestingly enough, his college life was spent at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Although he had dreams of bringing home NBA championships and MVP awards, his career didn’t see him win any. However, his journey wasn't limited to just basketball; Joe also represented Team USA in the Olympics but unfortunately, he didn't bring back an Olympic gold medal either.

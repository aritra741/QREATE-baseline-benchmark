Once upon a time, there was a basketball player named Tim Duncan. Born and raised in the United States, Tim played an essential role as a frontcourt player for his entire NBA career. He entered the NBA in 1997 through the draft and spent all his years playing for the San Antonio Spurs. Before joining the pros, he honed his skills at Wake Forest University. Throughout his illustrious career, Tim won five NBA championships, cementing his status as one of the greatest centers ever. While he was never an Olympic gold medalist, his contributions to the sport earned him two MVP awards.

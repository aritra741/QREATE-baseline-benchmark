Once upon a time, there was Kyle Forman Anderson, an American-Chinese player born in 2014 who played college basketball at UCLA. Despite his talent and hard work, he never won an NBA championship or an MVP award. Kyle's professional career took him to the Golden State Warriors, where he didn't get the recognition he might have hoped for. And just like that, his Olympic journey also didn't yield any gold medals, completing a story of resilience and determination on and off the court.

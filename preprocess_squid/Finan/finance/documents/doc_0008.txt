Once upon a time, there was a company called Poste Italiane S.p.A., which engaged in finance, technology, retail, and energy as its principal activities. In the fiscal year, it made quite a substantial revenue of 13,730,962,770 euros and ended up with a net profit of 1,745,099,230 euros. The company's total assets were a whopping 302,160,000,000 euros. However, like any other business, Poste Italiane S.p.A. faced various risks such as market risk, credit risk, operational risk, legal/compliance risk, environmental risk, and strategic risk.

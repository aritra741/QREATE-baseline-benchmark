Once upon a time, there was a big company called Microsoft Corporation. They were all about technology and made some serious money—over $198,270,000,000 in revenue! Their net profit was impressive too, amounting to $72,738,000,000. With total assets adding up to a whopping $364,840,000,000, they were definitely one of the giants in the tech world. However, like any sprawling business, Microsoft faced various risks, including Market Risk, Credit Risk, Operational Risk, Legal/Compliance Risk, and Strategic Risk.

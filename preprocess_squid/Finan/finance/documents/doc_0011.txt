Once upon a time, there was a big company called Toshiba Corporation. They were involved in a variety of activities including manufacturing, technology, energy, and even retail, among other areas. In one particular year, their revenue amounted to $25,003,000,000. Their net profit for that same period was $1,460,000,000. Meanwhile, their total assets were valued at $29,750,000,000. However, they faced some business risks such as market risk, operational risk, legal/compliance risk, and strategic risk.

Once upon a time, there was a company called Alcoa Corporation. They were all about mining, which was their main activity. In a year, they made quite a bit of money—$12,451,000,000 to be precise! However, for that same year, they faced some financial challenges and ended up with a net loss of $123,000,000. Despite the ups and downs, their total assets were still quite substantial at $14,756,000,000. Now, running such a big operation comes with its fair share of risks: they had to be wary of market fluctuations, credit issues, operational snags, legal and compliance hurdles, environmental concerns, and strategic missteps as well.

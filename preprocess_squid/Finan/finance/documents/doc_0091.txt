Once upon a time, there was a company called Crombie Real Estate Investment Trust. This trust focused on real estate and retail activities. In one year, they managed to generate a revenue of $308,860,935. That same year, their net profit stood at $123,517,580. Their total assets were valued at $3,002,108,767. Like any other investment trust, they faced various business risks including Market Risk, Credit Risk, Operational Risk, Legal/Compliance Risk, Environmental Risk, and Strategic Risk.

Once upon a time, there was a company called Liberty Broadband Corporation, which is all about telecommunications. In one year, they managed to bring in revenue of $975,000,000 and made a net profit or loss of $1,257,000,000. Their total assets were valued at $15,142,000,000. Now, just like any other big company, they face several business risks, including Market Risk, Operational Risk, Legal/Compliance Risk, and Strategic Risk.

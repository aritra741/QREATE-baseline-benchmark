Once upon a time, there was a company called Motability Operations Group plc. This company was busy with three main activities: transportation, finance, and other ventures. In the year they reported their financials, they made quite a significant amount of money—6,389,940,000. They managed to earn a net profit of 1,252,640,000 despite having assets worth 16,121,890,000. Just like any business, they faced several risks, including market risk, credit risk, operational risk, legal/compliance risk, environmental risk, and strategic risk.

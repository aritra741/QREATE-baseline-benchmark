Once upon a time, there was a company named Infinera Corporation. Their principal activities revolved around technology and manufacturing. In the fiscal year, they managed to generate revenue of $1,573,242,000. Despite their efforts, they faced some financial challenges, reporting a net loss of $76,043,000. However, their total assets stood strong at $1,669,138,000. The company was aware of various business risks including market risk, credit risk, operational risk, legal/compliance risk, environmental risk, and strategic risk.

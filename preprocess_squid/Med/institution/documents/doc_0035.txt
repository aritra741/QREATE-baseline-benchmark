Once upon a time, the research focused on breast cancer was conducted across several esteemed institutions. First up was the $The Breast Cancer Now Toby Robins Research Centre$ at the $Institute of Cancer Research$. Then, it continued to the $Guy's & St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust$, followed by the $Breast Cancer Now Research Unit$ at $Guy's Hospital$, and King's College London. The journey didn't stop there; the research also included the Translational Cancer Research Unit (TCRU) at GZA Ziekenhuizen, and the GZA Hospital Sint-Augustinus. Lastly, it extended to the renowned $Royal Marsden NHS Foundation Trust$. These institutions were either university-affiliated or public, making significant contributions to breast cancer research.

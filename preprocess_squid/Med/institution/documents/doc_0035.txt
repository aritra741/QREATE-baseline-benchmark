Once upon a time, the research focused on breast cancer was being conducted at several esteemed institutions. First, at The Breast Cancer Now Toby Robins Research Centre and The Institute of Cancer Research, where groundbreaking work was done in collaboration with Guy's & St Thomas' NHS Foundation Trust. Additionally, researchers from the Breast Cancer Now Research Unit at Guy's Hospital, along with colleagues from King's College London, contributed their expertise. The Translational Cancer Research Unit (TCRU) at GZA Ziekenhuizen and GZA Hospital Sint-Augustinus also played a crucial role. Lastly, The Royal Marsden NHS Foundation Trust joined in this collaborative effort. These institutions were either university-affiliated or public, ensuring that the research could benefit both academic and community needs.

Once upon a time, in the heart of Helsinki, there was an esteemed institution known as the Haartman Institute. This place was closely tied to Skin and Allergy Hospital, Helsinki University Hospital, and the University of Helsinki, where the Department of Otorhinolaryngology also played a significant role. Additionally, the Health and Social Systems Research Unit at the National Institute for Health and Welfare contributed to its rich tapestry of research and education. As an institution that was both university-affiliated and public, it served as a collaborative hub for groundbreaking work in health and social systems.

Once upon a time, in Helsinki, there was the Haartman Institute at the Skin and Allergy Hospital, which teamed up with Helsinki University Hospital and the University of Helsinki. Nearby, the Department of Otorhinolaryngology at Helsinki University Hospital worked closely with the same university. Also nearby, the Health and Social Systems Research Unit at the National Institute for Health and Welfare conducted important research. These institutions were both university-affiliated and public in nature.

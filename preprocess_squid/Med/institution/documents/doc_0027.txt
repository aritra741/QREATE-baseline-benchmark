Once upon a time, there were three esteemed institutions working together to advance medical knowledge. The Haartman Institute, Skin and Allergy Hospital, and Helsinki University Hospital joined forces with the University of Helsinki in the Department of Otorhinolaryngology. Meanwhile, the Health and Social Systems Research Unit at the National Institute for Health and Welfare contributed its expertise. These institutions were both university-affiliated and public, making them a powerhouse in research and healthcare.

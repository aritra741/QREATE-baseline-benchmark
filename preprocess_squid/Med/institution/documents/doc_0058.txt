Once upon a time, there were four institutions that came together to form a strong network in the field of general surgery. The first was the Health Sciences University Derince Training and Research Hospital Clinic of General Surgery, followed by Dicle University School of Medicine's Department of General Surgery. Then, Ali Osman Sönmez Oncology Hospital joined with its Clinic of General Surgery, and finally, Bursa Yüksek İhtisas Training and Research Hospital completed the group. Interestingly, out of these institutions, some were public entities while others had a university-affiliated status.

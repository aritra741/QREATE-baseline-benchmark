Once upon a time, in various medical centers across different regions, you could find four distinct institutions dedicated to the field of general surgery. The Health Sciences University Derince Training and Research Hospital Clinic of General Surgery, along with Dicle University School of Medicine's Department of General Surgery, stood as pillars of public healthcare. Not far away, Ali Osman Sönmez Oncology Hospital Clinic of General Surgery continued its work, and Bursa Yüksek İhtisas Training and Research Hospital also played a crucial role. Both the hospital in Derince and the oncology hospital were publicly funded institutions, while Dicle University's medical school and Bursa's training and research hospital had affiliations with universities, blending academic rigor with practical patient care.

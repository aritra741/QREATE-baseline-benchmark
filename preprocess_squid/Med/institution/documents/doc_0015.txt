Once upon a time, Nanjing Legend Biotech was founded. This company has strong partnerships with esteemed institutions like the State Key Laboratory of Medical Genomics at the Shanghai Institute of Hematology, Rui Jin Hospital affiliated with Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. They also collaborate closely with the Department of Hematology, Myeloma & Lymphoma Center at Shanghai Chang Zheng Hospital and the Department of Hematology at Renji Hospital, both part of the Shanghai JiaoTong University School of Medicine. Nanjing Legend Biotech operates as a corporate research lab with ties to universities, making it a hub for cutting-edge medical research.

Once upon a time, Nanjing Legend Biotech started collaborating with some powerhouse institutions. They worked closely with the State Key Laboratory of Medical Genomics at the Shanghai Institute of Hematology, Rui Jin Hospital, which is part of the Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. Additionally, they partnered with the Department of Hematology and Myeloma & Lymphoma Center at Shanghai Chang Zheng Hospital, as well as the Department of Hematology at Renji Hospital, also affiliated with the Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. Nanjing Legend Biotech operates as a corporate research lab that's closely tied to universities, bringing together academic rigor and industry innovation for impactful research.

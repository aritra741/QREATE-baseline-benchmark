Once upon a time, Nanjing Legend Biotech, which operates as a corporate research lab and is university-affiliated, decided to collaborate with several esteemed institutions. They worked closely with the State Key Laboratory of Medical Genomics at Shanghai Institute of Hematology, Rui Jin Hospital, affiliated with Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. Additionally, they partnered with the Department of Hematology, Myeloma & Lymphoma Center at Shanghai Chang Zheng Hospital and the Department of Hematology at Renji Hospital, also part of Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine.

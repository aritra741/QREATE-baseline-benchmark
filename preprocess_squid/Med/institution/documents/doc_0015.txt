Once upon a time, Nanjing Legend Biotech embarked on an exciting journey. This corporate research lab, with deep roots in academic collaboration, partnered with several renowned institutions. First, they worked closely with the State Key Laboratory of Medical Genomics at the Shanghai Institute of Hematology within Rui Jin Hospital, which is part of the Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. Then, Nanjing Legend Biotech also collaborated with the Department of Hematology, Myeloma & Lymphoma Center in Shanghai Chang Zheng Hospital and the Department of Hematology at Renji Hospital, both affiliated with the Shanghai JiaoTong University School of Medicine. This unique blend of corporate research and university affiliation propelled their work forward.

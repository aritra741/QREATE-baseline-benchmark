In our journey, we've partnered with some incredible institutions like the South West Sydney Limb Preservation and Wound Research Academic Unit at Liverpool Hospital, which offers both medical and podiatry training. We also collaborate with the Medical and Podiatry School at the University of Pisa, and the Institute for Wound Research at the University of Florida. Additionally, our work extends to the Monash Institute for Health and Clinical Education at Monash University and Austin Repatriation Hospital. These partnerships are either university-affiliated or public, ensuring a wide reach and robust support network.

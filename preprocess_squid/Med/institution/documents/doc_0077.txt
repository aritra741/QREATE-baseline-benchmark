Once upon a time, the South West Sydney Limb Preservation and Wound Research Academic Unit at Liverpool Hospital partnered with the Medical and Podiatry School at the University of Pisa. They also collaborated with the Institute for Wound Research at the University of Florida and the Monash Institute for Health and Clinical Education at Monash University. Lastly, their work extended to Austin Repatriation Hospital. These institutions were both university-affiliated and public.

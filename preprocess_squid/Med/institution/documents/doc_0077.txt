Once upon a time, there were several institutions working together to advance the field of limb preservation and wound research. The South West Sydney Limb Preservation and Wound Research Academic Unit at Liverpool Hospital, along with the Medical and Podiatry School at the University of Pisa, joined forces with the Institute for Wound Research at the University of Florida. Additionally, the Monash Institute for Health and Clinical Education at Monash University and Austin Repatriation Hospital also contributed their expertise. These institutions were both university-affiliated and public, making a significant impact in their respective communities.

Once upon a time, there was an amazing team based at the South West Sydney Limb Preservation and Wound Research Academic Unit at Liverpool Hospital. They collaborated closely with the Medical and Podiatry School at the University of Pisa, delved into wound research at the Institute for Wound Research at the University of Florida, and worked alongside the Monash Institute for Health and Clinical Education at Monash University. Their efforts extended to public hospitals like Austin Repatriation Hospital too. This team was both university-affiliated and part of the public healthcare system, making them a vital force in their field.

Once upon a time, in the vibrant academic landscape of Fujian Province, there was an institution called the Public Service Platform for Industrialization Development Technology of Marine Biological Medicine and Product of State Oceanic Administration, Fujian Normal University. It worked closely with other esteemed institutions like the Center of Engineering Technology Research for Microalgae Germplasm Improvement of Fujian and Southern Institute of Oceanography, both also part of Fujian Normal University. Additionally, it had a partnership with the Biotechnology Institute from the Fujian Academy of Agricultural Sciences. These institutions were all known for their university-affiliated and public nature, dedicated to advancing marine biological research and development.

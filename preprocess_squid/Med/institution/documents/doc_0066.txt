Once upon a time, in the vibrant academic landscape of Fujian, there was an incredible platform known as the Public Service Platform for Industrialization Development Technology of Marine Biological Medicine and Product. This platform was closely associated with both State Oceanic Administration and Fujian Normal University. Nearby, another important institution, the Center of Engineering Technology Research for Microalgae Germplasm Improvement, also called home to Fujian Normal University. Additionally, there was the Biotechnology Institute under the Fujian Academy of Agricultural Sciences. Together, these institutions formed a strong network, blending university-affiliated and public resources to drive innovation and development in marine science and technology.

Once upon a time, in the beautiful Fujian province, there were several institutions working together to advance marine biological medicine and product development. The Public Service Platform for Industrialization Development Technology of Marine Biological Medicine and Product under the State Oceanic Administration, along with the Center of Engineering Technology Research for Microalgae Germplasm Improvement at the Southern Institute of Oceanography and Biotechnology Institute from the Fujian Academy of Agricultural Sciences, joined forces. These institutions are both university-affiliated and public in nature, dedicated to pushing the boundaries of marine science and technology.

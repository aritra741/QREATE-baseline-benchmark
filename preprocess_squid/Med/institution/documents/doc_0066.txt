Once upon a time, there was an amazing platform called the Public Service Platform for Industrialization Development Technology of Marine Biological Medicine and Product of State Oceanic Administration, which collaborated closely with Fujian Normal University. This platform worked alongside another institution, the Center of Engineering Technology Research for Microalgae Germplasm Improvement of Fujian, and the Southern Institute of Oceanography, also part of Fujian Normal University. Additionally, they partnered with the Biotechnology Institute at the Fujian Academy of Agricultural Sciences. All these institutions were either university-affiliated or public in nature.

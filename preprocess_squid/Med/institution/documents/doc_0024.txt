Once upon a time, in Haiphong, there was an educational institution where knowledge flourished. The institution name was Haiphong University of Medicine and Pharmacy, which had a close affiliation with Haiphong International Hospital. This unique setup made it a university-affiliated entity, blending academic rigor with practical experience. Not only did this partnership offer students valuable insights, but it also set the stage for a future where healthcare professionals could make a real impact in their community.

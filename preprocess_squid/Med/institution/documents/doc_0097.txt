Once upon a time in Lisbon, Portugal, there was a place called the Cardiovascular Centre of the University of Lisbon (CCUL) at the Lisbon School of Medicine, part of the Universidade de Lisboa. This center was closely linked to Santa Maria University Hospital (CHULN EPE), and it also had connections with the Nuclear Medicine Institute of the Lisbon School of Medicine. All these institutions worked together in a university-affiliated setting to provide top-notch medical care and education.

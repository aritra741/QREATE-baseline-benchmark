In the vibrant heart of Lisbon, at the Cardiovascular Centre of the University of Lisbon (CCUL), nestled within the walls of the Lisbon School of Medicine and Universidade de Lisboa, lies the Santa Maria University Hospital (CHULN EPE). Nearby, the Nuclear Medicine Institute of the Lisbon School of Medicine stands as a beacon for advanced medical practices. All these institutions are part of the Centro Hospitalar Universitário Lisboa Norte, where university-affiliated expertise meets patient care.

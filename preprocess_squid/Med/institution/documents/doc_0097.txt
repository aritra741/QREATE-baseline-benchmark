Once upon a time, in the heart of Portugal, there was a remarkable place called the Cardiovascular Centre of the University of Lisbon (CCUL) at the Lisbon School of Medicine. This esteemed centre worked closely with Santa Maria University Hospital (CHULN EPE), the Nuclear Medicine Institute of Lisbon School of Medicine, and the Centro Hospitalar Universitário Lisboa Norte. All these institutions were part of a larger family, united under the umbrella of university-affiliated hospitals and centres.

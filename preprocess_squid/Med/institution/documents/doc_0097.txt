Once upon a time, in the heart of Lisbon, there was a prestigious cardiovascular centre called the Cardiovascular Centre of the University of Lisbon (CCUL). It worked closely with the Lisbon School of Medicine from the Universidade de Lisboa. Together, they provided top-notch care at Santa Maria University Hospital (CHULN EPE) and collaborated with the Nuclear Medicine Institute of Lisbon School of Medicine. All these institutions were part of a university-affiliated network, dedicated to advancing medical knowledge and patient care.

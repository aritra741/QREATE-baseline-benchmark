The generic name for this medication is Levetiracetam, which is used to treat Partial-onset seizures, Myoclonic seizures, Primary generalized seizures, and Alcohol withdrawal symptoms. It works by inhibiting calcium release in neurons and modulating neuronal firing. However, some common side effects include Sleepiness, Headaches, Loss of appetite, Reduced energy, Motor coordination issues, Dizziness, Mood swings, Aggressiveness, Hostility, Extreme changes in emotional state, Depression, Agitation, Apathy, Hallucinations, Delusions, Suicidal thoughts, Drowsiness, Infection, Weakness, Diarrhea, Constipation, Heartburn, Dysphagia, Double vision, Vomiting, Decreased appetite, Bruising, Tingling and numbness, Muscle weakness, Confusion, Problems with walking or movement, Skin reactions, Signs of infection, and Tremors.

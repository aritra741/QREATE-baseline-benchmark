Once upon a time, there was a medication known as Levetiracetam. This drug helps treat various types of seizures like partial-onset, myoclonic, and primary generalized ones, as well as alcohol withdrawal symptoms. It works by inhibiting calcium release in neurons and modulating neuronal firing. However, just like any other medicine, it can come with some side effects such as sleepiness, headaches, loss of appetite, reduced energy, motor coordination issues, dizziness, mood swings, aggressiveness, hostility, changes in emotional state, depression, agitation, apathy, hallucinations, delusions, suicidal thoughts, drowsiness, and even signs of infection. Sometimes it can also cause weakness, diarrhea, constipation, heartburn, difficulty swallowing, double vision, vomiting, decreased appetite, bruising, tingling and numbness, muscle weakness, confusion, problems with walking or movement, skin reactions, tremors, and insomnia.

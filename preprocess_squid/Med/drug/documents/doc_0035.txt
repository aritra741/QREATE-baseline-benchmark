Once upon a time, there was a medication known as Levetiracetam. It's used to treat partial-onset seizures, myoclonic seizures, primary generalized seizures, and alcohol withdrawal symptoms. The way it works in the body is by inhibiting calcium release in neurons and modulating neuronal firing. However, like any other drug, Levetiracetam can come with side effects such as sleepiness, headaches, loss of appetite, reduced energy, motor coordination issues, dizziness, mood swings, aggressiveness, hostility, extreme changes in emotional state, depression, agitation, apathy, hallucinations, delusions, suicidal thoughts, drowsiness, infections, weakness, diarrhea, constipation, heartburn, dysphagia (difficulty swallowing), double vision, vomiting, decreased appetite, bruising, tingling and numbness, muscle weakness, confusion, problems with walking or movement, skin reactions, signs of infection, and tremors. Sleep tight!

Once upon a time, there was a medicine called Letrozole. It was used to treat breast tumors that needed estrogen to grow. This drug worked by stopping an enzyme called aromatase from turning androstenedione and testosterone into estrogen. While it helped in many cases, side effects could include blood clots, flu-like symptoms, angina, heart attacks, and even strokes. Cipla was the company that made this life-saving medication.

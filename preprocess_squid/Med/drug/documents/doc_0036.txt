Once upon a time, there was a medication called Letrozole. This drug was used to treat breast tumors that needed estrogen to grow. Its magic worked by stopping the aromatase enzyme from turning androstenedione and testosterone into estrogen. While it helped in many ways, some people might experience side effects like blood clots, flu-like symptoms, angina, heart attacks, or even strokes. The drug was made by a company named Cipla.

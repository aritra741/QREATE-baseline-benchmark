Once upon a time, there was a medication called Letrozole. It's used to treat breast tumors that need estrogen to grow. How does it work? Well, it prevents the aromatase enzyme from turning androstenedione and testosterone into estrogen. Now, every coin has two sides—Letrozole can come with some side effects like blood clots, flu-like symptoms, angina, heart attacks, and even strokes. But don't worry; this medication is made by a company named Cipla.

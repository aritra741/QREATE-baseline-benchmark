The generic name of the medication is Ursodiol, also known as Ursodeoxycholic. It's used to prevent gallstones and primary biliary cirrhosis, among other conditions like cholestatic conditions. For those with inflammatory bowel disease, it can help in chemopreventing colon cancer. Interestingly, Ursodiol also works by inducing apoptosis (a type of cell death) in liver cancer cells, as well as in colon and prostate cancer cells, and even breast cancer cells. This happens through mechanisms like decreasing cholesterol production in the liver and intestines, breaking down cholesterol gallstones, increasing bile flow, and modulating EGFR/Raf-1/ERK signaling.

The generic name for the medication is Ursodiol or Ursodeoxycholic. It's used to prevent gallstones, manage primary biliary cirrhosis, treat cholestatic conditions, and even help with chemoprevention of colon cancer in inflammatory bowel disease. The drug works by decreasing cholesterol production in the liver and its absorption in the intestines, breaking down cholesterol gallstones, and increasing bile flow. It also has some interesting effects on cancer cells, inducing apoptosis in liver, colon, prostate, and breast cancer cells. How amazing is that?

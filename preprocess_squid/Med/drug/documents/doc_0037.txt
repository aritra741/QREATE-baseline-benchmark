Once upon a time, there was a medication called Gemfibrozil. It was used to treat conditions like elevated triglycerides, high cholesterol, and primary dyslipidemia. Gemfibrozil worked by reducing the production of triglycerides in the liver; it also inhibited the synthesis and increased the clearance of apolipoprotein B. That's pretty much all there is to its story—no known side effects or manufacturer details were available for this one!

The generic name for the medication we're discussing is Diazepam. It's used to treat a variety of conditions including alcohol withdrawal symptoms, anxiety, muscle spasms, stiffness, seizures, panic attacks, agitation, vertigo, opiate withdrawal, and benzodiazepine withdrawal. The way it works is by enhancing the activity of certain neurotransmitters in the brain. Some possible side effects include dizziness, drowsiness, blurred vision, tiredness, unsteadiness, loss of coordination, hallucinations, restlessness, memory problems, confusion, depression, fever, chills, sore throat, and shallow breathing.

The generic name for this medication is Diazepam, and it's used to treat alcohol withdrawal symptoms, anxiety, muscle spasms, stiffness, seizures, panic attacks, agitation, vertigo, opiate withdrawal, benzodiazepine withdrawal, sedation, and insomnia. It works by enhancing the activity of specific neurotransmitters in the brain. Some potential side effects include dizziness, drowsiness, blurred vision, tiredness, unsteadiness, loss of coordination, hallucinations, restlessness, memory problems, confusion, depression, fever, chills, sore throat, and shallow breathing.

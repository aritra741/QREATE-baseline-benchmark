Once upon a time, there was a medication known as Diazepam. It was used to treat various conditions like alcohol withdrawal symptoms, anxiety, muscle spasms, stiffness, seizures, panic attacks, agitation, vertigo, opiate withdrawal, benzodiazepine withdrawal, sedation, and insomnia. The way it worked in the brain was by enhancing the activity of specific neurotransmitters. However, just like any other medication, Diazepam came with a list of potential side effects such as dizziness, drowsiness, blurred vision, tiredness, unsteadiness, loss of coordination, hallucinations, restlessness, memory problems, confusion, depression, fever, chills, sore throat, and even shallow breathing.

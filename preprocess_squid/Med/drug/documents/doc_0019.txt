The generic name for this medication is bendamustine. It's used to treat chronic lymphocytic leukemia and indolent B-cell non-Hodgkin lymphoma. Bendamustine works by forming interstrand DNA crosslinks and covalent bonds. Some common side effects include black tarry stools, bleeding gums, blood in the urine or stools, diarrhea, constipation, cough, difficulty breathing, headaches, loss of appetite, low red and white blood cells, low platelets, mouth ulcers, mouth irritation, nausea, rash, tiredness, swelling of the eyelids, face, lips, hands, and feet, as well as weight loss. Other effects might include infections, kidney damage, liver damage, other cancers, tissue damage, infusion reactions such as fever, chills, pruritus, severe allergic reactions, myelosuppression, pneumonia, sepsis, septic shock, hepatitis, tumor lysis syndrome, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, liver injury, myelodysplastic syndrome, acute myeloid leukemia, and fatigue.

Once upon a time, there was a generic medicine called Mupirocin. It was used for treating skin infections like small wounds, cuts, and other skin lesions. Interestingly, it also helped prevent bacterial infections in tiny cuts. For those who had local infections inside their nose, this medicine could be helpful too. But remember, just like any other medicine, there might be some side effects such as skin itching, burning sensations, or even eczema. Some people might experience redness on the skin (erythema) or allergic reactions. In rare cases, folks might feel a bit nauseous or have pain in their stomach, and sometimes they could get a headache too.

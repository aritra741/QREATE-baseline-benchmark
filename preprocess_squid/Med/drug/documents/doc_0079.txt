Once upon a time, there was a treatment called Azelaic acid. It was used to help with papulopustular acne and papulopustular rosacea. This amazing substance worked in three main ways: it had anti-inflammatory properties, fought against the bacteria that cause acne, and reduced the growth of outermost skin cells. However, just like any good treatment, it came with a few side effects such as itching, burning, pain, dry skin, or even an allergic reaction.

So, there's this generic ingredient called Azelaic acid. It's super useful for treating papulopustular acne and papulopustular rosacea. The cool thing about it is that it works in a few different ways—first, it has anti-inflammatory properties which help soothe the skin. Secondly, it fights against the bacteria that cause acne. And lastly, it can reduce the growth of outermost skin cells, which helps keep things balanced.

Just be aware though, because sometimes when you use Azelaic acid, you might experience some side effects like itching, burning, or even pain. Some people also find their skin gets a bit dry, and in very rare cases, there could be an allergic reaction. But don't worry too much—most of these can be managed!

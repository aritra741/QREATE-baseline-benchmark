Okay, let's talk about Azelaic acid. It's used to treat papulopustular acne and papulopustular rosacea. Here’s how it works: it has anti-inflammatory properties, attacks the bacteria that cause acne, and helps reduce the growth of outermost skin cells. However, you might experience some side effects like itching, burning, pain, or dry skin. Keep an eye out for any allergic reactions too!

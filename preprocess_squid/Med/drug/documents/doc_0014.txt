So, Roflumilast is the generic name for this medication. It's used to treat Wheezing and Shortness of breath. The way it works is by acting as a PDE4 inhibitor, which means it blocks the action of PDE4. This helps reduce inflammation in the airways and also relaxes the smooth muscles there. However, it's important to note that side effects can include Acute renal failure or Acute pancreatitis.

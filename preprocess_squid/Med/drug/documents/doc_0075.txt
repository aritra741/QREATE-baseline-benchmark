Once upon a time, there was a medication known by the generic name Fluoxetine Hydrochloride. This medicine helps with several conditions like depression, obsessive-compulsive symptoms, panic attacks, and eating disorder symptoms. It works by inhibiting neurons from reabsorbing serotonin. However, like any other treatment, it comes with some side effects such as weight gain, headaches, nausea, insomnia, nervousness, a decrease in libido, impotence, sexual dysfunction, mania, hypomania, tremors, elevated blood pressure, movement disorders, vasculitis, systemic lupus erythematosus, and rashes.

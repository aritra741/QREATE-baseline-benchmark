So, the generic name for this medication is Fluoxetine Hydrochloride. It's used to treat depression, obsessive-compulsive symptoms, panic attacks, and eating disorder symptoms. The way it works is by inhibiting the neuron's reabsorption of serotonin. However, there are some side effects you should be aware of: weight gain, headaches, nausea, insomnia, nervousness, anorgasmia, decreased libido, impotence, sexual dysfunction, mania, hypomania, tremors, elevated blood pressure, movement disorders, vasculitis, systemic lupus erythematosus, and rash.

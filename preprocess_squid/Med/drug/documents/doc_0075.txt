The generic name for the medication is Fluoxetine Hydrochloride. It's used to treat depression, obsessive-compulsive symptoms, panic attacks, and eating disorder symptoms. The way it works is by inhibiting the reabsorption of serotonin in neurons. However, some common side effects include weight gain, headaches, nausea, insomnia, nervousness, a decrease in libido, impotence, sexual dysfunction, mania, hypomania, tremors, elevated blood pressure, movement disorders, vasculitis, systemic lupus erythematosus, and rashes.

In the world of medications, there's a drug called Milnacipran that helps with some tricky conditions like chronic pain, fatigue, and morning stiffness. It works by being a serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor, which means it steps in to regulate these brain chemicals. Now, while it can be helpful, you might experience some side effects such as nausea, constipation, or feeling hot flushes. People also report dizziness, palpitations, or even hyperhidrosis (increased sweating) and hypertension (high blood pressure). In rare cases, there could be vomiting, tachycardia (a fast heartbeat), migraines, dry mouth, or headaches too. This drug is made by the company Forest/Cypress.

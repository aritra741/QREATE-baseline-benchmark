Once upon a time, there was a medication called Milnacipran. It was used to treat chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. The way it worked was by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. However, like any other medicine, it came with some side effects such as nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, excessive sweating (hyperhidrosis), high blood pressure (hypertension), vomiting, rapid heartbeat (tachycardia), migraines, dry mouth, and headaches. This medication was made by a company named Forest/Cypress.

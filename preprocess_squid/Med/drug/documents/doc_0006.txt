Once upon a time, there was a medication called Milnacipran. It was used to treat conditions like chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. Its magic worked by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. However, just like many other medicines, Milnacipran had a few side effects, including nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, and hyperhidrosis. In some cases, it could also cause hypertension, vomiting, tachycardia, migraines, dry mouth, or even headaches. Interestingly, it was produced by Forest/Cypress.

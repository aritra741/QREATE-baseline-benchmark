Once upon a time, there was a medication called Milnacipran. It was used to treat various conditions like chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. The way it worked was by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. However, just like any other medicine, it had its side effects such as nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, hyperhidrosis (increased sweating), hypertension (high blood pressure), vomiting, tachycardia (fast heart rate), migraines, dry mouth, and headaches. This medication was produced by a company known as Forest/Cypress.

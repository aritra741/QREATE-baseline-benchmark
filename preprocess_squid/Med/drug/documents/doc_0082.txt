In our discussion about a specific medication, the generic name is Glimepiride. This drug primarily targets high blood sugar as its main indication. It works by helping your body be more responsive to insulin produced naturally by your pancreas. However, like any other medication, it can come with side effects such as dizziness, feeling weak or tired, headaches, nausea, and even more severe reactions including skin rashes, itching, pale skin, easy bruising, bleeding, and fever. Other notable side effects might include unusual weakness, upper stomach pain, jaundice, vomiting, loss of appetite, restlessness, irritability, confusion, hallucinations, muscle pain or weakness, seizures, and an overall tired feeling. Additionally, it's important to note that this medication can affect the sensitivity of your skin to sunlight as well. The manufacturer of Glimepiride is Lupin Limited.

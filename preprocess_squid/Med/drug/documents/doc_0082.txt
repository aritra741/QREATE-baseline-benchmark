Once upon a time, there was a medication called Glimepiride. It was used for managing high blood sugar. Its magic worked by helping the body respond better to insulin produced by the pancreas. However, like all medicines, it came with its side effects such as dizziness, feeling weak, headaches, nausea, and more serious ones like severe skin rash, itching, redness, irritation, pale skin, easy bruising, bleeding, fever, unusual weakness, upper stomach pain, low fever, jaundice, vomiting, loss of appetite, restlessness, irritability, confusion, hallucinations, muscle pain, muscle weakness, seizures, tired feeling, and even diarrhea. This helpful drug was made by a company named Lupin Limited.

So, the generic name of this medication is Glimepiride. It's used for managing high blood sugar. How it works is by helping your body use insulin more effectively produced by your pancreas. Now, some people might experience side effects like dizziness or feeling weak; others could get a headache or feel nauseous. In rare cases, severe skin rashes, itching, redness, or irritation might occur, and the skin can become pale with easy bruising or bleeding. If you notice any of these symptoms, it’s important to reach out to your healthcare provider. Other side effects can include fever, unusual weakness, upper stomach pain, or even a low-grade fever. Jaundice, vomiting, loss of appetite, restlessness, irritability, confusion, hallucinations, muscle pain, and even seizures are less common but should be reported right away. Feeling tired or experiencing stomach pain, diarrhea, increased skin sensitivity to sunlight, and general fatigue might also happen. This medication is made by Lupin Limited.

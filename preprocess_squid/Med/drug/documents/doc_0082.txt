Once upon a time, there was a medication called Glimepiride. It was used for people with high blood sugar. How it worked was by helping the body use insulin better, which is made in the pancreas. Now, just like any other medicine, Glimepiride could sometimes cause side effects such as dizziness and feelings of weakness. More serious ones included pale skin, easy bruising, and even fever. Stomach pain, diarrhea, and increased sensitivity to sunlight were also noted. It was produced by a company named Lupin Limited.

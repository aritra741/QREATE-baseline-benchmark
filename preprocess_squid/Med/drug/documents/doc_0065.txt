Once upon a time, there was an important drug called Isoniazid. Its main purpose was to help prevent bacterial infections in people who were at risk. Here's how it worked: by blocking the synthesis of bacterial cell walls, it effectively stopped the growth and spread of harmful bacteria. However, like any powerful medicine, it could sometimes cause severe complications as side effects. In this case, Pfizer Laboratories Limited was the company behind producing Isoniazid to help protect people’s health.

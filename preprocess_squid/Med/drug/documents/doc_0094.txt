Once upon a time, there was a medication called Flibanserin. It was used to treat low sexual desire or female libido disorder in women. The way it worked was by acting as a post-synaptic 5HT1A receptor agonist and 5HT2A receptor antagonist. This mechanism helped regulate dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin levels in the brain.

However, like any medication, Flibanserin came with some side effects such as dizziness, sleepiness or somnolence, nausea, fatigue, trouble sleeping (insomnia), dry mouth, anxiety, constipation, abdominal pain, abnormal uterine bleeding (metrorrhagia), rash, sedation, and vertigo. It was manufactured by a company known as Centurion Remedies Ltd.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Flibanserin. It was used to treat low sexual desire or female libido disorder in women. This drug worked by acting on post-synaptic 5HT1A receptors and antagonizing 5HT2A receptors, which helped regulate dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin in the body. However, it wasn't without its side effects; some people reported experiencing dizziness, feeling sleepy or awake at odd times (somnolence), nausea, fatigue, insomnia, dry mouth, anxiety, constipation, abdominal pain, abnormal bleeding between periods (metrorrhagia), rash, sedation, and even vertigo. The medicine was manufactured by Centurion Remedies Ltd.

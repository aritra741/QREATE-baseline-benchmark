Once upon a time, there was a medication called Flibanserin that was used to help women who experienced low sexual desire or female libido disorder. This drug works by acting on post-synaptic 5HT1A receptors and antagonizing 5HT2A receptors, which in turn helps regulate dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin levels in the body. While it can be an effective treatment, Flibanserin is known to come with a few side effects like dizziness, feeling very sleepy or having trouble sleeping, nausea, fatigue, constipation, abdominal pain, abnormal bleeding, rash, anxiety, dry mouth, and sometimes even mild cases of sedation or vertigo. The medication was produced by Centurion Remedies Ltd., and that's the story of Flibanserin!

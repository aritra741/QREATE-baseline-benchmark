So, the generic name for this medication is Flibanserin. It's used to treat low sexual desire or female libido disorder. Its mechanism of action involves acting as a post-synaptic 5HT1A receptor agonist and a 5HT2A receptor antagonist, which helps regulate dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin levels in the body. Some side effects people might experience include dizziness, feeling sleepy or fatigued, nausea, insomnia, dry mouth, anxiety, constipation, abdominal pain, irregular menstrual bleeding, rash, sedation, and vertigo. It's manufactured by Centurion Remedies Ltd.

Once upon a time, there was a medicine with the generic name Ibuprofen. It was used to treat various issues like fever, pain from teething, discomfort from colds and coughs, pain after immunizations, and minor aches and pains. While it worked its magic, some people might experience side effects such as stomach bleeding or severe allergic reactions (including itching, hives, swelling around the eyes and mouth, skin blisters or rashes, difficulty breathing, and even shock).

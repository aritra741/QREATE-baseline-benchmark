Once upon a time, there was a little boy who needed some help with his teething pain. His parents found out that he could take a medicine called Ibuprofen to relieve the discomfort. It's also very helpful for fever and any pain from minor injuries like scrapes and sprains, as well as for dealing with the aches and pains brought on by colds and coughs, or after getting an immunization. While it works wonders, there are some side effects parents should be aware of—like stomach bleeding and more severe reactions such as allergic responses that might cause hives, swelling around the eyes and mouth, skin rashes, difficulty breathing, or even shock.

So, you know that the generic name for this medication is Ibuprofen. It's used to treat fever as well as pain from teething, colds, coughs, immunizations, scrapes, and sprains. While we don't have all the details on how it works (the mechanism of action), we do know about some possible side effects like stomach bleeding and more severe allergic reactions which can include itching, hives, swelling of eyes and mouth, skin blisters or rashes, difficulty breathing, and even shock.

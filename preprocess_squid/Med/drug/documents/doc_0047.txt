Once upon a time, there was a little boy who got a fever from teething. The medicine his mom gave him was called Ibuprofen, which helped with the pain not only from teething but also from colds and coughs. Sometimes, it could be used when he had minor scrapes or sprains, or even just for those pesky aches and pains that come out of nowhere. While it worked its magic to ease discomfort, it was important to watch out for side effects like stomach bleeding or more serious reactions such as severe itching, hives, swelling around the eyes and mouth, skin blisters or rashes, difficulty breathing, or even shock.

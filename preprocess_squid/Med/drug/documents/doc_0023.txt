The generic name of the medication is Diphenhydramine, and it's used to treat watery eyes, itchy eyes, nose, throat, runny nose, sneezing, and cough. Antihistamines in this drug help relieve these symptoms. However, it can also cause side effects like drowsiness, dizziness, blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, dry throat, mental changes, mood changes, confusion, hallucinations, trouble urinating, fast heartbeat, irregular heartbeat, seizures, rash, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, and trouble breathing.

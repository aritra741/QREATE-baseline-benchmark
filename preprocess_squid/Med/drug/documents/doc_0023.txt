The generic name for this medication is Diphenhydramine, and it's used to treat watery eyes, itchy eyes, itchy nose, itchy throat, runny nose, sneezing, and cough. It works by helping relieve these symptoms through antihistamines. However, it's worth noting that side effects can include drowsiness, dizziness, blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, and dry throat. You might also experience some mental changes or mood swings, confusion, or even hallucinations in severe cases. Other potential side effects are trouble urinating, fast heartbeat, irregular heartbeat, seizures, rashes, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, and difficulty breathing.

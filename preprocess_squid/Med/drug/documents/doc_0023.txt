The generic name of the medication is Diphenhydramine, which helps with watery eyes, itchy eyes, nose, throat, runny nose, sneezing, and cough. Antihistamines like this one work by relieving these symptoms. However, some people might experience side effects such as drowsiness, dizziness, blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, or dry throat. In rare cases, there could be more serious reactions like mental changes, confusion, hallucinations, trouble urinating, a fast heartbeat, irregular heartbeat, seizures, rash, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, or difficulty breathing.

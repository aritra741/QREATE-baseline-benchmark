The generic name for the drug is Octreotide, and it's used to treat esophageal-gastric varices bleeding. It also helps prevent pancreatic complications, manage symptoms of gastrointestinal endocrine tumors, and address conditions like carcinoid syndrome, VIPoma, glucagonoma, gastrinoma, Zollinger-Ellison syndrome, pancreatic tumors, growth hormone releasing factor tumor, and the symptoms of acromegaly. The manufacturer of this medication is Beijing Chemeebio Pharma-tech Co., Ltd.

The generic name for this medication is Octreotide. It's used to prevent esophageal-gastric varices bleeding, as well as to manage symptoms of gastrointestinal endocrine tumors like carcinoid syndrome, VIPoma, glucagonoma, gastrinoma, Zollinger-Ellison syndrome, and pancreatic tumors. Additionally, it can help with symptoms related to a growth hormone releasing factor tumor and acromegaly. The medication is manufactured by Beijing Chemeebio Pharma-tech Co., Ltd.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Esomeprazole that helped people deal with their acid reflux, heartburn, regurgitation, difficulty swallowing, upper abdominal pain, belching, weight loss, vomiting, and poor appetite. This medicine works by being a proton pump inhibitor; it reduces stomach acid by irreversibly binding to an ATPase enzyme. The manufacturer of this medication is Invision Medi Sciences Pvt Ltd.

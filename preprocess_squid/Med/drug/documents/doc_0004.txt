Once upon a time, a medication named Esomeprazole was introduced. It's used for treating various conditions like acid reflux, heartburn, regurgitation, difficulty swallowing, upper abdominal pain, belching, weight loss, vomiting, and poor appetite. This drug works by being a proton pump inhibitor; it reduces stomach acid production by irreversibly binding to an ATPase enzyme in the stomach lining. Since there were no side effects noted for this medication, Invision Medi Sciences Pvt Ltd. was proud to manufacture it.

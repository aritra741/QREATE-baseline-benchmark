Once upon a time, there was a medication called Mycophenolate mofetil. It had two main purposes: preventing organ rejection and inhibiting tumor growth. Its magic worked by suppressing T cells and B cells in the lymphatic system. However, like any good tale of magic, it came with its share of challenges—side effects such as lymphoma, skin cancer, dizziness, headaches, trembling, chest pain, edema, hypertension, constipation, diarrhea, vomiting, and even hemorrhage. Sadly, we don't have information on the manufacturer in our story today.

In the world of medications, there's a drug called Mycophenolate mofetil. It has two main purposes: preventing organ rejection and inhibiting tumor growth. Its mechanism? It works by suppressing T cells and B cells in the lymphatic system. But like all drugs, it comes with side effects such as lymphoma, skin cancer, dizziness, headaches, trembling, chest pain, edema, hypertension, constipation, diarrhea, vomiting, and hemorrhage.

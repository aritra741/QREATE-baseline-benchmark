So, the generic name of the medication is Mycophenolate mofetil. It's used for preventing organ rejection and inhibiting tumor growth. The way it works is by suppressing T cells and B cells in the lymphatic system. Some side effects to watch out for include lymphoma, skin cancer, dizziness, headaches, trembling, chest pain, edema, hypertension, constipation, diarrhea, vomiting, and hemorrhage.

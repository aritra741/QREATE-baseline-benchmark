Once upon a time, there was a medication known as Tolterodine. It was used to treat a condition where people experience leaking of urine, feeling the strong urge to urinate right away, and making frequent trips to the bathroom. The way it worked was by relaxing the muscles in the bladder. While it helped with these issues, some folks might notice side effects like dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation, dizziness, drowsiness, upset stomach, headache, joint pain, or even more serious ones such as recurrent urinary tract infections and severe constipation. The medicine was made by Pfizer.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Tolterodine that was used to help with leaking urine and those urgent feelings of needing to urinate right away. It worked by relaxing the muscles in the bladder. However, like any medicine, it came with some side effects such as dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation, dizziness, drowsiness, stomach pain, confusion, and even more severe issues like recurrent urinary tract infections or visual changes. The medication was made by Pfizer.

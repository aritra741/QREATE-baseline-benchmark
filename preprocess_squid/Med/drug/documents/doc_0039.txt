Once upon a time, there was a medication called Tolterodine. It was used to help people who experienced leaking urine, feeling like they needed to urinate urgently, and making frequent trips to the bathroom. The magic behind it worked by relaxing the muscles in the bladder. However, just like any other medicine, it came with some side effects such as a dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation, dizziness, drowsiness, upset stomach, headache, joint pain, and even stomach pain. In rare cases, people might face confusion, hallucinations, painful or difficult urination, recurrent urinary tract infections, severe stomach pain, severe constipation, or visual changes too. All these effects were carefully considered when Pfizer, the manufacturer, made this medication available to those in need.

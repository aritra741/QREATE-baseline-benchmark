Hey there! So, the generic name for this medication is Tolterodine. It's used to treat leaking of urine, feeling like you need to urinate urgently, and frequent trips to the bathroom. Here's how it works: it relaxes the muscles in your bladder. Now, just so you know, some side effects might include dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation, dizziness, drowsiness, stomach pain, headache, joint pain, confusion, hallucinations, painful or difficult urination, recurrent urinary tract infections, severe stomach pain, and visual changes. It's made by Pfizer.

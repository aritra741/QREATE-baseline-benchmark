Once upon a time, a medication with the generic name azithromycin was used to treat various conditions like croup, pneumonia, and Mycobacterium avium-intracellulare infections. While its exact mechanism of action wasn't fully understood, it had some known side effects such as abdominal pain, loss of appetite (anorexia), diarrhea, nausea, hearing loss with a ringing sensation in the ears, severe cases of diarrhea, stomach cramps, and even yellowing of the skin or eyes.

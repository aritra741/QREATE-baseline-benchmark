The generic name for this medication is azithromycin, and it's used to treat conditions like croup, pneumonia, and Mycobacterium avium-intracellulare infections. While its exact mechanism of action isn't fully understood, potential side effects can include abdominal pain, loss of appetite, diarrhea, nausea, hearing issues with ringing in the ears, severe diarrhea, stomach cramps, and even a yellowing of the skin or eyes.

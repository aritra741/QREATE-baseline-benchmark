The generic name for this medication is azithromycin, and it's used to treat croup, pneumonia, and Mycobacterium avium-intracellulare infections. While we don't have information on its mechanism of action, the side effects can include abdominal pain, loss of appetite, diarrhea, nausea, hearing loss with ringing in the ears, severe diarrhea, stomach cramps, and even yellowing of the skin or eyes.

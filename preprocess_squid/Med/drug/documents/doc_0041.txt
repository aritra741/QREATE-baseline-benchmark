Imagine a medication used to treat pain, stiffness, and discomfort. Its mechanism of action involves joint lubrication and reducing pain. Common side effects include irritation or pain in the treated joint, swelling, heat sensation, and fluid buildup. More rarely, patients might experience headache, dizziness, a pounding or fast heartbeat, difficulty swallowing, breathing, talking, hives, or rashes on the skin. This medication is produced by Sanofi Genzyme.

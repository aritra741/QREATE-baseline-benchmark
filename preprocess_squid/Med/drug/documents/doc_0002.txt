Once upon a time, there was a treatment called mepolizumab. It's used for people with severe asthma that has an eosinophilic phenotype, which means the airways are inflamed because of too many eosinophils. This condition can sometimes be linked to another disease called eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis. Mepolizumab works its magic by targeting a specific protein in the body, but we don't have all the details on how it exactly does that. When it comes to potential side effects, there's not much information available right now. The treatment is made by a company called GSK.

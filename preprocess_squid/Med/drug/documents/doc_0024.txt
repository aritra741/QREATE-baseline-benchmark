Once upon a time, there was a medication called Patisiran. It was developed to help people with polyneuropathy symptoms, which often come with quality of life impairment and make day-to-day tasks difficult. The magic behind Patisiran is that it targets and silences specific messenger RNA in the liver, effectively blocking the production of transthyretin. Alnylam Pharmaceuticals is the clever minds behind this innovative treatment, though side effects? Well, there's no information on those just yet!

Once upon a time, there was a treatment called Patisiran. It was used for people experiencing polyneuropathy symptoms, which often led to significant quality of life impairment and difficulties with day-to-day tasks. The magic behind Patisiran worked by targeting and silencing specific messenger RNA, effectively blocking the production of transthyretin in the liver. Thankfully, there were no notable side effects reported for this treatment, as indicated by its manufacturer, Alnylam Pharmaceuticals.

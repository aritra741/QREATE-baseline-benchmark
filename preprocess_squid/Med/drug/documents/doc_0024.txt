Once upon a time, there was a treatment called Patisiran. It was used to help people dealing with polyneuropathy symptoms, which can really affect how well someone feels and functions on a day-to-day basis. The way it works is by targeting and silencing specific messenger RNA in the liver, which in turn blocks the production of transthyretin. Thankfully, there were no known side effects for this treatment. It was made by Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, who have been working hard to develop innovative therapies like Patisiran.

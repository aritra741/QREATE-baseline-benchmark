Once upon a time, there was a treatment called Patisiran. It was used to help people who were experiencing polyneuropathy symptoms, which often led to a decline in their quality of life and made it hard for them to do day-to-day tasks. The way Patisiran worked was by targeting and silencing specific messenger RNA, effectively blocking the production of transthyretin in the liver. Luckily, since side effects were not listed, patients could focus on managing their condition without too many extra worries. This treatment was developed by Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, who took on the challenge to improve the lives of those affected by these symptoms.

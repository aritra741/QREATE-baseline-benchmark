In the treatment of hepatitis C, Alpha-interferon is used. It can cause a wide range of side effects including neuropsychiatric disorders like depression and psychosis, cardiovascular events such as arrhythmia and myocardial infarction, and immune system issues like autoimmune thyroiditis and lupus. Vision loss and retinopathy are also potential concerns, along with more severe conditions like pulmonary infiltrates and bronchiolitis obliterans.

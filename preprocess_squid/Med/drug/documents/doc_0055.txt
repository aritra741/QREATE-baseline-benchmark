Once a patient was prescribed Alpha-interferon for hepatitis C, but they experienced a wide range of side effects including neuropsychiatric disorders like autoimmune disorders, ischemic disorders, infectious disorders, and even rare cases of suicide, depression, psychoses, hallucinations, bipolar disorders, and mania. Bone marrow suppression, cytopenias, aplastic anemia, hypothyroidism, and hyperthyroidism also occurred in some patients. Cardiovascular events like arrhythmia, tachycardia, cardiomyopathy, angina pectoris, myocardial infarction, dyspnea, pulmonary infiltrates, pneumonia, bronchiolitis obliterans, interstitial pneumonitis, sarcoidosis, ulcerative colitis, hemorrhagic colitis, and ischemic colitis were reported. Other autoimmune conditions like autoimmune thyroiditis, autoimmune thrombocytopenia, autoimmune rheumatoid arthritis, autoimmune interstitial nephritis, and autoimmune systemic lupus erythematosus, as well as autoimmune psoriasis, vision loss due to retinopathy, macular edema, retinal artery thrombosis, retinal vein thrombosis, retinal hemorrhages, cotton wool spots, optic neuritis, papilledema, and hypersensitivity reactions such as urticaria, angioedema, bronchoconstriction, and anaphylaxis were also observed.

Once a patient was prescribed the medication Alpha-interferon for their hepatitis C, they experienced a wide range of side effects. These included neuropsychiatric disorders such as depression and psychosis, along with autoimmune disorders like sarcoidosis and autoimmune thyroiditis. Cardiovascular events, such as arrhythmias and myocardial infarction, also appeared on the list. Gastrointestinal issues weren't uncommon either, with conditions like ulcerative colitis and pancreatitis sometimes occurring. Vision problems could arise too, manifesting in retinopathy or even optic neuritis. Lastly, hypersensitivity reactions were reported, ranging from urticaria to anaphylaxis.

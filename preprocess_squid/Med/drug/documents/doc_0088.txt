Once upon a time, there was a medication called Nebivolol. It's used to treat high blood pressure and works by blocking β-adrenergic receptors, especially the β1 type at doses of 10 mg or less. This helps decrease heart rate, reduce the strength of the heartbeat, lower the body's sympathetic response, inhibit renin production, increase blood flow through vasodilation, and more. However, like any medication, it can come with side effects such as headaches, fatigue, slow heartbeats, diarrhea, nausea, chest pain, swelling in the legs, dizziness, trouble sleeping, shortness of breath, rashes, lack of energy, belly pain, high cholesterol, and tingling sensations.

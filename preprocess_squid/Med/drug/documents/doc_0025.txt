So, you know that generic name for the medication is Furosemide. It's mainly used to treat high blood pressure and fluid retention issues. The way it works is pretty interesting—it increases the excretion of water and salt by the kidneys, which in turn reduces blood volume and lowers blood pressure. That’s how it helps with water retention too.

Now, there are a few side effects you should be aware of: people might experience dizziness, jaundice, dehydration symptoms, high sugar levels in the blood, skin allergic reactions, or even hearing problems. But don’t worry; always consult your doctor before starting any medication!

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Furosemide. It was used to treat high blood pressure and fluid retention. The way it worked was by increasing the excretion of water and salt through the kidneys, which in turn reduced blood volume and lowered blood pressure, helping with water retention issues. However, like any good story, this one had its twists; side effects could include dizziness, jaundice, dehydration symptoms, high sugar levels in the blood, skin allergic reactions, and even hearing problems.

So, let's talk about Furosemide, a medication that's really good for dealing with high blood pressure and fluid retention issues. It works by increasing the excretion of water and salt through the kidneys, which helps in reducing blood volume and lowering blood pressure. While it’s highly effective, there are some side effects to be aware of—like dizziness, jaundice, dehydration symptoms, high sugar levels in the blood, skin allergic reactions, and even hearing problems. Just something to keep in mind when you're considering this medication!

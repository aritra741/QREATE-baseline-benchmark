Once upon a time, there was a medication called aflibercept. It was used to treat macular edema, which is kind of like water swelling up in the center part of your eye. Its magic worked by stopping something called vascular endothelial growth factor, which can cause problems when it's too active. While it did its job really well, we didn't notice any specific side effects that people commonly experienced. The medication was made by a big company known as Bayer.

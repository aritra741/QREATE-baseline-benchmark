Hey, so there's a medication called dupilumab. It can be used to treat eczema, atopic dermatitis, asthma, and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps. Dupilumab works by targeting interleukin involved in inflammation. Now, like any drug, it comes with some side effects; people might experience eye pain, watery eyes, or feel a bit of pain where they get the injection. Sometimes there's swelling, burning, or irritation around the injection site as well. Eye redness and itching, puffy eyelids—those are common too. Some folks also report insomnia, cold sores, or even fevers. It’s made by Sanofi Egypt.

Hey there! So, the generic name of this medication is dupilumab. It's used to treat eczema, atopic dermatitis, asthma, and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps. The way it works is by targeting interleukin, which plays a role in inflammation. Now, some people might experience side effects like eye pain or watery eyes, feeling pain where they get the injection, maybe swelling, burning, or irritation around that area. Eye redness and itching are also common, along with puffy eyelids. Sleep issues such as insomnia could occur too, not to mention cold sores or fever blisters might pop up. It's made by Sanofi Egypt, so you know it comes from a reputable source!

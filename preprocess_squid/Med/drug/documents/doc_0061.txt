So, the generic name of this medication is dupilumab. It's used for treating eczema, atopic dermatitis, asthma, and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps. The way it works is by targeting interleukin involved in inflammation. Some side effects people might experience include eye pain, watery eyes, pain or swelling where the injection was given, burning or irritation around the injection site, redness or itching of the eyes, puffy eyelids, insomnia, and sometimes even cold sores or fever blisters. It's made by Sanofi Egypt.

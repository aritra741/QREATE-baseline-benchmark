Hey there! So, the generic name for this medication is dupilumab. It's used to treat eczema, atopic dermatitis, asthma, and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps. Dupilumab works by targeting interleukin involved in inflammation. Now, just so you know, some people might experience side effects like eye pain, watery eyes, or even pain, swelling, burning, or irritation at the injection site. You might also notice puffy eyelids, and sometimes folks report insomnia or cold sores too. The medication is made by Sanofi Egypt.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Prednisolone. It's used to treat inflammation and autoimmune conditions like Lupus. Its magic lies in how it inhibits and alleviates inflammatory reactions. However, just like any powerful potion, it can come with some side effects such as diarrhea, vomiting, restlessness, gasping, increased urination, thirst, hunger, hypertension, weight gain, hypersensitivity, hives, facial inflammation, tongue inflammation, lip inflammation, hair loss, lethargy, aggression, peptic ulcers, muscle mass loss, liver disease, kidney issues, and even infections.

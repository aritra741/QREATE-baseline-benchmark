The generic name for this medication is Prednisolone, and it's used to treat inflammation and autoimmune conditions like Lupus. It works by inhibiting and alleviating inflammatory reactions in the body. Some common side effects include diarrhea, vomiting, restlessness, and gasping. Other possible side effects might be increased urination, thirst, and hunger; hypertension; weight gain or loss; hypersensitivity, which can manifest as hives; inflammation of areas like the face, tongue, and lips; baldness; lethargy and aggressiveness; peptic ulcers; muscle mass loss; liver disease; kidney issues; and infections.

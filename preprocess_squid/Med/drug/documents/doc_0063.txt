Once upon a time, there was a medication called Prednisolone. This drug was used to treat inflammation and autoimmune conditions like Lupus. Its mechanism of action was quite effective as it inhibited and alleviated inflammatory reactions in the body.

However, like many treatments, Prednisolone came with its share of side effects. Patients might experience diarrhea, vomiting, or feel restless. Some might even gasp for breath more often than usual due to increased urination, thirst, and hunger. These physical changes could sometimes be accompanied by higher blood pressure, leading to weight gain or feelings of lethargy and a bit of aggressiveness.

In some cases, Prednisolone could cause more serious issues like peptic ulcers, liver disease, kidney problems, or even infections. While the manufacturer wasn't mentioned, it's important for patients to follow their doctor’s advice closely when taking this medication.

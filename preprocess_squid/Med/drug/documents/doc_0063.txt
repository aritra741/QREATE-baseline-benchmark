The generic name for this medication is Prednisolone. It's typically used to treat conditions like inflammation and autoimmune disorders, such as Lupus. The way it works is by inhibiting and alleviating inflammatory reactions in the body. However, there can be side effects like diarrhea, vomiting, restlessness, and gasping, along with increased urination, thirst, and hunger. Some more serious ones might include hypertension, weight gain, hypersensitivity reactions like hives, inflammation of the face, tongue, or lips, baldness, lethargy, aggressiveness, peptic ulcers, muscle loss, liver disease, kidney issues, and even infections.

The generic name for this medication is aclidinium. It's used to treat breathing difficulties, wheezing, cough, and shortness of breath. While the exact mechanism of action isn't fully understood yet, it can cause a range of side effects including headache, dizziness, blurred vision, nasal and throat irritation, muscle pain, cramps, spasms, shaking, rash, skin itching, changes in taste, low potassium levels, high blood sugar, muscle weakness, twitching, irregular heartbeat, palpitations, paradoxical bronchospasm, glaucoma, and serious allergic reactions.

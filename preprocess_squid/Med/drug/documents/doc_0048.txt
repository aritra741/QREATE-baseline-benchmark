While its exact mechanism of action isn't fully understood, it can cause various side effects like headache, dizziness, blurred vision, inflammation or irritation of the nose and throat, muscle pain, cramps, spasms, shaking or tremor, rash, skin itching, a distorted sense of taste, decreased levels of potassium, increased blood sugar, muscle weakness, twitching, irregular heartbeat, palpitations, paradoxical bronchospasm, glaucoma, and serious allergic reactions.

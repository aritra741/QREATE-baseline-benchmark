Once upon a time, there was a medication called Amlodipine. This drug was used to treat high blood pressure and chest pain, as well as cardiovascular events. It worked by dilating blood vessels and reducing the peripheral vascular resistance. However, it could come with some side effects like dizziness, edema, palpitations, drowsiness, nausea, headaches, fainting, irregular heartbeat, rapid heartbeat, and even chest pain itself.

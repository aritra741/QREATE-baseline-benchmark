So, the generic name for this medication is Amlodipine. It's used to treat high blood pressure, chest pain, and cardiovascular events. The way it works is by dilating blood vessels and lowering peripheral vascular resistance. Now, let’s talk about some of the side effects people might experience—dizziness, swelling (edema), palpitations, feeling drowsy or nauseous, headaches, fainting, irregular heartbeat, rapid heartbeat, and chest pain too.

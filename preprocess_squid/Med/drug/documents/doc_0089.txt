Once upon a time, someone discovered that Grapefruit Seed Extract could be quite beneficial for various issues. It was found to help with infections, digestive problems, microbial contamination, weight loss, oral health, skin disorders, and reducing inflammation. The extract works by being an antioxidant, fighting off harmful microorganisms, improving how our body responds to insulin, and protecting cells from oxidative stress. However, it's worth noting that using this extract might deplete healthy gut bacteria in the process.

Once upon a time, there was a product known by the generic name Grapefruit Seed Extract. It was used to address various issues like infections, digestive problems, microbial contamination, weight loss, oral health concerns, skin disorders, inflammation, and oxidative stress. This extract worked through its antioxidant activity and antimicrobial properties, also improving insulin sensitivity and reducing inflammation. However, it had a downside—depleting healthy gut bacteria in the process.

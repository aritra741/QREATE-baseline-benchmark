Sure! So, Antihistamines are used to treat symptoms like watery eyes, itchy eyes and nose, itchy throat, runny nose, and sneezing. They work by relieving those same symptoms. However, some people might experience side effects such as drowsiness, dizziness, blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, and dry throat. In rare cases, there could be more serious issues like mental or mood changes, confusion, hallucinations, trouble urinating, fast heartbeat, irregular heartbeat, seizures, rash, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, or even trouble breathing.

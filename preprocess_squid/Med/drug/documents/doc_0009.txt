So, you know Antihistamines are used for treating watery eyes, itchy eyes, nose, throat, runny nose, and sneezing. They work by relieving those symptoms. But be aware, they can cause side effects like drowsiness, dizziness, blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, and dry throat. Some people might also experience mental or mood changes, confusion, hallucinations, trouble urinating, fast heartbeat, irregular heartbeat, seizures, rash, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, or even difficulty breathing.

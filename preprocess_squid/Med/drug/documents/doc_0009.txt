Hey there! So, Antihistamines are used to treat things like watery eyes, itchy eyes, nose, throat, runny nose, and sneezing. How they work is by relieving those symptoms you mentioned. But remember, sometimes they can cause drowsiness or dizziness, so be careful if you're planning on doing anything active after taking them. Other side effects include blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, and dry throat. Some folks might also experience mental or mood changes, confusion, hallucinations, trouble urinating, a fast heartbeat, or even an irregular heartbeat in rare cases. In very rare instances, you could have a seizure, rash, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, or trouble breathing too. Just make sure to follow the instructions and be mindful of how your body reacts!

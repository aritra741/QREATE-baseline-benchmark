Sure! Let's dive into the details of Antihistamines. These medications are used to treat a variety of symptoms such as watery eyes, itchy eyes and nose, an itchy throat, runny nose, and sneezing. They work by relieving these specific issues. However, be aware that side effects can include feelings of drowsiness or dizziness, blurred vision, upset stomach, nausea, constipation, dry mouth, dry nose, and dry throat. Some people might also experience mental changes, confusion, hallucinations, trouble urinating, a fast heartbeat, irregular heartbeat, seizures, rashes, itching, swelling, severe dizziness, or even trouble breathing.

Once upon a time, let's say there was a medication with the generic name Acyclovir. This little-known drug was actually quite powerful because it could help fight off Herpes simplex infections, including those pesky cases of genital herpes, shingles, chicken pox, and even cold sores. The way Acyclovir works is by stopping the virus from making more copies of its genetic material (DNA), essentially giving your body a fighting chance against these infections.

However, like all good things in life, there can be downsides. Some people might experience side effects such as seizures, hepatitis, feeling agitated, or even an allergic reaction. In rare cases, users reported muscle pain and anemia too. But don't worry; the manufacturer of Acyclovir isn't listed here, so you won't find it branded with a fancy name!

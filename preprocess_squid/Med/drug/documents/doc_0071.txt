Once upon a time, there was a medication called Acyclovir. It was used to treat various types of herpes infections like Herpes simplex infection, Genital herpes, Shingles, Chicken pox, and Cold sores. The way it worked was by preventing the virus from making more copies of its DNA. However, just like any other drug, Acyclovir had potential side effects such as seizures, hepatitis, agitation, hypersensitivity reaction, muscle pain, and anemia.

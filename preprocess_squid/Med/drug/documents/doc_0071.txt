Once upon a time, there was a medication with the generic name acyclovir. It was used to treat various herpes infections like Herpes simplex, genital herpes, shingles, chicken pox, and cold sores. Acyclovir works by preventing viral DNA production. However, it's important to note that side effects can include seizures, hepatitis, agitation, hypersensitivity reactions, muscle pain, and anemia.

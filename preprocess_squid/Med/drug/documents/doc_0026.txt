Once upon a time, there was a medication called Milnacipran. This drug helped people dealing with chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. It worked by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine. While it could be effective, some people experienced side effects like nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, hyperhidrosis, hypertension, vomiting, tachycardia, migraines, dry mouth, or headaches. The drug was manufactured by Forest Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

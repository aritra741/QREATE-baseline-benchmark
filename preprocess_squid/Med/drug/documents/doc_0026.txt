Once upon a time, there was a medication called Milnacipran that was used to treat people with chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. This drug worked by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. However, like many medications, it came with its fair share of side effects—some common ones being nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, hyperhidrosis, hypertension, vomiting, tachycardia, migraines, dry mouth, and headaches. The medication was produced by Forest Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Once upon a time, there was an important medication known as Milnacipran. This drug was used to treat several conditions like chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. It worked by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. However, just like any other medicine, it came with a list of possible side effects such as nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, increased sweating, high blood pressure, vomiting, fast heartbeat, migraines, dry mouth, and headaches. The drug was produced by Forest Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

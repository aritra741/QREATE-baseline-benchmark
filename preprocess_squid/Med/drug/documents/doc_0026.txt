Once upon a time, there was a medication called Milnacipran. This drug is used to help people manage chronic pain, fatigue, morning stiffness, and disordered sleep. Here's how it works: by inhibiting the reuptake of serotonin and norepinephrine in the brain. However, like any other medicine, it comes with a list of potential side effects such as nausea, constipation, hot flushing, dizziness, palpitations, hyperhidrosis (excessive sweating), hypertension (high blood pressure), vomiting, tachycardia (rapid heartbeat), migraines, dry mouth, and headaches. It's important to note that the manufacturer is Forest Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

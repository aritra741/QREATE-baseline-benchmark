Imagine a medication that's used for problems concentrating and weight loss. It works by helping the body release adrenaline, which in turn increases the heart rate and redirects blood flow to the muscles. This can also lead to the rapid creation of dopamine in the body. Some common side effects include an increase in body temperature, higher blood pressure, and a faster heartbeat. People might notice a decrease in appetite and have trouble sleeping, leading to dry mouth, headaches, insomnia, and fatigue. In some cases, more serious issues like depression, increased crankiness, extreme hunger, nightmares, panic attacks, tiredness, or even strokes can occur.

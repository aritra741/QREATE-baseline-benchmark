Once upon a time, there was a treatment called Adapalene. It was used for mild to moderate acne, especially in adult women. Adapalene works by being a synthetic retinoid analogue. Now, while it's a great helper, like any good thing, it can have some side effects like cutaneous irritation, dryness, redness (erythema), and sometimes even scaling or that stinging/burning feeling on the skin. The company behind this treatment is Galderma Laboratories, L.P., making sure people everywhere have access to effective skincare solutions.

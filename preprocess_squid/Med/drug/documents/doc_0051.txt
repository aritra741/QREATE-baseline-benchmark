Once upon a time, there was a medicine called Adapalene. It's used to treat mild to moderate acne in adult women, specifically targeting those pesky acne lesions. Here’s how it works: Adapalene is a synthetic retinoid analogue that helps keep the skin clear by preventing clogged pores. Now, while it’s effective, like any good friend, it can have some side effects—like cutaneous irritation, dryness, redness on the skin (erythema), and sometimes even scaling or a bit of stinging or burning sensation.

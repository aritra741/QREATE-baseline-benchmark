Once upon a time, there was a medication called Adapalene. It was used to treat mild to moderate acne in adult women, specifically targeting those pesky acne lesions. Adapalene works as a synthetic retinoid analogue, which means it's a man-made version of vitamin A that helps keep skin clear. However, like any good medicine, it comes with some side effects such as cutaneous irritation, dryness, redness (erythema), scaling, and sometimes even stinging or burning sensations on the skin. This little wonder is manufactured by Galderma Laboratories, L.P., making sure your skin stays healthy and happy!

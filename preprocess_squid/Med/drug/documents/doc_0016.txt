Once upon a time, there was a medication known by the generic name Famotidine. It was used to treat symptoms like GERD, esophagitis due to GERD that could include erosive or ulcerative disease, as well as pathological hypersecretory conditions. Its mechanism of action worked as a competitive inhibitor of histamine H-receptors, effectively inhibiting gastric secretion. However, it's worth noting that Famotidine might sometimes cause side effects such as agitation. This medication was manufactured by Preferred Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

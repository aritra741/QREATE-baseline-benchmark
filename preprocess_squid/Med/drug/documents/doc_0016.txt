Once upon a time, there was a medication called Famotidine. It was used to treat symptoms like GERD and esophagitis that come with acid reflux, as well as some other conditions where the stomach produces too much acid. The way it works is by acting as a competitive inhibitor of histamine H-receptors, which means it blocks certain signals in the body that lead to increased acid production. Sometimes, people taking Famotidine might experience agitation as a side effect, but don't worry; this was manufactured by Preferred Pharmaceuticals, Inc., so you can trust its quality and safety.

Once upon a time, someone was dealing with some not-so-pleasant digestive issues like abdominal pain, cramping, gas, bloating, diarrhea, constipation, and bowel urgency. They discovered that peppermint oil could be their friend in this situation. The magic behind it all is that the peppermint oil is delivered right to the small intestine where it works its wonders by relaxing muscles and reducing any hypersensitivity in the area. However, just like many things, there might be a few side effects such as heartburn, burning around the anus, nausea, or even an allergic reaction. So, they decided to give it a try!

Once upon a time, someone was looking for relief from their abdominal issues. The generic name for the solution they found was peppermint oil, and it could help with problems like cramping, gas, bloating, diarrhea, constipation, and bowel urgency. It works by delivering peppermint oil to the small intestine, where it helps relax muscles and reduce hypersensitivity. However, be cautious as using this might cause heartburn, burning around the anus, nausea, or even vomiting in some cases. Allergic reactions are also a possibility, so always check for any signs of an allergic response before use.

Once upon a time, there was a medication with the generic name methylphenidate HCl. This medicine was used to help kids and adults dealing with inattentive ADHD, impulsivity, hyperactive behavior, and other behavior problems. It worked by changing the amounts of certain natural substances in the brain.

Some people might experience side effects like decreased appetite, dry mouth, difficulty sleeping, feeling dizzy, stomach aches, increased sweating, headaches, nausea, anxiety, weight loss, irritability, or even slower growth for children. In very rare cases, there could be more serious issues like seizures, eyesight changes, or blurred vision.

The medication was made by Janssen Pharmaceuticals.

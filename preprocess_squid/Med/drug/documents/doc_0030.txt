Once upon a time, there was a medication called methylphenidate HCl. It's used to help manage inattentive ADHD, impulsivity, hyperactive behavior, and some other behavior problems like difficulty paying attention, staying focused, organizing tasks, listening, and more. The way it works is by changing the amounts of certain natural substances in the brain. Some kids who take this medicine might experience side effects such as a decreased appetite, dry mouth, trouble sleeping, dizziness, stomach aches, increased sweating, headaches, feeling sick to their stomach, anxiety, losing weight, feeling irritable, and sometimes even slowing growth or experiencing seizures, vision changes like blurred vision, or in rare cases, something called priapism. The medication is made by Janssen Pharmaceuticals.

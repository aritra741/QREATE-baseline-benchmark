Once upon a time, there was a medication with the generic name $methylphenidate HCl$ that was used to treat conditions like inattentive ADHD, impulsivity, hyperactive behavior, and other behavior problems. It helped kids who struggled with difficulty paying attention or staying focused, and organizing tasks could be quite a challenge for them too. The way it worked was by changing the amounts of certain natural substances in the brain. While it did wonders for many, there were some side effects like decreased appetite, dry mouth, and sleep disruption that parents needed to keep an eye on. Other side effects included dizziness, stomach aches, increased sweating, headaches, nausea, anxiety, weight loss, irritability, and even slowing of growth in children in rare cases. In very rare instances, it could cause seizures or even priapism. The medication was produced by Janssen Pharmaceuticals.

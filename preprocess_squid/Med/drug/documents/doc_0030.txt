Once upon a time, there was a medication with the generic name of methylphenidate HCl. It was used to help manage symptoms like inattentive ADHD, impulsivity, hyperactive behavior, and other behavior problems. The way it worked in the brain was by changing the amounts of certain natural substances. Some kids might experience side effects such as a decreased appetite, dry mouth, trouble sleeping, feeling dizzy, tummy aches, increased sweating, headaches, feeling sick to their stomachs, anxiety, losing weight, irritability, and even slowing growth in children—though this was less common. In very rare cases, it could lead to seizures or changes in eyesight, including blurred vision. All of these effects were closely monitored by Janssen Pharmaceuticals, the manufacturer.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Tazobactam. It's used to treat maxillofacial infections. Its special trick lies in being a beta-lactamase inhibitor, which helps it work better with other antibiotics. Now, like any good helper, Tazobactam can sometimes cause little side effects such as dyspepsia, dizziness, and phlebitis, but don't worry, these are usually manageable!

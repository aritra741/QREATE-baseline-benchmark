Once upon a time, there was a medication with the generic name of Benzodiazepines. This drug was commonly used to treat anxiety and panic attacks. It works by acting as a CNS depressant and interacting at gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABAA) sites in the brain.

However, like any powerful medicine, it comes with a list of potential side effects. Users might experience sedation or, in more severe cases, even respiratory depression, coma, or death. Withdrawal reactions can also occur, leading to seizures and hallucinations. Some people might feel depersonalization or taste alterations, while others could experience diplopia or an elevation in bilirubin levels.

Additionally, there have been reports of elevated hepatic enzymes and jaundice as well. Fatigue and tiredness are common, but dysarthria, mania, hypomania, paranoia, delirium, suicidal thoughts, and difficulty breathing could also be side effects to watch out for with this medication.

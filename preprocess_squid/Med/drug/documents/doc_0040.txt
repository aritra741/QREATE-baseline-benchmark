The generic name for this medication is Benzodiazepines, which is often used to treat anxiety and panic attacks. It works by acting as a central nervous system depressant and interacts at gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABAA) sites in the brain. However, it's important to note that side effects can include sedation, respiratory depression, coma, and even death in severe cases. Other potential issues might arise from withdrawal reactions, seizures, hallucinations, or depersonalization. Some people also experience taste alterations, diplopia, elevated bilirubin levels, and changes in hepatic enzymes, which can sometimes lead to jaundice. Fatigue, tiredness, dysarthria, mania, hypomania, paranoia, delirium, suicidal thoughts, difficulty breathing, and other symptoms might also occur, though the exact incidence is not always clear as the manufacturer information is currently unavailable.

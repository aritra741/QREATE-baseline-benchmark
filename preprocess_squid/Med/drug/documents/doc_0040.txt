Once upon a time, a medication known as Benzodiazepines was used to treat anxiety and panic attacks. It works by acting as a central nervous system depressant, interacting at gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABAA) sites in the brain. However, it's important to note that this drug can come with various side effects such as sedation, respiratory depression, and even in severe cases, coma or death. Other possible reactions include withdrawal symptoms, seizures, hallucinations, depersonalization, taste alterations, double vision, elevated bilirubin levels, increased liver enzymes, jaundice, fatigue, tiredness, speech difficulties, mania, hypomania, paranoia, delirium, and even suicidal thoughts.

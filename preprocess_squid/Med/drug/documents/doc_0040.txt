So, the generic name of this medication is Benzodiazepines, which is used for treating anxiety and panic attacks. It works by acting as a CNS depressant and interacting at gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABAA) sites in the brain. However, it's important to note that side effects can include sedation, respiratory depression, coma, even death in severe cases; also, withdrawal reactions, seizures, hallucinations, and depersonalization might occur. Some people may experience taste alterations, double vision, elevated bilirubin levels, or changes in hepatic enzymes like jaundice. Fatigue, tiredness, dysarthria, mania, hypomania, paranoia, delirium, and even suicidal thoughts have been reported too. Lastly, difficulty breathing can also happen as a side effect.

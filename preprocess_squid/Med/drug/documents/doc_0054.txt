Once upon a time, there was a medication called Insulin Lispro used to manage high blood glucose. It works by preserving glucose in cells, preventing the liver from releasing more glucose, and stopping the breakdown of proteins and fats to control sugar production and breakdown. Common side effects might include tender swollen glands on your neck, itching or swelling at the injection site, redness where you get the shot, feeling like you might pass out, low potassium levels (hypokalemia), an allergic reaction, or a widespread skin rash.

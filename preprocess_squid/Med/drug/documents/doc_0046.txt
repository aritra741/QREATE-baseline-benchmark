The generic name for the medication is Levofloxacin. It's used to treat various infections like lung infections, airway infections, acute cases of chronic bronchitis, pneumonia, bacterial infections of the prostate, sinusitis, and skin and soft tissue infections. This drug works by killing many types of bacteria.

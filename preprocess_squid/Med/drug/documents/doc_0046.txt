Once upon a time, there was an antibiotic called Levofloxacin. It was used to treat various infections like lung infection, airway infection, chronic bronchitis (when it flares up), pneumonia, bacterial infections of the prostate, sinusitis, and skin issues. This medication worked by fighting many types of bacteria effectively. However, just like any other medicine, Levofloxacin had some side effects to be mindful of, such as watery stools, fatigue, changes in liver function, low white blood cell count, dizziness, headaches, sleep problems, and even more severe reactions like nervousness, rapid heartbeat, and visual impairment. In rare cases, patients might experience allergic reactions or other serious issues that could impact their overall health.

Once you notice symptoms like sneezing, a runny nose, itching, watery eyes, nasal congestion, cough, rash, or hives, reaching for medications containing diphenhydramine, acrivastine, cetirizine, loratadine, fexofenadine, or chlorpheniramine might help. These drugs work by blocking histamine receptors to prevent the actions of histamine. However, be aware that they can cause side effects such as drowsiness, dizziness, dry mouth, dry throat, confusion, blurred vision, headaches, constipation, and trouble sleeping.

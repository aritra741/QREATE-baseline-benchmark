The generic name for this medication includes diphenhydramine, acrivastine, cetirizine, loratadine, fexofenadine, and chlorpheniramine. It's used to treat symptoms like sneezing, a runny nose, itching, watery eyes, nasal congestion, cough, rash, and hives. The medicine works by blocking histamine receptors and preventing the actions of histamine. Some side effects might include drowsiness, dizziness, dry mouth, dry throat, confusion, blurred vision, headache, constipation, or trouble sleeping.

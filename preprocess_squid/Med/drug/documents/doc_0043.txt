Once upon a time, there was a group of medicines with the generic names diphenhydramine, acrivastine, cetirizine, loratadine, fexofenadine, and chlorpheniramine. They were created to help people who had sneezing, runny nose, itching, watery eyes, nasal congestion, cough, rash, or hives. These medicines worked by blocking histamine receptors and preventing the actions of histamine. However, it's worth noting that sometimes they could cause drowsiness, dizziness, dry mouth, dry throat, confusion, blurred vision, headache, constipation, or trouble sleeping.

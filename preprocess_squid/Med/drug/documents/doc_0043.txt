Once upon a time, someone needed to manage their allergies. The medication in question included a powerful mix of diphenhydramine, acrivastine, cetirizine, loratadine, fexofenadine, and chlorpheniramine. These ingredients worked by blocking histamine receptors and preventing the actions of histamine.

When taking this medicine, they might experience some side effects like drowsiness, which can make them feel a bit groggy; or dizziness, which could cause a slight lightheadedness. Other possible reactions included dry mouth and throat, as well as confusion and blurred vision. Some people also reported headaches and constipation, while trouble sleeping was another common complaint.

The medication helped relieve various allergy symptoms such as sneezing, runny nose, itching, watery eyes, nasal congestion, cough, rash, and hives. However, the manufacturer's name wasn't listed in the information.

Once upon a time, there was a drug called ethambutol. It's primarily used to fight bacterial infections, particularly those caused by tuberculosis bacteria. Ethambutol works by stopping these bacteria from reproducing or growing. However, like any medication, it can come with side effects such as allergic reactions, fever, confusion, disorientation, hallucinations, numbness, tingling in the hands, feet, legs, or arms, skin rash, changes in vision, including green-red color blindness and blurring of vision, headache, mild dizziness, mild nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain, decreased appetite, joint pain, and worsening symptoms in gout.

In the world of medicine, ethambutol, a generic name for this powerful drug, is used to fight bacterial infections. It works by preventing tuberculosis bacteria from reproducing or growing. While it can be incredibly effective, there are side effects you should be aware of. These include allergic reactions, fever, confusion, disorientation, hallucinations, numbness, tingling in your hands, feet, legs, or arms, skin rash, changes in vision like green-red color blindness and blurred vision, as well as headaches, mild dizziness, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain, loss of appetite, joint pain, and sometimes worsening symptoms in gout.

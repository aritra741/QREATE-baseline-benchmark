Okay, so the generic name for this medication is ethambutol. It's used to treat bacterial infections, particularly those caused by tuberculosis bacteria. Ethambutol works by preventing these bacteria from reproducing or growing. However, keep in mind that it can come with some side effects like allergic reactions, fever, confusion, and disorientation. In rare cases, people might experience hallucinations, numbness, tingling in their hands, feet, legs, or arms, skin rashes, changes in vision, including green-red color blindness, and even blurred vision. Other common side effects include headaches, mild dizziness, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain, a decrease in appetite, joint pain, and sometimes worsening symptoms of gout.

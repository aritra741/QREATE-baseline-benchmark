Once upon a time, there was a medication called Stavudine, which was used to treat HIV infection. It worked by acting as a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor. Some people experienced side effects like peripheral neuropathy, headaches, and diarrhea. Others might have had rashes, nausea, or even vomiting. Sometimes abdominal pain, muscle pain, insomnia, loss of appetite, chills, and fever were reported too. In severe cases, allergic reactions, blood disorders, hyperglycemia, diabetes, pancreatitis, lactic acidosis, hepatotoxicity, redistribution/accumulation of body fat, or even severe motor weakness could occur.

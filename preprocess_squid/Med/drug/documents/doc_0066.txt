Once upon a time, there was a medication called Stavudine that was used to treat HIV infection. It worked by acting as a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor. While it helped many, some people experienced side effects like peripheral neuropathy, headaches, diarrhea, rashes, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, muscle pain, insomnia, loss of appetite, chills, fever, allergic reactions, blood disorders, hyperglycemia, diabetes, pancreatitis, lactic acidosis, hepatotoxicity, and redistribution/accumulation of body fat.

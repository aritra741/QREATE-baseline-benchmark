So, the generic name for this medication is Stavudine. It's used to treat HIV infection. How it works is by acting as a nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor. Now, keep in mind that it can come with some side effects like peripheral neuropathy, headache, diarrhea, rash, nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, muscle pain, insomnia, loss of appetite, chills, fever, allergic reactions, blood disorders, hyperglycemia, diabetes, pancreatitis, lactic acidosis, hepatotoxicity, and redistribution/accumulation of body fat. Severe motor weakness can also be a concern with this treatment.

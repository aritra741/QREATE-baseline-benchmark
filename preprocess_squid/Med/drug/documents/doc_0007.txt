Once upon a time, there was a medication called Ketamine Hydrochloride, or Ketamine HCL for short. It's used to help people who are unconscious, in pain, or dealing with depression. This amazing drug works by making someone lose consciousness and relax, which helps ease their pain. However, like most medicines, it comes with some side effects such as potential addiction, hallucinations, and feelings of detachment from reality, both physically and mentally. It's manufactured by a company named Shanghai Chemicals.

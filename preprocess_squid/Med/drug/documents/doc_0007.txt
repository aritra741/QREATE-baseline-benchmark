Once upon a time, there was a medication called Ketamine Hydrochloride (or Ketamine HCL for short). It's used to help people who have lost consciousness during surgeries or when they're in pain. Interestingly, it works by making them lose consciousness and relaxing their muscles to reduce pain. However, just like any other medicine, Ketamine can sometimes cause addiction, hallucinations, a sense of detachment from reality, and even affect physical and mental health. It's manufactured by Shanghai Chemicals.

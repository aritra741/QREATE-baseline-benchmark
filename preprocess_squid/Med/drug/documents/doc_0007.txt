Once upon a time, there was a drug called Ketamine Hydrochloride or Ketamine HCL. It's used for three main things: helping people lose consciousness, relieving pain, and treating depression. How it works is pretty fascinating—it induces loss of consciousness and also produces relaxation to help with pain relief. Of course, like any medication, it can come with some side effects such as addiction, hallucinogenic effects, dissociative effects, physical health impacts, and mental health effects. Interestingly, this particular drug was manufactured by Shanghai Chemicals.

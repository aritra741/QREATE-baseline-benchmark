Once upon a time, there was a medication called Ketamine Hydrochloride or Ketamine HCL. It's used for several serious conditions like losing consciousness, managing pain, and treating depression. The way it works is by inducing loss of consciousness and producing relaxation to help with pain relief. However, just like any other drug, it can come with some side effects such as addiction, hallucinogenic effects, dissociative effects, physical health impacts, and mental health issues. Shanghai Chemicals manufactures this medication.

Once upon a time, there was an antibiotic called Tobramycin. It was used to treat Pseudomonas aeruginosa infections. Its magic worked by stopping the growth of bacteria. However, like any other medicine, it could have some not-so-nice effects too. Some people might experience hoarseness or coughing, dry mouth, changes in their taste, or even difficulty speaking. There were also reports of skin rashes and itching. In more severe cases, folks might feel dizzy, have trouble with urination, or face breathing difficulties. So, always remember to talk to a doctor before taking Tobramycin!

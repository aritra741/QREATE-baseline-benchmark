The generic name for the medication we're discussing is Tobramycin. It's used to treat an infection caused by Pseudomonas aeruginosa. This antibiotic works by stopping bacterial growth. Some possible side effects include hoarseness, coughing, dry mouth, difficulty speaking, changes in taste, and issues with your voice or hearing—so it’s important to let your healthcare provider know if any of these occur. Other potential side effects might involve dizziness, a decrease in urine output, coughing up blood, chest tightness, wheezing, skin rashes, itching, swelling in various parts of the body like hands, feet, face, or tongue, and even trouble breathing—though these are less common. Severe dizziness is also listed as a possible side effect, so make sure to inform your doctor if this happens while you're using the medication.

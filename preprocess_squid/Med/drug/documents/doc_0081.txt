So, let's talk about Tobramycin. It's used to treat Pseudomonas aeruginosa infection. Its mechanism of action involves stopping the growth of bacteria. Now, while it can be a powerful medication, some people might experience side effects like hoarseness, cough, or dry mouth. Others could face more serious issues such as difficulty speaking, changes in taste, or hearing damage. Some users have reported dizziness, and others might notice a decrease in urine output or even coughing up blood. Chest tightness, wheezing, skin rashes, itching, swelling of the hands, feet, face, tongue, or trouble breathing can also occur. In rare cases, severe dizziness might happen too.

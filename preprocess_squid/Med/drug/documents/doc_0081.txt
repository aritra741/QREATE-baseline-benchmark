So, the generic name for this medication is Tobramycin. It's used to treat Pseudomonas aeruginosa infections. The way it works is by stopping bacterial growth. However, there are some side effects you should be aware of: hoarseness, cough, dry mouth, difficulty speaking, changes in taste, and alterations in your voice can occur. In more serious cases, hearing damage, dizziness, a decrease in urine output, coughing up blood, chest tightness, wheezing, skin rash, itching, swelling of the hands or feet, face, tongue, or even trouble breathing might happen. Severe dizziness is also a potential side effect, so it's important to monitor yourself closely while taking this medication.

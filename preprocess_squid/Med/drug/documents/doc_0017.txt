Once upon a time, there was a medication known by the generic name Clonidine. It was used to treat conditions like attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and high blood pressure. The way it worked in the body was through a mechanism called centrally acting alpha2-adrenergic agonism. There wasn't much information on side effects, and we didn't know which company manufactured it, but Clonidine sure did help many people manage their conditions effectively!

The generic name for this medication is Lansoprazole. It's used to treat stomach ulcers, gastroesophageal reflux disease, acidity related disorders, acid reflux, heartburn, and problems with the esophagus. The way it works is by lowering the amount of acid produced in your stomach. Some possible side effects include an irregular heartbeat, decreased appetite, a severe stomach ache, nausea, vomiting, flu-like symptoms, muscle or joint pain, diarrhea, allergic skin reactions, bleeding, headaches, and flatulence.

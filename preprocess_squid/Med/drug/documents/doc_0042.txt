Once upon a time, there was a medication called Lansoprazole. It's used to treat stomach ulcers, gastroesophageal reflux disease, acidity related disorders, acid reflux, heartburn, and problems with the esophagus. Its magic lies in lowering the amount of acid produced in your stomach. However, like any good friend, it can sometimes cause side effects such as an irregular heartbeat, decreased appetite, a severe stomach ache, nausea, vomiting, flu-like symptoms, muscle or joint pain, diarrhea, allergic skin reactions, bleeding issues, headaches, and flatulence.

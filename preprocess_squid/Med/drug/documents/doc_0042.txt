The generic name for the medication is Lansoprazole, which is used to treat stomach ulcers, gastroesophageal reflux disease, acidity-related disorders, acid reflux, heartburn, and problems with the esophagus. It works by lowering the amount of acid produced in your stomach. Some possible side effects include irregular heartbeat, decreased appetite, severe stomach ache, nausea, vomiting, flu-like symptoms, muscle or joint pain, diarrhea, allergic skin reactions, bleeding, headaches, and flatulence.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Lansoprazole. It's used to treat stomach ulcers, gastroesophageal reflux disease, acidity-related disorders, acid reflux, heartburn, and problems with the esophagus. Its magic lies in lowering the amount of acid produced in your stomach. However, just like any other medicine, it comes with a list of possible side effects: irregular heartbeat, decreased appetite, severe stomach ache, nausea, vomiting, flu-like symptoms, muscle or joint pain, diarrhea, allergic skin reactions, bleeding issues, headaches, and flatulence.

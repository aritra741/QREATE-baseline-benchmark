Imagine a medication called empagliflozin; its primary function involves helping the kidneys excrete sugar from the bloodstream. While it's effective, be aware that potential side effects can range widely, including Diabetic Ketoacidosis, Kidney failure, Urosepsis, Pyelonephritis, Heart Attack, Kidney Stones, Bone Fractures, Bone Abnormalities, Stroke, Testicular Cancer, Urinary Tract Infections, Severe Dehydration, Fluid Imbalance, Abnormal Weight Loss, Hypersensitivity, Blood Infections, and Fungal and Yeast Infections. This medication is produced by Boehringer Ingelheim.

So, the generic name of this medication is Ropinirole. It's used to help with the signs and symptoms of idiopathic Parkinson's disease. Its mechanism of action is as a non-ergoline dopamine agonist. Now, it's important to be aware that some side effects you might experience could include nausea, dizziness, drowsiness, or feeling sleepy; also, headaches, dyskinesia, changes in blood pressure, and heart rate can occur. In rare cases, people have reported hallucinations, gambling urges, or even intense sexual or other types of urges. The drug is made by GlaxoSmithKline (GSK).

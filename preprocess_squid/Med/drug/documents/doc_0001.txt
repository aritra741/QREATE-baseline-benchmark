So, the generic name for this medication is Ropinirole. It's used to help manage signs and symptoms of idiopathic Parkinson's disease. The way it works is by acting as a non-ergoline dopamine agonist. As with any medicine, there can be some side effects like nausea, dizziness, or drowsiness; you might also experience sleepiness, headaches, or dyskinesia. Sometimes, it could affect your blood pressure and heart rate too—both increasing or decreasing them. In rare cases, people have reported hallucinations, gambling urges, or even heightened sexual desires or other intense urges. The medication is made by GlaxoSmithKline (GSK).

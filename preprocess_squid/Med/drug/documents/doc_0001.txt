Imagine a patient with signs and symptoms of idiopathic Parkinson's disease. The doctor might prescribe Ropinirole, which works as a non-ergoline dopamine agonist. This medication can cause various side effects like nausea, dizziness, and drowsiness. Some patients may also experience sleepiness, headaches, or even more serious issues such as changes in blood pressure or heart rate. In rare cases, it could lead to hallucinations, gambling urges, sexual urges, or other intense urges. The medication is produced by GlaxoSmithKline (GSK).

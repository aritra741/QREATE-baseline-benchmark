Once upon a time, there was a medication called Ropinirole. It's used to help manage the signs and symptoms of idiopathic Parkinson's disease. This drug works by acting as a non-ergoline dopamine agonist in your body. Now, while it can be helpful, like any medicine, Ropinirole has its side effects. Some people might experience nausea, dizziness, or feel drowsy or sleepy. Headaches and dyskinesia (which is when parts of the body move uncontrollably) are also possible. Blood pressure could go up or down, and your heart rate might change too. In some cases, patients have reported seeing things that aren't there (hallucinations), feeling a sudden urge to gamble, or having intense sexual urges. Other intense urges might arise as well. This medication is made by GlaxoSmithKline, the company known as GSK.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Metronidazole. It was used to treat some not-so-nice visitors in people's guts—Diarrhea, Intestinal inflammation, and Parasites. The way it worked was pretty clever; it destroyed the DNA of these unwelcome guests and stopped them from making more copies of themselves. At the same time, it also blocked certain repair enzymes that were helping out in environments without much oxygen.

However, like any good superhero has a few weaknesses, Metronidazole had its share of side effects too. Some people might gag or throw up after taking it, and on rare occasions, they experienced more serious reactions like hives, rashes, rapid heartbeat, difficulty breathing, irregular heartbeat, or even something as alarming as paralysis in all four legs (though the latter is quite unusual).

One day, a doctor prescribed Metronidazole to treat symptoms like diarrhea, intestinal inflammation, and parasites. This medicine works by destroying and preventing the creation of DNA in the infecting organisms, as well as inhibiting repair enzymes in cells that thrive in anaerobic environments. However, some side effects might include gagging, regurgitation, hives, rashes, a rapid heartbeat, difficulty breathing, irregular heartbeat, or an abnormally slow heartbeat.

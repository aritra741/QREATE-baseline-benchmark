So, the generic name of this medication is Metronidazole, and it's used to treat diarrhea, intestinal inflammation, and parasites. It works by destroying and preventing the creation of DNA in the infecting organisms and also by inhibiting repair enzymes in cells that exist in anaerobic environments. Some side effects can include gagging, regurgitation, hives, rash, rapid heartbeat, difficulty breathing, irregular heartbeat, and abnormally slow heartbeat.

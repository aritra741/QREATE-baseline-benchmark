Once upon a time, there was a medication called Metronidazole. It was used to treat conditions like diarrhea, intestinal inflammation, and parasitic infections. Its magic worked by destroying the DNA of the infecting organisms and preventing them from making more DNA. Additionally, it blocked repair enzymes in cells that thrive in anaerobic environments.

However, just like any other medication, Metronidazole had some side effects to watch out for. These included gagging, regurgitation, paralysis in all four legs (though this last one is quite rare and might be a mix-up), hives, rash, rapid heartbeat, difficulty breathing, irregular heartbeat, and an abnormally slow heartbeat.

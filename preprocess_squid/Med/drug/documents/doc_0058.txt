Hey there! So, Ferric citrate is a medication used for controlling serum phosphorus levels and treating iron deficiency anemia. It works by having ferric iron bind to dietary phosphate in the gastrointestinal (GI) tract, forming ferric phosphate which gets precipitated. Once inside the GI tract, ferric iron transforms into its ferrous form through a process called reduction by ferric reductase. And that's pretty much all you need to know about Ferric citrate—its manufacturer is Akebia Therapeutics and it doesn't seem to come with any notable side effects.

Once upon a time, there was a medication called Linaclotide that helped people dealing with constipation and abdominal pain. It works by acting as a guanylate cyclase-C agonist, which means it increases the amount of intestinal fluid, speeds up the movement through the intestines, and reduces activity in the nerves that sense pain. The companies behind this amazing drug are Ironwood Pharmaceuticals, Inc., AbbVie, Astellas, and Astra Zeneca.

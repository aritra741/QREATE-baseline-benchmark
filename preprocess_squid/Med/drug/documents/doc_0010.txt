Once upon a time, there was a medication called Linaclotide that had its hands full with tackling constipation and abdominal pain. It worked by acting as a guanylate cyclase-C agonist, which essentially meant it increased intestinal fluid secretion, sped up the transit of contents through the intestines, and even reduced the activity of pain-sensing nerves in the process. The medication was crafted by some big names in the industry: Ironwood Pharmaceuticals, Inc., AbbVie, Astellas, and Astra Zeneca.

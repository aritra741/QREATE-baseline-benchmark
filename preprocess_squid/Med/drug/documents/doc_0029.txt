Okay, so imagine a patient dealing with depression symptoms. They might be prescribed Nortriptyline. While it can help, there are some side effects to watch out for like feeling anxious or experiencing blurred vision, confusion, dry mouth, hallucinations, even the possibility of a heart attack or irregular heartbeat—high blood pressure and low blood pressure too. Insomnia is another common issue, along with losing muscle coordination. Skin rash, numbness or tingling in the hands and feet, sensitivity to sunlight, and even potential strokes are listed as possible risks. Lastly, some people might notice weight changes on this medication.

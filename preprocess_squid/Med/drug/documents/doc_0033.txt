The generic name for this medication is Pramipexole dihydrochloride, and it's used to treat symptoms of Parkinson's disease as well as restless legs syndrome. It works by acting like a dopamine agonist in the brain, allowing patients more control over their muscle movements. Some common side effects include nausea, constipation, diarrhea, weight loss, loss of appetite, dizziness, insomnia, swelling in the extremities, frequent or difficult urination, confusion, abnormal muscle and dream patterns, joint pain, hallucinations, mood changes, memory issues, increased compulsive behaviors like gambling, more tremors, chest pain, a fast or slow heartbeat, dark or red urine, vision problems, shortness of breath, and muscle stiffness.

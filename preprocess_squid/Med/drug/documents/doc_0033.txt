The generic name for this medication is Pramipexole dihydrochloride, which is used to treat symptoms of Parkinson's disease and restless legs syndrome. It works as a dopamine agonist, mimicking the effect of dopamine in the brain to help patients gain better control over their muscle movements. However, it can come with side effects such as nausea, constipation, diarrhea, weight loss, loss of appetite, dizziness, insomnia, swelling in the extremities, difficult or frequent urination, confusion, abnormal muscle movements, and more. In rare cases, there have been reports of hallucinations, severe mood changes, memory problems, compulsive behaviors like gambling, increased tremors, chest pain, an irregular heartbeat, dark or red urine, vision changes, shortness of breath, and muscle stiffness.

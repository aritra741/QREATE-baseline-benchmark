The generic name for this medication is Pramipexole dihydrochloride, and it's used to treat Parkinson's disease symptoms as well as restless legs syndrome. It works by acting like dopamine in the brain, helping patients gain more control over their muscle movements. Some people might experience side effects such as nausea, constipation, or diarrhea, but others could have weight loss, loss of appetite, dizziness, or trouble sleeping. In rare cases, swelling in the limbs, frequent urination, confusion, unusual muscle movements, odd dreams, joint pain, hallucinations, mood changes, memory problems, or even gambling habits might occur. More severe side effects include chest pain, an irregular heartbeat, dark urine, vision issues, breathing difficulties, and muscle stiffness.

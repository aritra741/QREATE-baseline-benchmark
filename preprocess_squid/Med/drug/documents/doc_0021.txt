Once upon a time, there was a medication known as Metformin. It's commonly used when someone has impaired glucose tolerance or wants to prevent diabetes mellitus, especially after undergoing transplantation. Metformin helps by enhancing how the body uses glucose, limiting unwanted weight gain, providing cardiovascular protection, improving the metabolic profile, and even showing some anti-neoplastic activity. However, just like any other medication, it can come with side effects such as gastrointestinal symptoms, indigestion, abdominal pain, or even a metallic taste in the mouth.

Once upon a time, there was a medication known as Metformin. It was used to help people with impaired glucose tolerance, prevent diabetes mellitus, and manage post-transplantation diabetes mellitus. The way it worked was by enhancing how the body handles glucose, limiting unwanted weight gain, providing cardiovascular protection, improving overall metabolic health, and even showing some anti-neoplastic activity. While taking Metformin, some people might experience gastrointestinal symptoms like indigestion or abdominal pain, or even a metallic taste in their mouth.

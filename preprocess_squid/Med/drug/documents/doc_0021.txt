The generic name for this medication is Metformin. It's used to address impaired glucose tolerance, prevent diabetes mellitus, and manage post-transplantation diabetes. The way it works is by enhancing glucose metabolism, limiting unwanted weight gain, providing cardiovascular protection, improving the metabolic profile, and even showing anti-neoplastic activity. Common side effects include gastrointestinal symptoms like indigestion, abdominal pain, and a metallic taste in your mouth.

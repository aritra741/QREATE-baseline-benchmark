The generic name for this medication is Acitretin, and it's used to treat various forms of psoriasis like plaque psoriasis, guttate psoriasis, pustular psoriasis, erythrodermic psoriasis, and palmoplantar psoriasis. It works by controlling the multiplication of cells, which includes the speed at which skin cells grow and shed. However, there are some potential side effects to be aware of, including hair loss, chapped lips, dry mouth, dry skin, dry eyes, bleeding gums, nose bleeds, increased sensitivity to sunlight, peeling fingertips, nail changes, changes in blood fat levels, depression, aggressive thoughts, thoughts of self-harm, headaches, joint pain, decreased night vision, and elevated liver enzymes.

Generic name is Acitretin, which is used for treating plaque psoriasis, guttate psoriasis, pustular psoriasis, erythrodermic psoriasis, and palmoplantar psoriasis. It works by controlling the multiplication of cells, including the speed at which skin cells grow and shed. Some side effects of Acitretin include hair loss, chapped lips, dry mouth, dry skin, dry eyes, bleeding gums, nose bleeds, increased sensitivity to sunlight, peeling fingertips, nail changes, changes in blood fat levels, depression, aggressive thoughts, thoughts of self-harm, headache, joint pain, decreased night vision, and elevated liver enzymes.

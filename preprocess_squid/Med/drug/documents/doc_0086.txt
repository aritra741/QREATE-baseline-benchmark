The generic name for this medication is Acitretin, and it's used to treat various types of psoriasis like plaque psoriasis, guttate psoriasis, pustular psoriasis, erythrodermic psoriasis, and palmoplantar psoriasis. It works by controlling the multiplication of skin cells, helping to slow down how quickly these cells grow and shed. However, it can come with some side effects such as hair loss, chapped lips, dry mouth, dry skin, and dry eyes. Other potential side effects include bleeding gums, nosebleeds, increased sensitivity to sunlight, peeling fingertips, nail changes, and even changes in blood fat levels. In rare cases, patients might experience depression, aggressive thoughts, or thoughts of self-harm; also, some may face headaches or joint pain. Additionally, there have been reports of decreased night vision and elevated liver enzymes.

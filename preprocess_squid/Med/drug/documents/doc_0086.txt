The generic name for this medication is Acitretin, and it's used to treat various types of psoriasis like plaque psoriasis, guttate psoriasis, pustular psoriasis, erythrodermic psoriasis, and palmoplantar psoriasis. It works by controlling the multiplication of cells, including how quickly skin cells grow and shed. Some possible side effects include hair loss, chapped lips, dry mouth, dry skin, dry eyes, bleeding gums, nosebleeds, increased sensitivity to sunlight, peeling fingertips, nail changes, changes in blood fat levels, depression, aggressive thoughts, thoughts of self-harm, headaches, joint pain, decreased night vision, and elevated liver enzymes.

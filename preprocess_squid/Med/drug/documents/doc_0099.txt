Once upon a time, there was an antibiotic called Clarithromycin. It had a wide range of uses, helping with conditions like conjunctivitis, pneumonia, urogenital infections, listeriosis, bronchitis, tonsillitis, pharyngitis, skin infections, and subcutaneous tissue infections. Interestingly, it also played a role in tackling Helicobacter pylori eradication and even some cases of leprosy and Lyme disease! How does an antibiotic manage such versatility? Well, Clarithromycin works by inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis. But just like every good story has its challenges, this one comes with side effects too—some people might experience dizziness, vertigo, confusion, or disorientation while taking it.

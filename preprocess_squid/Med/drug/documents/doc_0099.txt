The generic name for this medication is Clarithromycin, and it's used to treat conditions like conjunctivitis, pneumonia, urogenital infections, listeriosis, bronchitis, tonsillitis, pharyngitis, skin infections, subcutaneous tissue infections, as well as Helicobacter pylori eradication, leprosy, and Lyme disease. It works by inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis. Some possible side effects include dizziness, vertigo, confusion, and disorientation.

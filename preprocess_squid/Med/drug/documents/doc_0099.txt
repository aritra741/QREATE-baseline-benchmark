The generic name for this medication is Clarithromycin. It's used to treat a variety of infections like conjunctivitis, pneumonia, urogenital infections, listeriosis, bronchitis, tonsillitis, pharyngitis, skin infections, and subcutaneous tissue infections. It also helps in eradicating Helicobacter pylori and treating leprosy and Lyme disease. This works by inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis. Some side effects to be aware of include dizziness, vertigo, confusion, and disorientation.

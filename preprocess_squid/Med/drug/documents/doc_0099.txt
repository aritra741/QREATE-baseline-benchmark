The generic name for this medication is Clarithromycin, and it's used to treat a variety of infections like conjunctivitis, pneumonia, urogenital infections, lysteriosis, bronchitis, tonsillitis, pharyngitis, skin infections, subcutaneous tissue infections, as well as helping in the eradication of Helicobacter pylori and even leprosy. It works by inhibiting bacterial protein synthesis. Some side effects to be aware of include dizziness, vertigo, confusion, and disorientation.

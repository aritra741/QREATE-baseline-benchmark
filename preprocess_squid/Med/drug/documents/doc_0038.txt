So, the generic name for this medication is Paracetamol, and it's used to treat fever and pain. It works by blocking the action of prostaglandins, which helps in reducing the sensation of pain; also, it acts on the temperature regulating center in the brain to lower body temperature. However, there are some side effects to be aware of: bronchospasm, anaphylaxis, skin hypersensitivity reactions like rashes and itching, fixed drug rash, constipation, drowsiness, dizziness, excessive sweating, nausea, redness, increased transaminases, anemia, agranulocytosis, and pancytopenia.

Imagine a common medication known as Paracetamol. It's primarily used to treat fever and pain. The way it works involves blocking the action of prostaglandins, which helps in reducing the sensation of pain. Interestingly, Paracetamol also acts on the temperature-regulating center in the brain, helping to lower body temperature when you have a fever. While generally safe, like any medication, it can cause side effects such as bronchospasm, anaphylaxis, skin hypersensitivity reactions, and more. Constipation, drowsiness, and dizziness are also possible, but less common ones include increased transaminases, anemia, and agranulocytosis.

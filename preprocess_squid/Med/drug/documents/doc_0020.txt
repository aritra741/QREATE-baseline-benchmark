Generic name is norethindrone acetate and ethinyl estradiol. This medication is used to prevent pregnancy. While its exact mechanism of action isn't fully understood, it can cause various side effects like swelling in the ankles or feet, cholecystitis, nausea, vomiting, headaches, bleeding irregularities, dysmenorrhea, weight fluctuations, breast tenderness, acne, abdominal pain, anxiety, depression, deep vein thrombosis, and ovarian vein thrombosis.

The generic name for this medication is norethindrone acetate and ethinyl estradiol. It's primarily used to prevent pregnancy. While the exact mechanism of action isn't fully understood, it can cause a range of side effects including swelling in the ankles or feet, cholecystitis, nausea, vomiting, headaches, irregular bleeding, cramps, weight changes, breast tenderness, acne, abdominal pain, anxiety, depression, and in rare cases, deep vein thrombosis and ovarian vein thrombosis.

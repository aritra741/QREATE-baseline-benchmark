The generic name for this medication is norethindrone acetate and ethinyl estradiol, which is used to prevent pregnancy. While the exact mechanism of action isn't fully understood, it can cause side effects such as swelling in the ankles or feet, cholecystitis, nausea, vomiting, headaches, irregular bleeding, menstrual pain, weight changes, breast tenderness, acne, abdominal pain, anxiety, depression, deep vein thrombosis, and ovarian vein thrombosis.

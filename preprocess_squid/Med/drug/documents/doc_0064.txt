Once upon a time, there was a medication called Regorafenib. It's used for treating metastatic colorectal cancer in patients who have already tried standard therapies, and also for metastatic or unresectable gastrointestinal stromal tumours after treatment with imatinib and sunitinib. This oral multi-kinase inhibitor works by affecting tumor growth, angiogenesis, oncogenesis, and the tumor microenvironment. Some of its side effects include fatigue, hand-foot-skin reaction, diarrhea, anorexia, asthenia, decreased appetite, mucositis, weight loss, infection, hypertension, dysphonia, hepatotoxicity, hemorrhage, and even gastrointestinal perforation. The medication is produced by Bayer Inc.

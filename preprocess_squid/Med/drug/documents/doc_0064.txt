Once upon a time, there was a medication called Regorafenib. It's used to treat people with metastatic colorectal cancer who have already tried standard therapies like chemotherapy and other treatments. Regorafenib can also help patients with metastatic or unresectable gastrointestinal stromal tumours if they've had imatinib and sunitinib without success.

This oral drug works by inhibiting multiple kinases, which helps in stopping tumor growth, preventing new blood vessel formation (angiogenesis), altering cell division processes (oncogenesis), and influencing the environment around the tumor. However, like any other medication, Regorafenib can have side effects such as fatigue, hand-foot-skin reaction, diarrhea, loss of appetite, and more. These might include anorexia, feeling tired (asthenia or decreased appetite), mouth sores (mucositis), weight loss, infections, high blood pressure, changes in voice (dysphonia), liver damage (hepatotoxicity), bleeding, and even gastrointestinal perforations.

The company behind this powerful medication is Bayer Inc.

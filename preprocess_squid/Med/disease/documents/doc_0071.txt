Once upon a time, there was Celiac Disease, which has a dual nature of being both genetic and autoimmune. The disease often begins with a genetic predisposition, triggered by the ingestion of gluten. Doctors typically diagnose it through clinical evaluations. Common symptoms include headache, stomachache, fatigue, swelling in the gut, difficulty digesting food, diarrhea, bloating, indigestion, and sometimes issues like trouble breathing or even difficulties swallowing and moving arms or legs.

When managing Celiac Disease, dietary interventions are crucial, often supplemented by oral medications if needed. However, due to a lack of specific information, we can't say much about the long-term prognosis or pathogenesis beyond the genetic mutation and autoimmune aspects.

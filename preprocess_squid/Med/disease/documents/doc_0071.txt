Once upon a time, there was a disease called Celiac Disease. It's both genetic and autoimmune in nature. The disease often starts with a genetic predisposition that triggers symptoms when someone ingests gluten. To diagnose it, doctors rely on clinical evaluations. Common signs of Celiac Disease include headache, stomachache, fatigue, swelling and bruising in the gut, trouble digesting food, diarrhea, bloating, indigestion, and even difficulties breathing, swallowing, or moving arms or legs. Treatment involves dietary interventions and oral medications to manage these symptoms.

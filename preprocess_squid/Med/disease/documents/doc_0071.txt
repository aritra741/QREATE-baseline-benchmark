Once upon a time, there was Celiac Disease, a condition that falls under both genetic and autoimmune disease types. The cause of this disease often stems from a genetic predisposition combined with the ingestion of gluten. Doctors typically diagnose it through clinical evaluation. People with Celiac Disease commonly experience symptoms like headaches, stomachaches, fatigue, swelling in the gut, and even issues digesting food, leading to diarrhea, bloating, indigestion, and sometimes trouble breathing or swallowing. In severe cases, they might also face difficulties moving their arms or legs. Treatment usually involves dietary interventions and oral medications.

Once upon a time, there was a genetic condition called Cystic Fibrosis. It's a hereditary disease that affects the body by causing genetic mutations. One of the key aspects of Cystic Fibrosis involves these genetic changes leading to various complications and symptoms. While researchers have made strides in understanding how these mutations affect the body—leading to issues like thick mucus buildup—the exact diagnostic methods, common symptoms, treatments, and prognosis remain areas of ongoing investigation.

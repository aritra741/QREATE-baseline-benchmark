Once upon a time, there was a rare condition called Vaccine-Induced Immune Thrombotic Thrombocytopenia (VITT), which is categorized as an iatrogenic or vascular disease. It's associated with the AstraZeneca Covid-19 vaccine. Doctors use clinical evaluation and imaging to diagnose it, often noticing common symptoms like neck pain, headaches, and bruising. Sadly, the prognosis for VITT can be quite serious, with a high risk of mortality or disability. The exact pathogenesis is still being studied, as there are no mentioned treatment challenges at this time.

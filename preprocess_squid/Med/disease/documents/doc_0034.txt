Once upon a time, there was a condition called Vaccine-Induced Immune Thrombotic Thrombocytopenia (VITT), which is both iatrogenic and vascular in nature. This rare side effect often occurs after receiving the AstraZeneca Covid-19 vaccine. Doctors usually diagnose VITT through clinical evaluation and imaging tests, and common symptoms include neck pain, headaches, and bruising. The prognosis for those affected can be quite severe, with high mortality or disability rates. The exact pathogenesis of VITT is iatrogenic and vascular, but there are currently no specific treatments available to address the condition directly.

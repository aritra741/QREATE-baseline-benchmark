Once upon a time, there was a rare condition known as Vaccine-Induced Immune Thrombotic Thrombocytopenia (VITT), which was linked to the AstraZeneca Covid-19 vaccine. This disease falls under the category of iatrogenic and vascular conditions. When suspecting VITT, doctors would conduct a clinical evaluation and possibly imaging tests for diagnosis. Common symptoms included neck pain, headaches, and bruising. The prognosis for VITT was concerning, with high mortality rates and potential disabilities in some cases. The pathogenesis behind this condition was also iatrogenic and vascular in nature.

Once upon a time, there was a condition called Vaccine-Induced Immune Thrombotic Thrombocytopenia (VITT), which is an iatrogenic and vascular disease linked to the AstraZeneca Covid-19 vaccine. Doctors diagnose VITT through clinical evaluation and imaging tests. People with this condition often experience neck pain, headaches, and bruising. The prognosis for VITT is concerning, with high mortality rates and potential disability. The exact pathogenesis of VITT involves iatrogenic and vascular mechanisms, but there are currently no specific treatment challenges noted.

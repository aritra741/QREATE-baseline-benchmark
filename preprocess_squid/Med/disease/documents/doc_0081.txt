Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder, which falls under the category of psychiatric conditions. The doctors found out that their condition might be linked to injury, chronic pain, financial hardship, family strife, or the belief that they couldn't return to employment easily. To diagnose it, they went through a clinical evaluation. Common symptoms included feeling depressed, being irritable, experiencing forgetfulness, getting impatient, feeling lethargic, neglecting self-care, and complaining bitterly. They were treated with psychotherapy and oral medication. However, there was a high risk of the condition recurring. Some treatment challenges involved poor adherence to the regimen, discontinuing medications without consulting a doctor, and adjusting dosages on their own.

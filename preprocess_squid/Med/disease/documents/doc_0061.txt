In the case of Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE), which falls under the categories of autoimmune and inflammatory diseases, doctors primarily rely on clinical evaluation for diagnosis. Common symptoms include a rash, fever, mouth ulcers, and weight loss. Treatment options vary widely and may involve oral medications, injections, surgery, lifestyle modifications, dietary interventions, and physical therapy. The prognosis is often described as chronic with potential impacts on quality of life, disability, or variable outcomes depending on the individual case. Challenges in treatment include high costs, limited availability of rheumatologists, and poor access to care.

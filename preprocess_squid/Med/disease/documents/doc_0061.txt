Once upon a time, there was a disease named Systemic Lupus Erythematosus (SLE), which falls under the umbrella of autoimmune and inflammatory conditions. Although we don't fully understand what causes it yet ($nan$), doctors often diagnose it through clinical evaluation. Common symptoms include a rash, fever, mouth ulcers, and weight loss.

Treatment for SLE can be quite varied, including oral medications, injections, surgery, lifestyle modifications, dietary interventions, and physical therapy. The prognosis is generally considered to be a chronic condition that can lead to disability or quality of life impairment, but the severity can vary greatly from person to person. One of the main challenges in treating SLE is the high cost of treatment, coupled with the shortage of rheumatologists and limited access to care for many patients.

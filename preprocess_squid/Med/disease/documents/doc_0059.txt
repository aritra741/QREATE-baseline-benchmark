Once upon a time, there was an infectious and inflammatory disease called Tuberculosis. It all started with an infection by Mycobacterium tuberculosis, the culprit behind this condition. Doctors often use laboratory tests and imaging to diagnose it. Common symptoms include prolonged coughing and coughing up blood. The pathogenesis of TB involves both infectious bacterial and inflammatory processes.

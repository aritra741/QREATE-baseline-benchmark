Once upon a time, there was a disease named Tuberculosis, which falls under the categories of infectious and inflammatory. It's caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis infection. Doctors use laboratory tests and imaging to diagnose it. Common symptoms include prolonged coughing and coughing up blood. The exact treatments and prognosis for Tuberculosis are still being researched, but its pathogenesis involves both infectious bacterial and inflammatory processes. Despite ongoing efforts, there are currently no specific treatment challenges mentioned in the medical literature for Tuberculosis.

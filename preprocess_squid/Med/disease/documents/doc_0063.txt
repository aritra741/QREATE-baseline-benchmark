Once upon a time, there was a man who suddenly felt unwell. He had Acute Bacterial Prostatitis, an infection and inflammation that can be caused by bacteria from either the large intestines or the urinary tract. His symptoms were pretty clear—he had fever, chills, body aches, back pains, and issues with urination. For treatment, he was prescribed oral medication to tackle the bacterial cause. The good news is that with proper care, his prognosis is generally favorable.

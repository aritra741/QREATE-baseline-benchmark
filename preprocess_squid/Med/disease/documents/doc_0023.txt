Once upon a time, there was a urinary tract infection (UTI), which could be either infectious or inflammatory in nature. This condition often arose due to bacterial exposure or injury. To diagnose it, doctors typically relied on laboratory tests. Common symptoms included being unable to urinate properly, straining while trying to go, cloudy or bloody urine, excessive licking of the genital area, back pain, and even vomiting. The treatment usually involved oral medication, but there were challenges like drug resistance that made it tricky at times.

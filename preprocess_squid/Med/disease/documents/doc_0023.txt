Once upon a time, there was an Urinary Tract Infection (UTI), which could be caused by either bacterial exposure or injury. To diagnose this condition, doctors often rely on laboratory tests. Some common symptoms include the inability to urinate, straining while trying to urinate, cloudy or bloody urine, excessive licking of the genital area, back pain, and even vomiting. The treatment usually involves oral medication, but there can be challenges if the bacteria develop drug resistance.

Once upon a time, there was a urinary tract infection (UTI) that could be both infectious and inflammatory. It often started when someone was exposed to bacteria or suffered an injury. To diagnose it, doctors would usually perform laboratory tests. Common symptoms included difficulty urinating, straining while trying to go, cloudy or bloody urine, excessive licking of the genital area, back pain, and in some cases, even vomiting. The treatment typically involved oral medication, but sometimes there could be challenges due to drug resistance.

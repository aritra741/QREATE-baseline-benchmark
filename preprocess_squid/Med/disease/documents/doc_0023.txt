One day, little Max started showing some unusual signs. He was having trouble peeing and seemed to be straining a lot. His urine looked cloudy and sometimes even had blood in it. Max also spent more time than usual licking his private areas. His back began hurting, and he even threw up once or twice. After visiting the vet, they decided that a laboratory test would help figure out what was going on. The vet suspected either an infection from bacteria or possibly some kind of injury. Once diagnosed, Max started taking oral medication to fight off the infection. While the treatment seemed straightforward, there was always the worry about drug resistance.

So, let's talk about Urinary Tract Infections (UTIs). UTIs can be either infectious or inflammatory in nature. They often start when bacteria gain access to the urinary tract, or sometimes due to an injury. To diagnose a UTI, doctors usually rely on laboratory tests. Common signs that someone might have this condition include being unable to urinate easily, straining while trying to go, having cloudy urine, and even bloody urine. Some pets also exhibit excessive licking of their genital area, back pain, or vomiting as symptoms. Treatment typically involves oral medication, but there can be challenges if the bacteria develop resistance to the drugs used.

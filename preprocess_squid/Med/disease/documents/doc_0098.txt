Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Benign Prostatic Hypertrophy (BPH). One of the most common issues people with BPH face is increased urinary frequency. When it comes to managing this condition, doctors might prescribe oral medication or opt for surgery. The good news is that while we don't have much information about its exact cause or long-term outcomes, there are effective ways to address the symptoms and improve quality of life.

Once upon a time, there was a person who was diagnosed with Heart Failure. This condition can be of three types: degenerative, vascular, or others. The causes for this disease might include chronic diseases, trauma, infections, drugs, poisons, electric shock, heat stroke, tumors, valve diseases, or left-to-right shunts.

To diagnose the issue, doctors rely on clinical evaluation and imaging techniques. Common symptoms that individuals might experience include collapse, coughing, shallow and rapid breathing, swelling, weakness, weight loss, heart murmurs, a rapid heart rate, throbbing of the jugular veins, and swelling along the surface of the abdomen.

Treatment options for Heart Failure usually involve oral medications or surgery. The prognosis can vary from person to person or it might become a chronic condition. Pathogenesis can also be degenerative, vascular, or other. One challenge in treating Heart Failure is managing these various factors effectively.

In the case of Heart Failure, which can be either degenerative or vascular in nature, it might develop due to chronic diseases, trauma, infections, drugs, poisons, electric shock, heat stroke, tumors, valve disease, or left-to-right shunts. Diagnosing this condition often involves clinical evaluation and imaging tests. Common symptoms include collapse, coughing, shallow and rapid breathing, swelling, weakness, weight loss, heart murmur, a fast heartbeat, throbbing of the jugular veins, and swelling in the abdomen's surface. Treatments can range from oral medications to surgery, with the outcome being variable or chronic. The pathogenesis is often degenerative or vascular too, but it could also be other factors.

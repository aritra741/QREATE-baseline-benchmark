Once upon a time, there was Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), which falls under the psychiatric category. While we don't have clear information on its cause ("etiology" is listed as "nan"), doctors diagnose it through clinical evaluations. Common symptoms include behavioral issues, developmental delays, learning disabilities, mood disorders, and irritability. Treatment options for ADHD typically involve a combination of oral medication, psychotherapy, and lifestyle modifications. The prognosis for ADHD can be quite variable; it's often considered a chronic condition that can significantly impact quality of life. However, treatment challenges abound, including risks of overdiagnosis, overtreatment, polypharmacy, side effects, drug abuse, diversion, and poor long-term efficacy.

Once upon a time, there was a condition called Attention-Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD), which falls under the psychiatric category. While we don't know exactly what causes ADHD, doctors diagnose it through clinical evaluations. Common symptoms include behavioral issues, developmental delays, learning disabilities, mood disorders, and irritability. Treatment options typically involve oral medications, psychotherapy, and lifestyle modifications. The prognosis for ADHD is generally considered a chronic condition that can significantly impact quality of life but varies from person to person. Challenges in treatment often revolve around overdiagnosis, overtreatment, polypharmacy, side effects, drug abuse, diversion, and poor long-term efficacy.

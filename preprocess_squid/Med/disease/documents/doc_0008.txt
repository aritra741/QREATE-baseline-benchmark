Once upon a time, there was a disease called Multiple Myeloma, which falls under the category of neoplastic diseases. The journey to understanding this condition led researchers to explore various treatments like chemotherapy and stem cell transplantation, as no specific etiology or diagnostic methods were known at the time. While the common symptoms weren't clearly defined, patients often faced challenges during treatment. Despite these hurdles, there was hope for remission, making Multiple Myeloma a complex yet treatable condition.

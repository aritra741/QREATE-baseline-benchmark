Once upon a time, there was a person dealing with Plaque Psoriasis, an inflammatory disease. The doctors would diagnose it through clinical evaluation and sometimes a biopsy to confirm the condition. Common symptoms included the loss of the granular layer in the skin and a thickened stratum corneum. Unfortunately, details about treatments and prognosis for this specific condition were not readily available at that time. The underlying cause and mechanisms contributing to Plaque Psoriasis were understood as inflammatory processes, but there wasn't much known about the treatment challenges faced by patients.

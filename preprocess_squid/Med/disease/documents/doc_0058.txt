Once upon a time, there was a skin condition known as Plaque Psoriasis. This disease falls under the category of inflammatory disorders. While the exact cause isn't fully understood, doctors often diagnose it through clinical evaluation and sometimes a biopsy to confirm the presence of thickened stratum corneum and loss of granular layer. Unfortunately, due to the lack of specific treatments mentioned and unknown prognosis, managing this condition can be quite challenging. The inflammatory nature of the disease makes it particularly tricky to treat effectively.

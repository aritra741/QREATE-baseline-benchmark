So, let's talk about Plaque Psoriasis. It's an inflammatory disease where the skin becomes thick and scaly. Doctors primarily diagnose it through a clinical evaluation and sometimes a biopsy to confirm the presence of the condition. People often notice that their skin loses its granular layer and develops a thickened stratum corneum, which is just a fancy way of saying the outer layer of skin gets thicker. The exact cause isn't fully understood yet, but we know it's related to inflammation. Unfortunately, there aren't many treatment options available right now, which can make managing this condition quite challenging.

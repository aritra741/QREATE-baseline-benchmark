Imagine a story about Plaque Psoriasis, an inflammatory disease. During diagnosis, doctors rely on clinical evaluation and sometimes a biopsy to confirm the condition. People with this disease often experience loss of the granular layer in their skin and a thickened stratum corneum, making their skin appear abnormally dry or scaly. However, there's no specific treatment mentioned for Plaque Psoriasis, which makes managing its symptoms quite challenging. The exact cause remains unknown as well, adding to the mystery surrounding this inflammatory condition.

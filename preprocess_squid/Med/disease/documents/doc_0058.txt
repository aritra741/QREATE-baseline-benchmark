Once upon a time, there was a person who was diagnosed with Plaque Psoriasis, an inflammatory disease. The doctors would typically use clinical evaluation and possibly a biopsy to diagnose it. Common symptoms included the loss of granular layer and thickened stratum corneum, but no specific treatments or prognosis were noted by the medical community at that time. The pathogenesis of this condition was also linked to inflammation, though there weren't any known treatment challenges mentioned in their records.

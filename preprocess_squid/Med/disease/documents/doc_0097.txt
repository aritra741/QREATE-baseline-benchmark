Once upon a time, there was an imbalance in the gut microbiome, which could be caused by medication use or antibiotics, or even a lack of exposure to germs and bacteria. To diagnose this condition, doctors relied on clinical evaluations. Common symptoms included bloating, gas, diarrhea, stomach pain, and nausea. Treatments typically involved lifestyle modifications and dietary interventions, with the potential for either full recovery or developing into a chronic condition. The exact pathogenesis could be iatrogenic, infectious bacterial, nutritional deficiency, or other factors.

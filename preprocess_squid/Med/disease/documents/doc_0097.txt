Once upon a time, there was an imbalance in the gut microbiome, which can be caused by factors like medication use or antibiotics. Sometimes, it might also happen due to a lack of exposure to germs and bacteria. Diagnosis usually involves a clinical evaluation, and common symptoms include bloating, gas, diarrhea, stomach pain, and nausea. Treatment often includes lifestyle modifications and dietary interventions. The prognosis varies; the condition can either fully recover or turn into a chronic one. The pathogenesis can be iatrogenic, infectious bacterial, nutritional deficiency-related, or other factors.

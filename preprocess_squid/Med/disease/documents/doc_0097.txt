To diagnose it, doctors would usually rely on clinical evaluation. Common symptoms included bloating, gas, diarrhea, stomach pain, and nausea. Treatment often involved making lifestyle modifications and changes to dietary habits. The prognosis varied; some people recovered fully, while others might have a chronic condition.

Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), which falls under the category of functional disorders. While many people experience abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, and changes in bowel movements, doctors diagnose IBS through functional tests. The good news is that treatments like oral medications and dietary interventions can help manage symptoms. However, without a clear understanding of its pathogenesis or prognosis, treating IBS presents some challenges.

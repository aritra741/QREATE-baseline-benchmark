Once upon a time, there was a person dealing with Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), a functional disease. Although the exact cause wasn't clear, they knew that their condition often brought on abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, and altered bowel movements. To manage it, doctors recommended oral medication and dietary changes. While the long-term outlook for IBS was uncertain, both the treatment's effectiveness and its challenges remained topics of ongoing research.

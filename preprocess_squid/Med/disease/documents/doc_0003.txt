Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), which falls under the category of functional diseases. The common symptoms included abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, and altered bowel movements. To manage these issues, they might try oral medication or dietary interventions. While there's no clear information on etiology, diagnostic methods often involve functional tests. Unfortunately, we don't have much data on either prognosis or pathogenesis, nor do we know the specific challenges in treating this condition.

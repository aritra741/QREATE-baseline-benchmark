Once upon a time, someone was dealing with Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), which falls under the category of functional diseases. They went through some tests called functional tests to diagnose their condition. Common symptoms they experienced included abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, and altered bowel movements. To manage their symptoms, doctors suggested both oral medications and dietary interventions. Unfortunately, there's still a lot we don't fully understand about what causes IBS or its long-term outcomes, so more research is needed in these areas.

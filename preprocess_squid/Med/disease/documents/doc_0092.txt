Once upon a time, there was a disease called Primary Biliary Cholangitis. While we don't have all the details about its causes or what exactly happens in the body to cause it (the etiology and pathogenesis are still a bit of a mystery), we know that people with this condition often experience intense itching as a common symptom. Diagnosing this disease involves a combination of laboratory tests, imaging scans, and biopsies. For treatment, doctors typically prescribe oral medication since only one effective drug is currently available. However, managing the condition can be challenging due to limited treatment options.

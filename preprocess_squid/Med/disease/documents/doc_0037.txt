Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Postpartum Depression and Anxiety. It falls under the category of psychiatric diseases. While we don't have specific information on its causes or diagnostic methods yet, we know it can manifest in various ways like excessive worry, fear, extreme sleep deprivation, difficulty sleeping, anger, and frustration. The good news is that psychotherapy is often an effective treatment approach. However, more research is needed to fully understand the pathogenesis and any potential treatment challenges.

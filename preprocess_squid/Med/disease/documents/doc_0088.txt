The disease name is Sarcopenia, which falls under the categories of degenerative, nutritional, endocrine, and inflammatory. Its causes can include a lack of physical activity, poor diet, questionable lifestyle vices, systemic inflammatory disorders, decreased growth hormone levels, or significant underlying medical problems. For diagnosis, doctors might rely on clinical evaluations, functional tests, or imaging techniques. Common symptoms often include a decline in muscle mass and strength, as well as issues like bone fractures, difficulty standing or walking, body pains, balance problems, a shortened life span, fear of injury, social isolation, and emotional challenges such as anxiety and depression. These can manifest in daily activities like caring for oneself, driving, shopping, bathing, dressing, and completing household chores. Treatments might involve physical therapy, lifestyle modifications, dietary interventions, or oral medications, with the prognosis varying from partial recovery to a chronic condition that can be progressive, leading to quality of life impairment.

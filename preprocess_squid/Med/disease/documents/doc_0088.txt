Sarcopenia, a degenerative condition influenced by nutritional and endocrine factors along with inflammatory disorders, often develops due to a lack of physical activity, poor diet, questionable lifestyle habits like smoking or drinking, and decreased growth hormone levels. It can also be linked to significant underlying medical issues. Diagnosing Sarcopenia typically involves clinical evaluation, functional tests, and imaging.

Common symptoms include the decline in muscle mass, strength, and function, as well as increased risk of fractures, difficulty standing and walking, body pains, balance problems, reduced lifespan, fear of injury, social withdrawal, anxiety, depression, and difficulties with daily tasks like caring for oneself, driving, shopping, bathing, dressing, and completing household chores.

Treatment options may include physical therapy to improve muscle function, lifestyle modifications such as regular exercise, dietary interventions to address nutritional deficiencies, and oral medications. The prognosis can vary widely, ranging from partial recovery to a chronic condition that progressively impacts quality of life.

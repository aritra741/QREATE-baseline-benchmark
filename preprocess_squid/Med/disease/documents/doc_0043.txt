Once upon a time, there was a skin condition known as Acne. It could be both infectious and inflammatory in nature. Although the exact cause of Acne wasn't fully understood (due to missing information on etiology), doctors had ways to diagnose it. The common treatments for Acne included oral medication and lifestyle changes. However, there were challenges like drug resistance and side effects that made treating Acne a bit tricky sometimes. Despite these hurdles, with the right approach, people could often see improvement in their skin condition over time.

In the world of musculoskeletal issues, Low Back Pain can be caused by a variety of factors like muscle strain or herniated disc. During diagnosis, healthcare providers often start with clinical evaluation and imaging to pinpoint the exact cause, which could range from compressed nerve roots to degenerative discs. Common symptoms include lower back pain, tenderness, and sometimes even coordination impairment. For treatment, options vary widely from oral medication and physical therapy to more invasive methods like surgery or injections. The prognosis can be quite variable; some people might fully recover while others may face chronic conditions or disabilities. Pathogenesis can involve degeneration, inflammation, trauma, infections, tumors, or other causes. Treatment challenges include managing side effects, the risk of addiction, and potential negative drug interactions.

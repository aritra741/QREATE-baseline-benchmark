Once upon a time, there was a condition called Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD), which is an inflammatory disorder. The main issue here is that the antireflux barrier, specifically the lower esophageal sphincter, has some abnormalities. Doctors usually diagnose GERD through clinical evaluation, endoscopy, and functional tests.

People with GERD often experience heartburn, chest pain, chronic hoarseness, chronic cough, and regurgitation. Treatments can involve oral medications, lifestyle changes, and even surgery in some cases. While it's a chronic condition with a high risk of recurrence, managing symptoms is crucial for maintaining quality of life.

The pathogenesis of GERD involves inflammation, making the treatment journey sometimes challenging due to drug resistance, frequent relapses, side effects from medications, and poor patient adherence.

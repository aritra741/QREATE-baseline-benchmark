Once upon a time, there was a person who developed Psoriatic Arthritis (PsA), an autoimmune and inflammatory condition. The immune system overactivity associated with psoriasis often led to symptoms like joint stiffness, joint pain, and swelling, along with fatigue. To diagnose the condition, doctors conducted clinical evaluations, laboratory tests, imaging, and screening processes. Treatment options included oral medications, injections, lifestyle modifications, and dietary interventions. Although PsA is a chronic condition that can impair quality of life, its progression varies from person to person. The pathogenesis involves autoimmune and inflammatory mechanisms, which pose challenges such as side effects and cardiovascular risks from some drugs.

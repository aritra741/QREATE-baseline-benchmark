Once upon a time, there was a person who developed Cystoid Macular Edema, an inflammatory condition linked to uveitis. To diagnose this, doctors relied on imaging and clinical evaluations. The common symptoms included reduced vision, which impacted their daily life significantly. For treatment, oral medications, injections, and even surgery were used, but they often faced challenges like the complexity of the therapy, potential side effects, prolonged and costly treatments, and there wasn't a clear 'silver bullet' for effective therapy. Despite these hurdles, some individuals found relief leading to periods of remission, while others experienced chronic conditions with high recurrence risks affecting their quality of life in varying degrees.

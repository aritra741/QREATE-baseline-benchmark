Once upon a time, someone was dealing with a condition called Opioid-Induced Constipation, which is caused by using opioids. The main symptoms included difficult defecation, painful defecation, incomplete bowel-emptying, decreased frequency of emptying, hard and lumpy stools, and straining. To manage this, treatments often involved lifestyle modifications, oral medications, and dietary interventions. The prognosis for Opioid-Induced Constipation can vary, and some treatment challenges might include a failure with first and second line laxatives, requiring specialist medications instead.

Once someone starts taking opioids, they might develop Opioid-Induced Constipation, which is an iatrogenic condition. To diagnose it, doctors usually rely on a clinical evaluation. The common symptoms include difficult defecation, painful defecation, incomplete bowel-emptying, decreased frequency of emptying, hard and lumpy stools, and straining. Treatment might involve lifestyle modifications, oral medications, or dietary interventions. Prognosis can vary, but some people may face challenges like failure of first and second-line laxative treatments, which might require specialist medications.

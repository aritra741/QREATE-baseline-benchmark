Once upon a time, there was a condition called Antibiotic-associated diarrhea. This disease could be both iatrogenic (caused by medical intervention) and infectious in nature, often stemming from the use of antibiotics. Loose bowel motion and diarrhea were common symptoms for those affected.

The treatments typically involved dietary interventions to help manage symptoms. However, the exact diagnostic methods and treatment challenges weren't well-defined at the time. The iatrogenic factor played a significant role in its pathogenesis, making it an interesting case study in medical conditions.

Once upon a time, there was a condition called Fibromyalgia. It's considered both neurological and metabolic in nature, with a chronic course. Some experts are studying whether Clinical Endocannabinoid Deficiency (CECD) might be the cause, but the exact etiology remains largely unknown.

The common symptoms of Fibromyalgia include chronic fatigue, joint stiffness, insomnia, overall weakness, digestive trouble, headaches, anxiety, and something called "fibro fog." Doctors use a combination of oral medication, lifestyle modifications, and other treatments to manage it. The prognosis for patients with this condition is often described as a chronic condition that can severely impact their quality of life.

Pathogenesis involves both neurological and metabolic disorders. One major challenge in treating Fibromyalgia is that current medical treatments fall far short of providing effective relief, and the side effects of pharmaceutical drugs can be quite difficult to manage.

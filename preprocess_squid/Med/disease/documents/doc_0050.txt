Once upon a time, there was a condition called Fibromyalgia. This disease falls under the categories of neurological, metabolic, and chronic conditions. While some researchers are exploring if Clinical Endocannabinoid Deficiency (CECD) might be the root cause, for now, the exact etiology remains unknown.

The common symptoms experienced by those with Fibromyalgia include chronic fatigue, joint stiffness, insomnia, overall weakness, digestive trouble, headaches, anxiety, and a condition known as "fibro fog." Treatment options range from oral medication, lifestyle modifications, to other approaches. The prognosis for people living with this disease often involves managing it as a chronic condition which can significantly impact their quality of life.

The pathogenesis, or the underlying processes leading to these symptoms, is believed to involve both neurological and metabolic disorders. However, one significant challenge in treating Fibromyalgia lies in the fact that current medical treatments fall far short for many patients, often accompanied by unpleasant side effects from pharmaceutical drugs.

Once upon a time, there was a person suffering from Fibromyalgia, a disease that falls under the categories of neurological and metabolic conditions. The exact cause of this chronic condition is still being studied, with some suggesting it might be due to Clinical Endocannabinoid Deficiency (CECD). Symptoms like chronic fatigue, joint stiffness, insomnia, overall weakness, digestive trouble, headaches, anxiety, and a condition known as "fibro fog" are quite common. Treatment options include oral medications, lifestyle modifications, and other approaches. While the disease is often described as a chronic condition that can significantly impact quality of life, one of the biggest challenges in treating Fibromyalgia is that current medical treatments fall far short, often accompanied by side effects from pharmaceutical drugs.

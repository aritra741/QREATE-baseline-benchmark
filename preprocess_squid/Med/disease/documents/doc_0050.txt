Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Fibromyalgia. This condition falls under the umbrella of neurological and metabolic issues, making it a chronic condition as well. Some scientists are studying whether Clinical Endocannabinoid Deficiency (CECD) might be at the root of this illness, but the exact cause remains unclear.

The common symptoms for someone dealing with Fibromyalgia include chronic fatigue, joint stiffness, insomnia, overall weakness, digestive troubles, headaches, anxiety, and a condition known as "fibro fog." There are various treatment options available, such as oral medication, lifestyle modifications, and other approaches. However, the prognosis of this disease is often seen as a long-term struggle that can significantly impact one's quality of life.

The pathogenesis or underlying cause of Fibromyalgia involves both neurological and metabolic disorders. One of the main challenges in treating Fibromyalgia is that current medical treatments are far from perfect; they often come with significant side effects, making them less than ideal for many patients.

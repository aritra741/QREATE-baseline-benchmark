Once upon a time, there was a disease called Fibromyalgia. It's a complex condition that falls under the categories of neurological and metabolic disorders. The exact cause is still being studied; some believe it might be linked to something called Clinical Endocannabinoid Deficiency (CECD), but others think it could just be due to unknown factors. Unfortunately, there are no specific diagnostic methods for Fibromyalgia yet.

People with this condition often experience a range of symptoms like chronic fatigue, joint stiffness, insomnia, overall weakness, digestive troubles, headaches, anxiety, and something called "fibro fog." Doctors recommend various treatments including oral medication, lifestyle changes, and other approaches. The prognosis is generally considered to be a chronic condition that can significantly impact the quality of life.

One of the main challenges in treating Fibromyalgia is that current medical treatments fall far short of what's needed, and many pharmaceuticals come with side effects.

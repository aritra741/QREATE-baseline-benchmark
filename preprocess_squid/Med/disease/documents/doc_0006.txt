Once upon a time, there was a disease called Gout. Gout is both metabolic and inflammatory in nature. This condition arises due to hyperuricemia – an excess of uric acid in the blood. To diagnose Gout, doctors rely on clinical evaluation. Common symptoms include joint pain, toe pain, and recurring attacks. Treatment for Gout involves oral medication, lifestyle modifications, and dietary interventions. While effective, these treatments come with a high risk of recurrence. The pathogenesis or underlying cause is due to a metabolic disorder that leads to inflammation. Additionally, patients may face challenges like withdrawal symptoms as they try to manage their condition over the long term.

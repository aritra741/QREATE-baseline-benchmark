Once upon a time, there was a disease called Gout, which is both metabolic and inflammatory. It all starts with hyperuricemia, an excess of uric acid in the blood, leading to painful conditions like joint pain and toe pain that often recur. Doctors diagnose it through clinical evaluation, but managing Gout isn't easy; treatment involves oral medication, lifestyle modifications, and dietary changes due to its high recurrence risk. Despite these challenges, withdrawal symptoms can also pose a hurdle for those trying to stick to their treatment plan.

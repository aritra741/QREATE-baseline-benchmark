In the realm of women's health, Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS) is a condition that many are familiar with. While there isn't much known about its exact cause or "etiology," and no specific diagnostic methods are universally accepted, common symptoms include mood swings, bloating, and headaches. Treatments can vary widely depending on the individual, but often involve lifestyle changes and sometimes medication. The prognosis for PMS is generally good, with most women finding relief through these approaches. However, there are still challenges in fully understanding and managing this condition.

Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS). While the exact cause of PMS wasn't fully understood, and no specific diagnostic methods had been established, women often experienced a range of common symptoms like mood swings and physical discomfort. The good news is that various treatments were available to help manage these symptoms, though the long-term outlook was still being studied. There were challenges in treating PMS effectively, but ongoing research aimed to improve understanding and treatment options.

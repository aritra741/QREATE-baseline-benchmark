Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS). While the exact causes of PMS were still being explored, and there weren't definitive diagnostic methods or clear common symptoms, it was widely recognized for its impact on women's health. The treatments available were varied, but their effectiveness could vary from person to person. Despite these challenges, researchers continued to delve into understanding the complexities of how PMS develops and progresses, hoping to uncover more effective ways to manage this condition.

Once upon a time, there was an infectious disease called Drug Resistant Tuberculosis (DR TB). It's known for its drug-resistant nature and bacterial origins. The diagnosis usually involves both clinical evaluation and laboratory tests. People with DR TB often experience coughing and weakness.

The treatment for DR TB typically involves oral medication, but there are challenges due to poor responses to first-line drugs and the presence of drug resistance. In terms of outcomes, full recovery is possible, but in many cases, the mortality rate is quite high. The disease spreads through infectious bacterial agents that make it even more problematic to manage.

So, if someone suspects they or a loved one might have DR TB, it's crucial to seek medical help promptly and follow the prescribed treatment regimen closely.

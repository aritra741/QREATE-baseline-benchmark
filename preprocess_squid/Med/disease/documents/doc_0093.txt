Once upon a time, there was a disease called Drug Resistant Tuberculosis (DR TB), which falls under the category of infectious diseases. This particular ailment arises due to drug resistance and bacterial infection. To diagnose it, doctors rely on laboratory tests and clinical evaluations. Common symptoms include persistent coughing and feeling weak. The treatment typically involves oral medications, but there are challenges like drug resistance and poor response to first-line drugs. In terms of recovery, the prognosis can be either full recovery or high mortality depending on various factors.

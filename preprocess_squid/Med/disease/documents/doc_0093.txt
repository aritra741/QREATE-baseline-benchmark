Once upon a time, there was a disease called Drug Resistant Tuberculosis (DR TB). It's an infectious condition caused by drug-resistant bacteria. The main symptoms include persistent cough and feeling weak. Doctors use laboratory tests and clinical evaluations to diagnose it. Treatment usually involves oral medications, but the pathogenesis can make things tricky due to the drug resistance and poor response to first-line drugs. Despite these challenges, some patients recover fully, while others face a high risk of mortality.

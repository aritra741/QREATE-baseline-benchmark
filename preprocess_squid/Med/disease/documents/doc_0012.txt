Once upon a time, there was a patient who developed Acute Graft-Versus-Host Disease after undergoing bone marrow transplantation. This disease is iatrogenic and inflammatory in nature. Common symptoms included abdominal pain, hair loss, hepatitis, lung disorders, and digestive tract issues. The treatment primarily involved injections, but the prognosis was concerning with high mortality rates. The exact pathogenesis was also attributed to being iatrogenic and inflammatory, presenting significant challenges for effective treatment.

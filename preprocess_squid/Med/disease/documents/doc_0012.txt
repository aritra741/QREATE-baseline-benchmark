Once upon a time, there was a patient who developed Acute Graft-Versus-Host Disease after undergoing bone marrow transplantation. This condition is iatrogenic and inflammatory in nature. The common symptoms included abdominal pain, hair loss, hepatitis, lung disorders, and digestive tract issues. Treatment often involved injections, but the prognosis was quite serious with high mortality rates. The exact pathogenesis of this disease is due to iatrogenic and inflammatory factors, though there were no specific diagnostic methods or treatment challenges mentioned in medical literature at that time.

Once upon a time, there was a person who underwent bone marrow transplantation. This procedure led to something called Acute Graft-Versus-Host Disease (AGVHD), which can be iatrogenic and inflammatory in nature. The common symptoms included abdominal pain, hair loss, hepatitis, lung disorders, and digestive tract issues. Treatment typically involved injections, but the prognosis was not very favorable with high mortality rates. Despite these challenges, understanding the pathogenesis as both iatrogenic and inflammatory helped guide treatment approaches.

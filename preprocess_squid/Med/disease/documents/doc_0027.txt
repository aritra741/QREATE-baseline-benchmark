Once upon a time, there was a disease called Onchocerciasis. It's an infectious and parasitic condition caused by the parasite Onchocerca volvulus, often transmitted through bites from black flies. People affected by this disease might experience skin itching and vision impairment. The main treatment involves taking oral medication. While we know a lot about how it works as an infectious and parasitic condition, there's still much to learn about its prognosis and the challenges in treating it effectively.

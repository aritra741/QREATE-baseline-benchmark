Once upon a time, there was a disease called Onchocerciasis. This condition is both infectious and parasitic, caused by the tiny worm Onchocerca volvulus or through a bite from a black fly. The common symptoms included intense skin itching and vision impairment. Luckily, it can be treated with oral medication, though the long-term outlook isn't always clear due to a lack of detailed information on prognosis and treatment challenges.

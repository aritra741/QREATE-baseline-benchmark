Once upon a time, there was an infectious disease called Legionnaires' Disease. It's caused by Legionella bacteria found in water systems like poorly maintained air-conditioners, residential hot water systems, and swimming pools. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluation. The common symptoms include fever, gastrointestinal issues, chest pain, shortness of breath, and confusion. Treatment mostly involves oral medication, and the good news is that with proper care, most people make a full recovery with low mortality rates. This disease works by causing an infectious bacterial infection, but there aren't any significant treatment challenges to note right now.

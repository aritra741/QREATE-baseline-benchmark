Once upon a time, there was an infectious disease called Legionnaires' Disease. This condition arises from Legionella bacteria that can be found in water systems like poorly maintained air-conditioners, residential hot water tanks, and swimming pools. Doctors diagnose the illness through clinical evaluation, and common symptoms include fever, gastrointestinal issues, chest pain, shortness of breath, and confusion. Luckily, most patients recover fully with oral medication, although there's a low mortality rate. The disease works its way in via infectious bacteria, but thankfully, full recovery is quite common!

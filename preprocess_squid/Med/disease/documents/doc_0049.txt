Once upon a time, someone was dealing with anxiety disorders, which fall under the psychiatric category. The causes of their condition were often linked to stress, substance abuse, and job-related pressures. To diagnose them accurately, doctors typically rely on clinical evaluations.

Their symptoms included frequent headaches, irrational fears, feelings of nervousness, nausea, vomiting, panic attacks, excessive worry, and sweating. For treatment, they might have tried oral medications, psychotherapy sessions, lifestyle modifications like regular exercise, dietary interventions, relaxation techniques, and meditation practices. However, one challenge was managing the side effects that came with some of these treatments.

That's their story!

Once a person starts experiencing anxiety, they might deal with various symptoms like headaches, irrational fear, and nervousness. These feelings can be exacerbated by stress or substance abuse at their workplace. To diagnose the condition, doctors rely on clinical evaluations. Treatment options include oral medications, psychotherapy sessions, lifestyle changes such as regular exercise, dietary interventions, relaxation techniques, and meditation to manage daily stresses better. While these methods are effective, they might come with side effects that need careful management.

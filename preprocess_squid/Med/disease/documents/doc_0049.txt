Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Anxiety Disorders, which falls under the psychiatric category. The disease often stems from stress, substance abuse, or job-related pressures. To diagnose it accurately, doctors rely on clinical evaluations. Common symptoms include headaches, irrational fears, nervousness, nausea, vomiting, panic attacks, excessive worry, and sweating.

For treatment, options range widely: oral medication, psychotherapy, lifestyle modifications like regular exercise, dietary interventions, relaxation techniques, and meditation. While these treatments can be highly effective, one challenge is managing the side effects that come with some of them.

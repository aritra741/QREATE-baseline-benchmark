Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Anxiety Disorders, which fall under the psychiatric category. The root causes often included stress, substance abuse, and job-related stress. To diagnose it, doctors relied on clinical evaluations. Common symptoms were headache, irrational fear, nervousness, nausea, vomiting, panic attacks, excessive worry, and sweating. Treatment options ranged from oral medication and psychotherapy to lifestyle modifications like regular exercise and relaxation techniques such as meditation. While dietary interventions played a role too, the challenges of treatment often included dealing with side effects.

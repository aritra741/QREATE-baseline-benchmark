Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Human African Trypanosomiasis, also known as Sleeping Sickness. This disease falls under the categories of infectious and parasitic diseases. It's caused by an infection from either Trypanosoma brucei gambiense or Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense. Common symptoms include fever, headaches, joint pains, itching, and disruptions in sleep patterns. The treatment usually involves oral medication or injections. However, the prognosis can be quite serious, with a progressive condition leading to high mortality rates. One of the main challenges in treating this disease is the limited number of available treatments.

Once upon a time, there was a disease known as Human African Trypanosomiasis, also called Sleeping Sickness. It's an infectious and parasitic condition caused by the Trypanosoma brucei gambiense infection or Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense infection. While we don't have specific diagnostic methods for it yet, people often experience symptoms such as fever, headaches, joint pains, itching, and disruptions in their sleep patterns. The treatment usually involves oral medication or injections, but the prognosis can be quite serious, with the disease being progressive and having a high mortality rate. The pathogenesis of this condition is due to an infectious parasite, which makes treating it challenging because of limited options available.

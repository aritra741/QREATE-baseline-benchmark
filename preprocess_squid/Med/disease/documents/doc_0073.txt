Once upon a time, there was an infectious and parasitic disease called Human African Trypanosomiasis, also known as Sleeping Sickness. This condition is caused by either Trypanosoma brucei gambiense infection or Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense infection. Common symptoms of the disease include fever, headaches, joint pains, itching, and disruptions in sleep patterns. Luckily, treatments involve oral medications and injections, but the prognosis can be progressive with a high mortality rate. The pathogenesis is infectious and parasitic, making treatment challenging due to limited options available.

Once upon a time, there was a disease called Human African Trypanosomiasis, also known as Sleeping Sickness. It's an infectious and parasitic condition caused by the Trypanosoma brucei gambiense infection or Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense infection. While there aren't any specific diagnostic methods mentioned for this disease, its common symptoms include fever, headaches, joint pains, itching, and disruptions in sleep patterns. The treatments involve oral medication and injections, though the prognosis can be progressive with a high mortality rate. Understanding the pathogenesis as an infectious parasitic process also highlights the challenges in treatment due to limited options available.

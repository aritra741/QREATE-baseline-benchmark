Once upon a time, there was an infectious and parasitic disease called Human African Trypanosomiasis, also known as Sleeping Sickness. It's caused by the infection with either Trypanosoma brucei gambiense or Trypanosoma brucei rhodesiense. The symptoms can include fever, headaches, joint pains, itching, and disruptions in sleep patterns. Common treatments involve oral medication or injections, but due to limited treatment options, the prognosis can be progressive with a high mortality rate. This disease progresses through infectious parasitic processes, making it challenging to manage effectively.

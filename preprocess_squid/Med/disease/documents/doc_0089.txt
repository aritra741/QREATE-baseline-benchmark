Once upon a time, there was a patient diagnosed with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC), a type of neoplastic disease. The diagnostic methods involved clinical evaluation and laboratory tests, often followed by imaging to confirm the diagnosis. Treatment options included chemotherapy and immunotherapy injections, but only a minority found benefit from these therapies due to their high cost. Identifying who would respond well was critical, as the prognosis for this condition could be quite variable, with progressive disease leading to significant quality of life impairment in many cases.

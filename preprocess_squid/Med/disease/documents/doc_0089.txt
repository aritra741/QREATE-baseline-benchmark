Once upon a time, there was a disease called Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC), which includes advanced non-small–cell lung cancers and non-squamous NSCLCs as well as squamous NSCLCs. This type of cancer can be neoplastic or inflammatory in nature. The doctors use clinical evaluation, laboratory tests, and imaging to diagnose it, though there are no common symptoms specifically noted for this condition.

When treating the disease, options include injection, chemotherapy, and immunotherapy. However, prognosis varies widely; some may face a high mortality rate while others might experience progressive conditions or quality of life impairment. The pathogenesis can be neoplastic or inflammatory as well. One significant challenge in treatment is that only a minority benefit from immunotherapy due to its high cost, making it crucial to identify the right responders early on.

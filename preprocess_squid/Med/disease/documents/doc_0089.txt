Once upon a time, there was a patient diagnosed with Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC), which includes both non-squamous and squamous types. This disease falls under the category of neoplastic diseases. The diagnostic methods typically involve clinical evaluation, laboratory tests, and imaging scans. While common symptoms are not always listed, patients often experience progressive quality-of-life impairment as the condition worsens.

The treatments for this advanced form include chemotherapy and immunotherapy through injections, but only a minority benefit from immunotherapy due to its high cost, with identifying responders being critically important. Despite these challenges, the prognosis can be highly variable, with some experiencing significant mortality while others may have a more progressive course.

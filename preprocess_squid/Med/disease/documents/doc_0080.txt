Once there was a person who experienced recurring headaches, often feeling dizzy and having issues with light, sound, and smell sensitivity. They also noticed nasal congestion, runny nose, tearing of the eyes, ear pain, pressure across the forehead, and pain behind their eyes and nose. These symptoms would come and go, making every day quite uncomfortable. After consulting a doctor, they found that clinical evaluations were sufficient for diagnosing this condition known as Sinus Migraine, which is believed to be either inflammatory or idiopathic in nature. The treatments often involved oral medication, but there were some challenges since the patient had a poor response to standard sinusitis treatments.

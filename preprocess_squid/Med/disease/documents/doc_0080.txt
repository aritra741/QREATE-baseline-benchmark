One day, a person was experiencing some concerning symptoms that included nasal congestion, tearing of the eyes, and runny nose. They also felt ear pain, pressure behind their ears, dizziness, headaches, nausea, light sensitivity, sound sensitivity, and smell sensitivity. The pain seemed to be concentrated across their forehead, behind their eyes, and beneath their cheeks. After a clinical evaluation, it was determined that they were dealing with a Sinus Migraine, which is an inflammatory condition of unknown cause (idiopathic). Despite the condition being challenging to treat due to poor response to standard sinusitis treatments, oral medication was prescribed as a potential solution.

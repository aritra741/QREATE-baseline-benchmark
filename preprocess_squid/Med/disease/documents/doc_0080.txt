Once a person experiences a Sinus Migraine, they might encounter various symptoms like nasal congestion, tearing of the eyes, and runny nose. They could also feel ear pain or pressure, dizziness, head pain, nausea, light sensitivity, sound sensitivity, smell sensitivity, along with pressure across the forehead and pain behind the eyes or in the nose and beneath the cheeks. The doctors usually diagnose this condition through clinical evaluation, and treatment often involves oral medication. However, some people might face challenges due to a poor response to standard sinusitis treatments.

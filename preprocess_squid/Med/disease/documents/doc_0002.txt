Once upon a time, someone was dealing with Pleural Effusion, which can be caused by various conditions like pneumonia, pulmonary embolism, kidney disease, or congestive heart failure. The common symptoms included shortness of breath and chest pain. For treatment, oral medication or other methods were often used, but the prognosis wasn't always clear-cut due to the complexity of the pathogenesis, which could be inflammatory, infectious bacterial, vascular, or other.

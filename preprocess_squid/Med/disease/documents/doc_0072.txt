Once upon a time, there was a disease called Rabies. This disease falls under the categories of infectious and inflammatory conditions. It typically starts with a viral infection or a bite from an infected dog. To diagnose it, doctors rely on laboratory tests and clinical evaluations.

The common symptoms include hydrophobia (the fear of water), fever, headache, sore throat, and pain while drinking water. The treatment usually involves administering injections as they are the primary method to combat this condition. However, despite these efforts, the prognosis for Rabies is quite grim, often leading to high mortality rates or severe neurological complications due to its infectious viral nature and inflammatory pathogenesis.

Once upon a time, there was a disease called Rabies. It's both an infectious and inflammatory condition caused by a viral infection or a dog bite. Doctors diagnose it through laboratory tests and clinical evaluations. The common symptoms include hydrophobia (fear of water), fever, headache, sore throat, and pain while drinking water. Treatment usually involves injections, but the prognosis is not very good; Rabies has a high mortality rate and can lead to severe neurological complications.

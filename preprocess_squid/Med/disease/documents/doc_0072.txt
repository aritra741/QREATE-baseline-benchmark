Once upon a time, there was Rabies, a disease that can be both infectious and inflammatory. It typically spreads through viral infection or dog bites. Doctors diagnose it using laboratory tests and clinical evaluations. The common symptoms include hydrophobia (the fear of water), fever, headache, and sore throat—especially pain while drinking water. Treatment usually involves injections, but the prognosis is often bleak with high mortality rates and severe neurological complications. Understanding how Rabies leads to such devastating outcomes through its infectious viral nature and inflammatory response can help us appreciate why early diagnosis and prevention are so crucial.

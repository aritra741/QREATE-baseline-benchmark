To diagnose the condition, doctors might use laboratory tests or conduct a clinical evaluation. Common symptoms include hydrophobia (the fear of water), fever, headache, sore throat, and pain while drinking water. The primary treatment is an injection, but unfortunately, the prognosis for Rabies is often poor due to its high mortality rate and severe neurological complications.

Once upon a time, there was an illness called Peritonitis. This condition could be either infectious or inflammatory, often caused by bacteria like Staphylococcus aureus and Ewingella americana, disruptions in the peritoneum, catheterization, or even hospital water sources. To diagnose it, doctors would rely on clinical evaluation and laboratory tests. Common symptoms included generalized abdominal pain, decreased appetite, left chest pain, pressure, and tenderness with a positive rebound sign. The usual treatment involved injections.

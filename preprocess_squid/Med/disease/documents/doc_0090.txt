In the case of Peritonitis, a disease that can be both infectious and inflammatory, it's often caused by Staphylococcus aureus or Ewingella americana. Other factors include the disruption of the peritoneum, catheterization, or even contaminated hospital water sources. To diagnose this condition, doctors rely on clinical evaluation and laboratory tests. Common symptoms include generalized abdominal pain, decreased appetite, left chest pain, and tenderness with a positive rebound sign. The treatment typically involves injections to combat the infection.

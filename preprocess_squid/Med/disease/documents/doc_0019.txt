Once upon a time, there was a condition called Hemophilia B, which is a genetic disease. It stems from the lack of functional factor IX (FIX). While there are no specific diagnostic methods mentioned, people with this condition often experience chronic pain and acute pain, bleeding, joint problems, and mobility issues. They might also deal with pain and discomfort, as well as anxiety and depression due to their condition. The treatment involves regular injections, but managing the high rates of chronic pain requires a multidisciplinary approach. Unfortunately, it's a long-term condition that impacts the quality of life for many affected individuals.

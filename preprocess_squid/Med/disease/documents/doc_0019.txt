Once upon a time, there was a genetic condition called Hemophilia B, which is caused by a lack of functional factor IX (FIX). People with this disease often experience chronic and acute pain, bleeding, joint issues, mobility problems, and may also face anxiety and depression. To manage their condition, injections are commonly used as treatment. The prognosis for those affected can be quite challenging, often leading to ongoing health issues that impact the quality of life. This is due to a genetic mutation that affects the body's ability to clot blood properly. Managing Hemophilia B comes with its own set of challenges, including high rates of chronic pain and a need for multidisciplinary care involving various specialists.

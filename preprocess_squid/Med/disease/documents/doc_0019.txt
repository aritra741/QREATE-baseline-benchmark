Once upon a time, there was a genetic condition called Hemophilia B. This disease happens because the body lacks functional factor IX (FIX), which is crucial for blood clotting. The common symptoms include chronic and acute pain, bleeding, joint issues, mobility problems, as well as pain or discomfort that can lead to anxiety and depression. To manage this challenging condition, doctors often prescribe injections. While treatment helps, Hemophilia B tends to be a long-term issue with significant impacts on the quality of life. The root cause is usually a genetic mutation, which complicates matters further due to high rates of chronic pain and frequent use of analgesics, making multidisciplinary care essential for managing this condition effectively.

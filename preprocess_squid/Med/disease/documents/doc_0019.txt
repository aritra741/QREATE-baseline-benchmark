Once upon a time, there was a genetic condition called Hemophilia B. It's caused by a lack of functional factor IX (FIX), which is a protein that helps the blood clot. People with this disease often experience chronic and acute pain, bleeding in joints, mobility problems, and sometimes even anxiety and depression due to these issues. The treatments involve regular injections, but managing it can be quite challenging, especially dealing with high rates of chronic pain and the need for multidisciplinary care. Despite these difficulties, living with Hemophilia B is often a lifelong condition that impacts the quality of life.

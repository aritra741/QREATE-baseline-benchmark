In the world of respiratory health, asthma stands out as a condition that can be both autoimmune and allergic in nature. Often, it's triggered by an allergic reaction or hypersensitivity, sometimes even linked to vitamin D deficiency. While there's no specific diagnostic method mentioned for asthma, its common symptoms include wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing. Treatments can involve oral medications, inhalers, and dietary interventions. However, the challenges and prognosis of managing asthma aren't fully understood yet.

Once upon a time, there was a health issue called Asthma. It's considered both an autoimmune and allergic condition, often triggered by allergic reactions or hypersensitivity or even a lack of vitamin D. Symptoms are quite noticeable, including wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing. For treatment, doctors might prescribe oral medications, inhalers, or dietary interventions to help manage the condition. However, there's no specific diagnostic method mentioned for Asthma in medical literature. The exact pathogenesis is linked to autoimmune and allergic responses, but there are no known challenges in treating Asthma at this moment.

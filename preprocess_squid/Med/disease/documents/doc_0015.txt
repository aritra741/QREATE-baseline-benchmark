Once upon a time, there was a condition called Asthma. It's known to be both an autoimmune and allergic disease, often triggered by allergic reactions, hypersensitivity, or even a lack of vitamin D. The most common symptoms include wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, and coughing. Treatment options typically involve oral medications, inhalers, and dietary interventions.

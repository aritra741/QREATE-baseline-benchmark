Once upon a time, there was a person diagnosed with Crohn's disease, an inflammatory condition. The common symptoms included diarrhea, stomach pain, cramps, fatigue, bloody stools, mouth sores, weight loss, joint pain, back pain, changes in vision, and fever. For treatment, they often relied on oral medication, but this came with challenges like side effects, poor adherence, and withdrawal symptoms. Despite these hurdles, the good news was that remission was possible, turning their condition into a manageable chronic one.

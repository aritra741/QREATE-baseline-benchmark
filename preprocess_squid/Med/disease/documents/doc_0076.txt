Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Osteoarthritis. Despite the prevalence of this disease, many aspects such as its exact cause (etiology), how to diagnose it (diagnostic methods), and what common symptoms people might experience (common symptoms) remain somewhat unclear. Luckily, there are various treatments available (treatments), but predicting how someone with Osteoarthritis will fare in the long term (prognosis) can be tricky. The exact biological process behind its development (pathogenesis) and some of the challenges faced when trying to treat it (treatment challenges) are still areas that need more research.

Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Thoracic Outlet Syndrome (TOS), which can be caused by various factors like injuries from car or motorcycle accidents, repetitive stress from sports or work, pregnancy, or even anatomical differences such as having an extra rib. To diagnose it, doctors rely on clinical evaluations. The common symptoms include muscle wasting in the hands, numbness and tingling in the arms and fingers, aches and pains in the shoulder area, arm pain and swelling, and weakening grip strength. Treatment options often involve physical therapy, oral medications, or even surgery if necessary. While TOS can become a chronic condition, managing it with proper treatment and care can significantly improve one's quality of life.

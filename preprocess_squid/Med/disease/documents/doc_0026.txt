Once upon a time, there was a condition called Thoracic Outlet Syndrome (TOS), which can be caused by different factors like an injury in an automobile or motorcycle accident, repetitive stress injuries from sports or employment, pregnancy, or even anatomical differences such as having an extra rib. Diagnosis usually starts with a clinical evaluation.

The symptoms of TOS are quite varied and can include muscle wasting in the hands, numbness and tingling in the arms and fingers, aches and pains in the shoulder area, arm pain and swelling, and weakening grip and arm strength. Treatment options vary widely, from physical therapy to oral medication or even surgery.

Unfortunately, TOS often becomes a chronic condition, which can present some challenges during treatment.

Once upon a time, there was a mysterious condition known as Chronic Fatigue Syndrome and Myalgic Encephalomyelitis (ME). This disease can be categorized both as psychiatric and infectious/viral in nature, with some considering it idiopathic. The underlying causes might include psychological factors, stress, or even viruses similar to those linked to mouse leukemia.

Doctors diagnose this condition primarily through clinical evaluation since there's no specific test for Chronic Fatigue Syndrome and ME. A common symptom experienced by many is fatigue, often accompanied by physical functioning impairment. To manage the symptoms, treatments such as psychotherapy, physical therapy, and lifestyle modifications are commonly recommended.

While living with a chronic condition can significantly impact one’s quality of life, there are challenges in treating Chronic Fatigue Syndrome and ME. Poor insurance coverage for treatment, the potential worsening of symptoms during treatment, and ensuring that severe cases are excluded from typical treatments can make managing this condition quite difficult.

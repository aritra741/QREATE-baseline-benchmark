Once upon a time, there was a common ailment called Periodontal Disease. This disease can be both infectious and inflammatory in nature. It's often caused by bacteria, genetic factors, other diseases, or even trauma to the teeth or gums.

To diagnose it, doctors typically rely on clinical evaluations and imaging techniques. Common signs that you might have this condition include bleeding from your gums, bone loss around your teeth, excessive tooth mobility, gum recession, exudate pus (which is a clear fluid along or under the gumline), and other symptoms related to inflammation.

Treatment options for Periodontal Disease can vary but often involve surgery and oral medications. The exact prognosis isn't always clear-cut due to the complexity of the condition, which involves infectious bacteria and inflammatory responses.

Once upon a time, there was a person who started to experience some troubling symptoms. They noticed that their gums were bleeding, and over time, they began to lose bone around their teeth, making the teeth feel loose. There was also gum recession, pus coming from under the gums, and sometimes clear fluid along or under the gumline. After visiting the dentist for a clinical evaluation and imaging tests, the diagnosis was Periodontal Disease. This disease has both infectious and inflammatory origins, driven by bacteria, genetic factors, and trauma. The treatment options ranged from oral medications to surgical procedures to address these issues.

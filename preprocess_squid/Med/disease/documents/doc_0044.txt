Once upon a time, there was Periodontal Disease. This disease falls into two categories: infectious and inflammatory. The disease starts when bacteria, genes, or even trauma can trigger it. To diagnose it, dentists rely on clinical evaluations and imaging techniques. Common symptoms include bleeding gums, bone loss, loose teeth, gum recession, pus, and clear fluid along or under the gumline. Treatments might involve surgery or oral medication to address these issues.

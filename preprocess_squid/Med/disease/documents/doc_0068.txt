There's a condition called a Heart Murmur, and while we know the diagnostic methods involve clinical evaluation, people experiencing it often report symptoms such as chest pain, faintness, fainting, dizziness, palpitations, leg swelling, weakness, fatigue, or shortness of breath during physical exertion. Treatment options might include oral medication or surgery, but the exact prognosis and any specific challenges in treating Heart Murmurs are still being researched.

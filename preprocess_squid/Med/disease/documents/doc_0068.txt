Once upon a time, someone was dealing with a condition called a Heart Murmur. During their diagnosis, doctors relied mainly on clinical evaluations to understand what was going on. The person often experienced symptoms like chest pain, feeling faint, fainting spells, dizziness, palpitations, swelling in the legs, and feelings of weakness and fatigue. Sometimes, they also had shortness of breath during physical exertion. To manage their condition, doctors prescribed oral medications or even considered surgery as a treatment option. However, there wasn't much information available about how this condition would progress over time (prognosis) or what the underlying causes might be (pathogenesis and treatment challenges).

Let's talk about Chlamydia, which is an infectious sexually transmitted infection. If you think you might have it, common symptoms to look out for include unusual vaginal discharge, pain or burning sensation during urination, frequent urination, bleeding between periods, bleeding after sex, grey or pale yellow discharge, and pain during sex. You might also experience problems with urination, abdominal pain, pain during sex, pain during urination, unusually painful or heavy periods, or even green or yellow discharge. More severe symptoms can include severe pain in the abdomen, fever, nausea, or vomiting.

If diagnosed, you'll likely be treated with oral medication or an injection and, with proper care, most people make a full recovery or remain asymptomatic. The disease is caused by infectious bacteria, so catching it early makes a big difference.

The disease name is Chlamydia, which falls under the category of an infectious sexually transmitted infection (STI). To diagnose it, doctors typically use laboratory tests or screenings. Common symptoms include unusual vaginal discharge, pain or burning sensation during urination, frequent urination, bleeding between periods, bleeding after sex, grey or pale yellow discharge, pain during sex, problems with urination, abdominal pain, and unusually painful or heavy periods. You might also experience green or yellow discharge, severe pain in the abdomen, fever, nausea, or vomiting. Treatment usually involves oral medication or an injection, and most patients have a full recovery or may be asymptomatic afterward. The pathogenesis of Chlamydia is due to infectious bacteria.

The disease name is Chlamydia, which falls under the category of an infectious sexually transmitted infection. To diagnose it, doctors typically use laboratory tests or screening methods. Common symptoms include unusual vaginal discharge, pain or burning sensation during urination, frequent urination, bleeding between periods, bleeding after sex, grey or pale yellow discharge, pain during sex, problems with urination, abdominal pain, pain during sex, pain during urination, unusually painful or heavy periods, and unusual discharge that may be green or yellow. Treatment usually involves oral medication or an injection, and the prognosis is generally full recovery, though some people might remain asymptomatic. The pathogenesis of Chlamydia involves infectious bacteria.

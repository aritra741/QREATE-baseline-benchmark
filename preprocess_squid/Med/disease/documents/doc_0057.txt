Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Stomach Cancer, which falls under the neoplastic disease type. It turns out that eating foods preserved by drying, smoking, salting, or pickling, as well as smoking cigarettes, can increase one's risk of developing this condition.

To diagnose it, doctors might use endoscopy, biopsy, or imaging techniques. Common symptoms include vomiting blood, passing black tar-like stools, indigestion, and heartburn. Treatment options vary widely and may include surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, biological therapy, and dietary intervention. While the treatment can be challenging due to how difficult it is to cure, these methods aim to address the neoplastic pathogenesis.

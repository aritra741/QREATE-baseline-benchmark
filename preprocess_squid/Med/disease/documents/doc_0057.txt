Once upon a time, there was a disease called Stomach Cancer. This cancer falls under the neoplastic category and can be triggered by eating certain preserved foods like those dried or smoked, salted, or pickled. Another risk factor is smoking cigarettes.

To diagnose it, doctors might use methods such as endoscopy, biopsy, or imaging tests. Common symptoms to look out for include vomiting blood, passing black tar-like stools, indigestion, and heartburn.

Treatment options can vary but often involve surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, biological therapy, and dietary interventions. While the pathogenesis of Stomach Cancer is neoplastic, treating it can be quite challenging due to its difficult-to-cure nature.

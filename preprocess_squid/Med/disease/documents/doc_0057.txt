Once someone was diagnosed with Stomach Cancer, a neoplastic disease. They found out that their condition might have been influenced by eating foods preserved through drying, smoking, salting, or pickling, as well as by smoking cigarettes. To confirm the diagnosis, doctors used endoscopy, biopsy, and imaging techniques. Common symptoms included vomiting blood, passing black tar-like stools, indigestion, and heartburn. The treatment plan often involved surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, biological therapy, and dietary intervention to manage their condition. Although challenging to cure, they hoped for the best with each step of the treatment process.

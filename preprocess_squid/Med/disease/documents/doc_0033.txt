Once upon a time, there was a disease called Duchenne muscular dystrophy. It's both genetic and degenerative, stemming from a genetic mutation. While the diagnostic methods for this condition are not well-established yet, we do know that muscle degeneration and weakness are common symptoms. People affected by it often struggle with walking and breathing difficulties as well. Doctors use injections and oral medications to treat it, but these treatments come with challenges such as limited drug applicability, high costs, and regulatory hurdles. The prognosis is generally not good, with progressive deterioration leading to a poor quality of life and high mortality rates.

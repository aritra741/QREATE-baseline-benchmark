Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Duchenne muscular dystrophy. This disease falls under the categories of genetic and degenerative conditions. The root cause of this ailment lies in genetic mutations. While specific diagnostic methods aren't widely recognized yet, the common symptoms include muscle weakness and degeneration, making it challenging for individuals to walk or breathe easily.

The treatments available are mainly injections and oral medications, but these come with their own set of challenges. Currently, the effectiveness of existing drugs is limited, which adds to the cost and complicates regulatory approval processes. Ultimately, the prognosis for those affected by Duchenne muscular dystrophy is often associated with high mortality rates, progressive disease progression, and significant impairment in quality of life.

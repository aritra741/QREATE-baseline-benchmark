Once upon a time, there was a condition called Duchenne muscular dystrophy. It's a genetic and degenerative disease caused by a genetic mutation. Although the diagnostic methods aren't widely known yet, we do know that it often leads to muscle degeneration, muscle weakness, difficulty in walking, and difficulty in breathing. Treatments include injections and oral medications, but there are challenges like limited applicability of current drugs, high costs, and regulatory hurdles. The prognosis is generally poor with high mortality rates and progressive impairment of quality of life.

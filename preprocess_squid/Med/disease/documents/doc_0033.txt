Once upon a time, there was a young boy named Jack who was diagnosed with Duchenne muscular dystrophy. This disease is both genetic and degenerative, caused by a genetic mutation. His symptoms included muscle degeneration, muscle weakness, difficulty in walking, and trouble breathing. To manage his condition, doctors prescribed injections and oral medications. Sadly, the prognosis for Duchenne muscular dystrophy is generally poor, with high mortality rates, progressive nature, and significant impairment of quality of life. The pathogenesis involves genetic mutations leading to a degenerative process. One major challenge in treating this disease is the limited applicability of current drugs, along with associated costs and regulatory hurdles.

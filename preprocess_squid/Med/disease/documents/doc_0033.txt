Once upon a time, there was a disease called Duchenne muscular dystrophy. This condition belongs to the genetic and degenerative types. It all starts with a genetic mutation. While we don't have any specific diagnostic methods yet, we can identify it through common symptoms like muscle degeneration, muscle weakness, difficulty in walking, and difficulty in breathing. Treatment options include injections and oral medications, but there are challenges such as the limited applicability of current drugs, cost, and regulatory hurdles. Unfortunately, the prognosis isn't very optimistic; it's marked by high mortality rates, progressive nature, and a significant impact on the quality of life.

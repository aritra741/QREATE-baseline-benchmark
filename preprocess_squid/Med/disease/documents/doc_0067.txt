Once upon a time, there was a genetic condition called Huntington's disease or Huntington's Chorea. This ailment falls under the categories of genetic, degenerative, and psychiatric diseases due to its complex nature. The root cause lies in an abnormal number of CAG repeats within a specific gene. Common symptoms include movement disorders like low eye movements and muscle stress, behavioral changes such as communication difficulties and loss of confidence, and psychiatric issues like difficulty with simple tasks, change in attitude, and sadness. Patients might also experience a forced smile, loss of energy, fatigue, learning difficulties, and even a loss of sexual desire.

For treatment, oral medication and lifestyle modifications are the primary approaches available today. Unfortunately, Huntington's disease is incurable and progressive, leading to significant impairment in quality of life. The condition stems from genetic mutation and degeneration, making it challenging to find a proper cure.

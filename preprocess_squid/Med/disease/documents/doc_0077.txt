Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with Advanced Breast Cancer, which falls under the category of neoplastic diseases. While the exact cause and diagnostic methods weren't clearly known in this case, they experienced several common symptoms such as hot flashes, nausea, feeling tired (asthenia), pain, headaches, back pain, bone pain, increased coughing, difficulty breathing (dyspnea), sore throat (pharyngitis), and swelling in the limbs (peripheral edema). To manage these symptoms, they were prescribed oral medication. However, managing the disease came with its own set of challenges, mainly due to side effects from the treatment.

In the case of Advanced Breast Cancer, a neoplastic disease, patients often experience a variety of symptoms including hot flashes, nausea, fatigue (asthenia), pain, headaches, back pain, and bone pain. They might also face increased coughing, difficulty breathing (dyspnea), sore throat (pharyngitis), and swelling in the extremities (peripheral edema). The treatment usually involves oral medication, but side effects can pose significant challenges for patients.

Once a friend was diagnosed with Advanced Breast Cancer, a type of neoplastic disease. The common symptoms she experienced included hot flashes, nausea, feeling tired (asthenia), pain in various parts of her body, headaches, and back pain. Additionally, she also faced bone pain, increased coughing, difficulty breathing (dyspnea), sore throat (pharyngitis), and swelling in her limbs (peripheral edema). Her treatment focused on oral medication, but managing side effects proved to be a significant challenge during the process.

Once upon a time, there was a disease called Alzheimer's Disease. It falls under the category of degenerative diseases and its causes can be genetic, physical, or environmental. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluations. Some common symptoms include short-term memory loss, trouble finding words, difficulty with direction, asking the same questions repeatedly, and finding it challenging to manage everyday tasks.

The treatments for Alzheimer's Disease often involve oral medications, but unfortunately, there is no known cure yet. The condition is chronic and progressive, which can significantly impact a person’s quality of life.

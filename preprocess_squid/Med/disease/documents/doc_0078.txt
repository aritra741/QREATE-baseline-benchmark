Once upon a time, there was a disease called Alzheimer's Disease. It falls under the category of degenerative diseases, and its causes can be genetic, physical, or environmental. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluations. People with this condition often experience short-term memory loss, trouble finding words, difficulty navigating directions, repeating questions, and having troubles managing familiar tasks.

Currently, the treatments involve oral medications, but the disease is known to be a chronic and progressive condition that significantly impacts the quality of life. The degenerative process makes it challenging to find a cure.

Once upon a time, there was a disease called Alzheimer's Disease. It falls under the category of degenerative diseases and can be influenced by genetic, physical, or environmental factors. To diagnose it, doctors rely on clinical evaluations. Common symptoms include short-term memory impairment, difficulty with word-finding, trouble finding one's way around, asking repetitive questions, and challenges in managing familiar tasks.

The disease is a chronic condition that progresses over time, significantly impacting the quality of life for those affected. Degenerative pathogenesis means it gets worse as time goes on. While there are oral medications available to manage some symptoms, currently, there isn't a cure for Alzheimer's Disease.

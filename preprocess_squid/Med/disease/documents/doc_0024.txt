Once upon a time, there was a disease called Prostate Cancer. It's classified as neoplastic, which means it involves abnormal cell growth. While we don't have specific information on what causes it (that part is marked as 'nan'), doctors use a variety of methods to diagnose it, including laboratory tests, screenings, clinical evaluations, and imaging techniques.

People with Prostate Cancer might experience urinary obstruction or an enlarged prostate, which can be quite uncomfortable. For treatment, options include surgery, hormone therapy, and chemotherapy. Unfortunately, these treatments come with challenges such as side effects and a risk of renal failure. The prognosis isn't great; it often has high mortality rates and can progressively impair the quality of life for those affected.

Once upon a time, the disease in question was Prostate Cancer, which falls under the category of neoplastic conditions. Although we don't have specific information about its causes (etiology), doctors diagnose it through laboratory tests, screenings, clinical evaluations, and imaging techniques. Common symptoms include urinary obstruction and an enlarged prostate. Treatment options range from surgery to hormone therapy and chemotherapy. Prostate Cancer often has a poor prognosis with high mortality rates and can progressively impair the quality of life. The pathogenesis or development of this disease is linked to neoplastic changes. One major challenge in treating it is managing side effects, including the risk of renal failure.

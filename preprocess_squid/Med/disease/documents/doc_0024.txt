Once upon a time, there was a man diagnosed with Prostate Cancer. This type of cancer is neoplastic in nature. While the exact cause isn't fully understood, doctors use a mix of laboratory tests, screenings, and clinical evaluations to diagnose it. Common symptoms include urinary obstruction and an enlarged prostate.

The treatment options for this condition are quite diverse: surgery, hormone therapy, and chemotherapy are commonly used. However, these treatments come with their own challenges; side effects, the risk of renal failure, and a generally poor prognosis that often includes high mortality rates and progressive quality-of-life impairment due to its pathogenic nature.

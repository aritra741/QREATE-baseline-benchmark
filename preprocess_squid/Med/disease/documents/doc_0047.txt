Once upon a time, someone developed an irregular heart rhythm after using azithromycin (Zithromax or Zmax). The doctors diagnosed this condition through clinical evaluation and functional tests. They often observed symptoms like an irregular heartbeat, shortness of breath, dizziness, and even fainting in such cases. Unfortunately, the prognosis for this disease was quite serious, with a high mortality rate. The cause of this irregular heart rhythm was linked to iatrogenic factors, meaning it was brought about by medical treatment itself.

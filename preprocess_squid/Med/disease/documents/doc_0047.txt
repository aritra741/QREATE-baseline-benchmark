Once upon a time, there was a person who developed an irregular heart rhythm after taking azithromycin (Zithromax or Zmax). The doctors suspected the condition might be vascular and iatrogenic, meaning it was caused by the medication. To diagnose this, they conducted both clinical evaluations and functional tests.

The patient experienced some common symptoms such as an irregular heartbeat, shortness of breath, dizziness, and even fainting spells. While there were no specific treatments mentioned for their condition, the prognosis was quite serious with a high mortality rate. The pathogenesis or the underlying cause of this issue was found to be iatrogenic, meaning it stemmed from the use of azithromycin.

Despite the challenges in treatment, the focus remained on managing symptoms and preventing further complications due to the drug-induced condition.

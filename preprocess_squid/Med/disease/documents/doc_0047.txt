Once upon a time, there was a person who developed an irregular heart rhythm after taking azithromycin (Zithromax or Zmax). The doctors suspected it might be iatrogenic in nature. To diagnose the condition, they conducted both clinical evaluations and functional tests. Common symptoms included an irregular heartbeat, shortness of breath, dizziness, and even fainting. Sadly, due to high mortality rates, the prognosis wasn't very encouraging. The pathogenesis was linked to the iatrogenic factor, meaning it might have been caused by the medication itself.

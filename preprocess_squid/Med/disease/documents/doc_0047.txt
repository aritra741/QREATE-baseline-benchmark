Once upon a time, someone was diagnosed with an irregular heart rhythm, which falls under the category of vascular and iatrogenic diseases. The irregularity in their heartbeat began after they started taking azithromycin (Zithromax or Zmax). To confirm this diagnosis, doctors conducted both clinical evaluations and functional tests. They experienced symptoms like an irregular heartbeat, shortness of breath, dizziness, and even a few fainting spells. Sadly, the prognosis for this condition is associated with high mortality, making treatment quite challenging.

Once upon a time, there was a person who developed an irregular heart rhythm after taking azithromycin (Zithromax or Zmax). The doctors diagnosed the condition through clinical evaluation and functional tests. Common symptoms included an irregular heartbeat, shortness of breath, dizziness, and even fainting. Sadly, the prognosis for this disease is associated with high mortality due to its iatrogenic pathogenesis, which means it was caused by a medical treatment.

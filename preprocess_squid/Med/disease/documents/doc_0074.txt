Once upon a time, there was a disease called Diabetic Peripheral Neuropathy. This condition falls under two types: metabolic and vascular. It all started because of diabetes. To diagnose it, doctors rely on clinical evaluations and screenings. One common symptom people experience is neuropathic pain. Treatment often involves oral medications and physical therapy. Sadly, this condition tends to be chronic. The disease develops due to a metabolic disorder or vascular issues. Doctors face challenges in treatment, mainly due to potential side effects.

Once upon a time, there was a condition called Diabetic Peripheral Neuropathy. This disease falls into two main categories: metabolic and vascular, both stemming from diabetes. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluations and screening tests. One of the most common symptoms patients experience is neuropathic pain. Treatments often involve oral medications and physical therapy, but there are challenges such as side effects to be managed. Sadly, this condition tends to persist as a chronic condition due to its complex pathogenesis involving metabolic disorders and vascular issues.

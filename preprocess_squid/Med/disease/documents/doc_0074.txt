Once upon a time, there was a condition called Diabetic Peripheral Neuropathy. This disease falls into the categories of both metabolic and vascular issues, usually stemming from diabetes. Doctors diagnose it through clinical evaluations and screenings, noting one of its common symptoms as neuropathic pain. Treatment options include oral medications and physical therapy, but managing these can be tricky due to potential side effects. The prognosis for this condition is generally considered chronic. This all happens because the disease involves both metabolic disorders and vascular issues in the body.

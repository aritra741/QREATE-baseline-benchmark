Once upon a time, there was a person dealing with a condition known as Depression. This type of issue falls under the category of psychiatric diseases. While there wasn't much information about the causes or how to diagnose it, people who experienced similar conditions often reported feeling low mood and loss of interest in activities they once enjoyed. Over time, various treatments were tried, but the long-term outlook was still unclear due to the lack of detailed understanding about its development and the challenges faced during treatment.

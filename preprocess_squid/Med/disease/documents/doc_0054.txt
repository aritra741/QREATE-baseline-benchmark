Once upon a time, there was a disease called Immune Reconstitution Syndrome (IRS), which can occur in people infected with HIV when they start taking HAART ( Highly Active Antiretroviral Therapy ). This syndrome has various symptoms like fever, dyspnea, abdominal pain, diarrhea, headache, and weakness. Doctors diagnose it through clinical evaluation and laboratory tests. The good news is that there are treatments available, mainly oral medication and injections. However, the prognosis can vary significantly, sometimes leading to significant morbidity or even occasional mortality. The pathogenesis of IRS is often due to inflammatory processes, bacterial infections, viral infections, fungal infections, or iatrogenic factors.

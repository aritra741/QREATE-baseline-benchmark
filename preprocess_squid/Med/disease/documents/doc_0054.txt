Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Immune Reconstitution Syndrome (IRS). This syndrome can be of infectious, inflammatory, or iatrogenic nature. It usually affects HIV-infected patients who start taking HAART ( Highly Active Antiretroviral Therapy ). Doctors diagnose IRS through clinical evaluation and laboratory tests.

Symptoms commonly include fever, dyspnea, abdominal pain, diarrhea, headache, weakness, lymphadenitis, subcutaneous nodules, and abnormal skin lesions. Treatment can involve oral medication or injections. The prognosis of IRS is quite variable; it sometimes leads to significant morbidity and occasionally results in mortality. Its pathogenesis involves inflammatory processes as well as infectious bacterial, viral, or fungal elements, along with iatrogenic factors.

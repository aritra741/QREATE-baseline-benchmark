The disease in question is Immune Reconstitution Syndrome (IRS), which can be of infectious, inflammatory, or iatrogenic type. It typically occurs when HIV-infected patients begin HAART ( Highly Active Antiretroviral Therapy ). Diagnosis usually involves a clinical evaluation and laboratory tests. Common symptoms include fever, dyspnea, abdominal pain, diarrhea, headache, weakness, lymphadenitis, subcutaneous nodules, and abnormal skin lesions. Treatments often consist of oral medication or injections. The prognosis can be quite variable, sometimes leading to significant morbidity and even occasional mortality. Pathogenesis involves inflammatory responses as well as infectious bacterial, viral, or fungal processes, along with iatrogenic factors.

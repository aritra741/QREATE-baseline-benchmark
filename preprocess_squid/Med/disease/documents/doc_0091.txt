Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Atrial Fibrillation. This disease falls under the category of vascular and degenerative diseases. While we don't have much information on what causes it or how to diagnose it, one thing is clear—people with this condition often face significant challenges in their quality of life. The primary treatment involves oral medication, but unfortunately, the prognosis isn't very optimistic; disability, high mortality rates, and impaired quality of life are common outcomes. The exact process by which Atrial Fibrillation develops within the vascular system remains a bit of a mystery, adding to the complexity of treating this condition.

Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Atrial Fibrillation, which falls under the categories of vascular and degenerative diseases. Despite this, we don't have much information on its exact causes or how it's diagnosed, nor do we know what common symptoms to look out for. The main treatment involves oral medication, but the prognosis isn't great, with a high risk of disability, mortality, and quality-of-life impairment. Understanding that its pathogenesis is related to vascular issues, doctors face challenges in managing this condition effectively.

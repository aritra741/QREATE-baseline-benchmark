Once upon a time, there was a condition called Atrial Fibrillation, which falls under the categories of vascular and degenerative diseases. While we don't have all the information about its causes or how to diagnose it, one thing is clear—people often need oral medication as part of their treatment. The outlook for those with this disease isn't always rosy; high disability rates and a significant impact on quality of life are common challenges. The condition is linked closely to vascular issues, making it complex to manage effectively.

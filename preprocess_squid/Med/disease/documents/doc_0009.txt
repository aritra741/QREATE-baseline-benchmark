Once upon a time, there was a person who experienced Phantom Limb Pain after having their limb amputated due to trauma. The pain they felt wasn't just any ordinary ache; it was more intense and persistent. Doctors couldn't pinpoint the exact diagnostic methods for this condition, but common symptoms included constant pain and aches in the missing limb. To manage these symptoms, various treatments were employed: physical therapy to improve mobility, injections for localized pain relief, oral medications to address overall discomfort, and even device implantation for long-term solutions. However, one challenge they faced was the financial burden of ongoing treatment.

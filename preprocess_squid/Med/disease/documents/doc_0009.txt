Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Phantom Limb Pain. It often affected people who had lost a limb due to traumatic amputation. While the exact symptoms were primarily characterized by pain and aching sensations where the missing limb used to be, diagnosis methods were not well-established yet. Common treatments included physical therapy, injections, oral medications, and even implantation of devices. However, one challenge patients faced was the financial burden associated with these treatment options.

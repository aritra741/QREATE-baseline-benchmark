Once upon a time, someone was dealing with Phantom Limb Pain after they had to have an arm or leg amputated due to traumatic injuries. The pain they experienced wasn't just any ordinary ache; it was intense and persistent, causing them great discomfort. Fortunately, various treatments were available to help manage their condition, including physical therapy, injections, oral medications, and even the option of device implantation. However, one challenge they faced was the financial burden associated with these treatments. Despite this hurdle, there was hope for relief and an improved quality of life as they navigated their journey.

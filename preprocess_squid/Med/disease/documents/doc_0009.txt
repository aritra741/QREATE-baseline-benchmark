Once upon a time, someone was dealing with Phantom Limb Pain, which they experienced after losing a limb due to an accident. The pain came in the form of constant aches and sharp pains, even though their limb no longer existed. Doctors couldn't pinpoint exact diagnostic methods at the time, but they did recommend several treatments like physical therapy, injections, oral medications, and sometimes even implanting devices to help manage the pain. While these treatments could be effective, they often came with a significant financial burden, making it tough for some people to access all the necessary care.

In our story, we're dealing with a common illness called Influenza. It's both an infectious and inflammatory disease, caused by a viral infection. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluation. The typical symptoms include fever, difficulty breathing, vomiting, dehydration, dry lips, sunken eyes, listlessness, and not eating. The main treatments are vaccination and oral medication. Sadly, there's no information about the prognosis or treatment challenges for Influenza in our data.

Once upon a time, we were dealing with a disease called Influenza. It's both an infectious and inflammatory condition caused by a viral infection. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluation. The common symptoms include fever, difficulty breathing, vomiting, dehydration, dry lips, sunken eyes, listlessness, and not eating. To prevent or treat it, vaccination and oral medication are commonly used. However, we don't have much information on the prognosis of Influenza yet. The disease spreads through infectious viral particles and can lead to inflammatory responses in the body. While there are challenges in treating Influenza, they remain largely unknown for now.

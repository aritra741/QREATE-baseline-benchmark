Once upon a time, there was a condition called Acute Appendicitis. It's an inflammatory disease that causes a lot of discomfort. The most common symptoms people experience include abdominal pain, sometimes feeling like there's a palpable mass, and issues with obstruction or distension in the abdomen. Doctors typically diagnose it through clinical evaluation and imaging tests before recommending surgery as the primary treatment. While we know what to look for and how to treat it, some details about its exact cause (etiology) and long-term outcomes (prognosis) are still a bit unclear.

Once upon a time, someone had an unfortunate encounter with poison oak. The disease they developed was called Poison Oak Rash and it fell under the categories of allergic and toxic reactions. Exposure to urushiol, a substance found in poison oak, triggered their symptoms.

Itchy skin, blisters, and a rash were some of the common signs that appeared. Doctors would typically recommend oral medications or other treatments for managing these symptoms. While side effects and scarring could be challenges during treatment, the prognosis was generally favorable once the right course of action was taken. The allergic and toxic nature of the reaction played a significant role in how the disease progressed.

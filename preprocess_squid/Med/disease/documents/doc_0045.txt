Once upon a time, someone was dealing with a condition called Panic Disorder, which falls under the psychiatric category. They experienced recurrent panic attacks, severe anxiety, rapid heart rate, difficulty breathing, and felt like their heart was racing. Their palms would get sweaty, they couldn’t catch their breath, felt lightheaded, and had intense fear of dying. Sadly, there's not much information available on how this condition is diagnosed or treated, or what the outcome might be.

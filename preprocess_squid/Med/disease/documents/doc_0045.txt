Once upon a time, someone was dealing with a condition called Panic Disorder, which falls under the category of psychiatric diseases. They experienced recurrent panic attacks that brought severe anxiety, along with rapid heart rate, difficulty breathing, and feeling like their heart was racing. Their palms would get sweaty, they couldn't catch their breath, felt lightheaded, and had intense fear of dying. Sadly, there wasn't much information available about how to diagnose or treat this condition back then, nor could we predict the prognosis or fully understand the pathogenesis or treatment challenges involved.

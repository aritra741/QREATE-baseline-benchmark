Once upon a time, someone was dealing with something called Panic Disorder, which falls under the category of psychiatric conditions. They would often experience recurrent panic attacks, severe anxiety, rapid heart rate, difficulty breathing, and felt like their heart was racing. Their palms would get sweaty, they'd struggle to breathe, feel lightheaded, and have intense fear of dying. Despite these challenging symptoms, there wasn't much detail available about the diagnostic methods or treatment options for this condition.

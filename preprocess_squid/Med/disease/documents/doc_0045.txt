Once upon a time, there was someone who experienced recurrent panic attacks. These weren't just any ordinary feelings; they were severe anxiety that came with a rapid heart rate and difficulty breathing. Their hearts would race, their palms got sweaty, and they felt like they couldn't breathe properly or even lightheaded. The intense fear of dying made every moment feel incredibly scary. Without specific diagnostic methods or known causes for these symptoms, doctors struggled to provide targeted treatments and predict the prognosis or understand the pathogenesis fully. Despite all this, finding ways to manage these challenges was crucial for their well-being.

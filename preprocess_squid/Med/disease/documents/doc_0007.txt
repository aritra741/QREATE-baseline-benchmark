Once upon a time, there was a condition known as Lung Cancer, which falls under the category of neoplastic diseases. The factors that can contribute to its development include smoking, exposure to secondhand smoke, environmental factors like asbestos and radon, along with arsenic and chromium, not to mention radiation exposure in some cases.

To diagnose this condition, doctors typically rely on clinical evaluations. Common symptoms reported by patients often include a persistent cough, hemoptysis (coughing up blood), shortness of breath, chest pain, hoarseness of the voice, facial swelling, neck swelling, weight gain leading to a moon-like face, and even weakness or bone pain.

In terms of treatment, options such as chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and surgery are commonly used. However, despite these treatments, the prognosis is often marked by high mortality rates, with the disease being progressive in nature. The pathogenesis involves genetic mutations and neoplastic changes within the cells.

That's the story behind Lung Cancer!

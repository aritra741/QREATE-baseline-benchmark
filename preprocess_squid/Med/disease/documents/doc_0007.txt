Once upon a time, there was a serious disease known as Lung Cancer. This type of cancer falls under the category of neoplastic diseases. The main causes include smoking, secondhand smoke, environmental exposures like asbestos and radon, as well as other factors such as arsenic and chromium exposure or even radiation. Doctors usually diagnose it through clinical evaluation.

Lung Cancer can present itself with a variety of symptoms including persistent coughing, blood in the mucus (hemoptysis), difficulty breathing, chest pain, hoarseness of voice, facial swelling, neck swelling, weight gain leading to a moon face appearance, feeling weak, and even bone or kidney pain. The treatment options often involve chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and surgery.

Unfortunately, the prognosis for Lung Cancer is quite serious with high mortality rates and progressive nature. This is mainly due to genetic mutations that contribute to its development. However, there are challenges in treating it effectively.

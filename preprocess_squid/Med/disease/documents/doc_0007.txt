In the case of lung cancer, a neoplastic disease often linked to smoking, secondhand smoke, environmental exposures like asbestos and radon, as well as chemicals such as arsenic and chromium, the diagnosis typically starts with a clinical evaluation. Common symptoms include coughing, hemoptysis (coughing up blood), shortness of breath, chest pain, hoarse voice, facial swelling, neck swelling, weight gain leading to a "moon face," and weakness. Bone pain and kidney stones can also occur in more advanced stages. Treatment options may involve chemotherapy, radiotherapy, or surgery, but the prognosis is generally challenging with high mortality rates and progressive disease course due to genetic mutations that contribute to its development.

In the case of Lung Cancer, a neoplastic disease, common risk factors include smoking, secondhand smoke, environmental exposures to asbestos and radon, as well as exposure to arsenic, chromium, or radiation. Diagnosis often involves clinical evaluation, and symptoms can vary but may include coughing, hemoptysis (coughing up blood), shortness of breath, chest pain, a hoarse voice, facial or neck swelling, weight gain with a moon-like face, weakness, bone pain, or even kidney stones. Treatments typically involve chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and surgery, though the prognosis is often associated with high mortality rates that can progress over time due to genetic mutations.

Once upon a time, a person was diagnosed with Lung Cancer, which falls under the category of neoplastic diseases. The common causes for this condition include smoking, secondhand smoke, environmental exposures like asbestos and radon, as well as other factors such as arsenic and chromium exposure. Diagnosing it usually involves clinical evaluation.

Symptoms can vary widely but might include a persistent cough, hemoptysis (coughing up blood), shortness of breath, chest pain, hoarse voice, facial swelling, neck swelling, weight gain with a moon face appearance, weakness, bone pain, and even kidney stones. The treatment options available are chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and surgery.

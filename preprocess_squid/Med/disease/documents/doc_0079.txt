Once upon a time, there was a disease called Cancer. It's a neoplastic condition, which means it involves abnormal cell growth. While we don't have all the details on what causes it or how to diagnose it, when symptoms do appear, one common treatment is chemotherapy. Unfortunately, the prognosis for this disease is quite serious with high mortality rates. Understanding its pathogenesis as a neoplastic process helps us in identifying potential challenges in treating Cancer effectively.

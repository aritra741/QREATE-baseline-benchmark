   - After passing a kidney stone, some residual pain may persist due to inflammation and irritation of the ureter and surrounding tissues.
   - This can lead to muscle spasms in the back and abdomen, contributing to lingering discomfort.

3. **Potential Causes for Lingering Back Pain**:
   - **Muscle Strain**: The intense pain during passage of a kidney stone can cause muscles around the kidneys and spine to spasm or strain.
   - **Inflammation**: The body's inflammatory response to the stone passing through may continue, causing ongoing discomfort.
   - **Nerve Irritation**: The ureter runs close to the spinal cord, and irritation from the stone could potentially affect nearby nerves.

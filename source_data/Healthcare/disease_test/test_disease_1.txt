Disease Name: Diabetes Mellitus
Disease Type: metabolic
Pathogenesis: metabolic disorder
Etiology: obesity, increased lipid levels, inflammation, insulin resistance
Diagnostic Methods: laboratory test
Common Symptoms: increased thirst, polyuria, weight loss, fatigue, blurry vision
Complications: blindness, cardiovascular disorders, kidney damage, eye damage, nerve damage
Affected Organs: large blood vessels, kidneys, eyes, nerves, pancreas
Treatments: oral medication, lifestyle modification, dietary intervention, regular exercise
Drugs: metformin, insulin
Prognosis: chronic condition, quality of life impairment
Risk Factors: obesity, increased lipid levels, inflammation, insulin resistance

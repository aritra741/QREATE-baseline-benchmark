Disease Name: Asthma
Disease Type: autoimmune
Pathogenesis: autoimmune disorder, allergic reaction
Etiology: allergic reaction, hypersensitivity, vitamin D deficiency
Diagnostic Methods: clinical evaluation, lung function test
Common Symptoms: wheezing, chest tightness, shortness of breath, coughing
Complications: severe asthma attack, respiratory failure
Affected Organs: respiratory system, lungs, airways
Treatments: inhaler, oral medication, dietary intervention
Drugs: salbutamol, theophylline, corticosteroids
Prognosis: chronic condition, manageable with treatment
Risk Factors: smoking, obesity, vitamin D deficiency, allergen exposure

Disease Name: Tuberculosis
Disease Type: infectious
Pathogenesis: infectious disease, bacterial infection
Etiology: Mycobacterium tuberculosis infection, respiratory droplets
Diagnostic Methods: chest X-ray, tuberculin skin test, sputum test
Common Symptoms: persistent cough, fever, night sweats, weight loss, chest pain
Complications: pulmonary tuberculosis, extrapulmonary TB, treatment-resistant TB
Affected Organs: lungs, lymph nodes, bones, brain
Treatments: anti-tuberculous drugs, respiratory support
Drugs: isoniazid, rifampicin, pyrazinamide, ethambutol
Prognosis: curable with proper treatment
Risk Factors: HIV infection, poverty, crowded conditions, malnutrition

Financial Results
The Company reported an increase in revenue by approximately 22.8% to $16.30 million from $13.27 million in the previous 
reporting period. 
The increase in revenue from 30 June 2021 resulted from the demand increase for the electric vehicle chargers market with 
the global economy's recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic.
Profit before tax increased to $1.23 million in the current reporting period compared to $1.03 million in the last reporting period. 
We have successfully managed the business growth despite the COVID interruption and global supply chain issues in FY 
2022.
